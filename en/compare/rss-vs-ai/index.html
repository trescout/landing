How does TreScout differ?

Reading depends on the day

Report length and reading time vary with the day’s source volume. We do not promise a fixed duration.

Repeat handling

Items in normal reports from the last 30 days are excluded from the fresh-only option. New finds and major updates stay visible.

Source, glossary, and discovery links

Open a technical term in the glossary and move from the report to the detailed page for the tool and original source.
