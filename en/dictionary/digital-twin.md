# What is Digital Twin?

It is a digital copy of a physical object or system.

## Overview
A digital twin is an exact copy of a physical object or system in the digital world. This replica is constantly updated with data from the real-world object, simulating its behavior.

*Analogy: It is like reflecting all parts of an airplane in a digital environment and simulating its digital copy with the same data while the real airplane is in the air.*

## How it works
Data is collected from the real world through sensors. This data is transferred to the digital model, and the model calculates when the real object may fail or how it will perform.

## Where it is used
It is used to estimate the maintenance time of machines in factories or to simulate traffic flow in city planning.

## Commonly confused with
Not to be confused with a simple 3D model; Digital twin is a dynamic structure that is constantly fed with live data.

## Frequently asked questions
**Are digital twins and simulations the same?**
A simulation can be run once, while a digital twin is a constantly living system with real-time data.

**Can everything have a digital twin?**
Theoretically yes, but cost and data collection possibilities are decisive.
