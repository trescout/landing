# What is Digital Twin?

*Dictionary · Data · Last updated: September 22, 2026*

Digital twin is a copy of the object fed with live data.

## Definition and Word Origin

"Twin" means twin. Sensor data flows from the real object, the model simulates behavior. Malfunction and performance are foreseen. He runs factory and city jobs.

***Analogy:** It is like a digital copy of the plane flying with the same data.*

## How to Know and Use in Daily Life?

**Factory:** Maintenance estimate.
**City:** Traffic simulation.
**Energy:** Turbine monitoring.

## Technical Depth and Architecture

Flow:

```
sensör → model → tahmin → bakım emri
```

Parts:

**Data:** Sensor and recording flow.
**Model:** A mix of physics and learning.
**Action:** Maintenance and adjustment order.

Rule: If data is cut off, the twin becomes blind. Continuity of supply is essential.

## Frequently Mixed Things

It is mistaken for a 3D model. That is the static picture, that is the living system. One is a photograph, the other is a mirror.

## Use in Different Disciplines

**Aeroplane:** Co-flight in the air and in simulation.
**Mirror:** A surface that reflects movement.
**Shadow:** Darkness following the object.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the simulation difference?**

The simulation runs once, the twin is fed alive. One is film, the other is broadcast.

**Can everything have a twin?**

Theoretically yes, in practice cost and data determine.

**What does it cost?**

There is a sensor, model and maintenance fee. It is paid for the fault profit.

**Where is it used?**

In factory, city and energy. The critical asset comes first.
