What is Text-to-Image?

It is an artificial intelligence technology that analyzes written text prompts and generates original images corresponding to them.

Overview

Text-to-image technology is an AI model structure that takes natural language descriptions and converts them into pixel data. It comprehends the objects, styles, and relationships within the prompt entered by the user. Thus, it can generate highly detailed digital images from scratch.

Analogy: It is similar to describing in detail to a painter what you want them to draw. As you explain with words, the painter draws the image of your dreams on the canvas.

How it works

The system uses diffusion models trained on millions of text and image pairs. The text provided as input is converted into embedding vectors, and the AI transforms the noise data step by step into a clear image.

Where it is used

It is heavily used in digital marketing, game design for creating concept art, and social media content designs. It is also preferred for preparing quick visual drafts when prototyping products.

Commonly confused with

It should not be confused with visual search engines. While search engines find existing images, this technology creates a completely new and original image.

Frequently asked questions

Can text-to-image systems generate copyrighted images?

Since the models rely on training data, they may generate images resembling copyrighted characters, and this situation is a subject of legal regulations.

What is required to obtain a high-quality image?

Using detailed and clearly written prompt expressions directly increases the quality of the image.
