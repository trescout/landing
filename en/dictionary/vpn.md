# What is VPN?

*Dictionary · Dev · Last updated: June 11, 2026*

> Virtual Private Network

It is a tool that hides your identity and provides a secure connection by encrypting your data on the internet.

## Overview

It passes your data through a tunnel as you connect to the internet. Thanks to this tunnel, outsiders cannot see what you are doing or where you are connecting from. Even your internet service provider can't read your traffic.

***Analogy:** Instead of walking naked on the street, it is like traveling in a special armored vehicle where it is not clear who you are from the outside.*

## How it works

Download a VPN app, select the server you want to connect to and activate the connection; All your traffic will now be encrypted.

## Where it is used

It is used in public WiFi networks, for work that requires confidentiality or to overcome access barriers.

## Commonly confused with

It may be confused with a proxy, but a VPN encrypts all device traffic, whereas a proxy usually only routes certain applications.

## Frequently asked questions

**Will VPN slow down my speed?**

Since the data is encrypted, there may sometimes be a slight loss of speed.

**Are free VPNs safe?**

Generally no, they can generate revenue from your data.
