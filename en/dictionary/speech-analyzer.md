# What is SpeechAnalyzer?

It is an analysis tool that converts audio recordings into text and analyzes the emotion, tone or meaning in this text.

## Overview
SpeechAnalyzer tries to understand not only what is said, but also how it is said. Can detect excitement, anger, or hesitation in a speaker's voice. This technology is used in many areas, from customer service conversations to psychological analysis.

*Analogy: It's like a friend who understands the real meaning behind someone's 'I'm fine' from the tremor or tone of their voice.*

## How it works
It converts sound waves into digital data, transcribes it into text with artificial intelligence models, and then processes it with algorithms that analyze emotions.

## Where it is used
It is used in call centers, meeting analysis software and voice assistants.

## Commonly confused with
It differs from tools that only perform audio-text conversion by performing sentiment analysis.

## Frequently asked questions
**Does SpeechAnalyzer violate privacy?**
These tools are often used to improve business processes, but must be managed in accordance with data privacy rules.
