# What is Cyber Capabilities?

*Dictionary · Dev · Last updated: August 15, 2026*

They are technical capabilities and tools used to protect or intervene in digital systems.

## Overview

Cyber ​​capabilities define how defensible a system is in the world of cyber security or how resistant it is to attacks. It covers both defensive (firewalls, monitoring) and offensive (testing tools) aspects.

***Analogy:** It is like all the military equipment of a castle, such as walls, watchtowers and defensive weapons.*

## How it works

It is implemented with software tools, security protocols and technical methods that require expertise.

## Where it is used

It is used in cyber security operations, defense industry and information technology security.

## Commonly confused with

It is often confused with just cybersecurity, but this term focuses more on the 'what we can do' part.

## Frequently asked questions

**Are these abilities only for attack?**

No, it is also used to protect digital systems.

**How to develop?**

Through continuous training, up-to-date threat analysis and the use of technical tools.
