# What is LLM-Wiki?

It is a structured information library where artificial intelligence models are fed from external sources to provide more accurate and up-to-date answers.

## Overview
Artificial intelligence models cannot update their knowledge when their training ends. LLM-Wiki is a reference source that allows these models to access up-to-date and verified information from the outside world. The model produces more reliable results by looking at this library when it encounters a question.

*Analogy: It is like a student taking an exam who is not content with just what he has memorized but also writes answers with help from the open textbook next to him.*

## How it works
The system takes the user's question and searches for relevant information in this library. The information found is transmitted to the artificial intelligence as a note. Artificial intelligence uses these notes to prepare the most accurate answer for you.

## Where it is used
It is used in corporate information systems, technical support bots and artificial intelligence applications that need to follow current news.

## Commonly confused with
It is similar to the RAG system, but here the emphasis is on keeping information in a more organized and encyclopedic structure.

## Frequently asked questions
**Why is LLM-Wiki necessary?**
It enables artificial intelligence to accurately convey private company data or current events that it does not know or may remember incorrectly on its own.

**Can anyone create their own LLM-Wiki?**
Yes, you can specialize your AI by putting together your own documents or database.
