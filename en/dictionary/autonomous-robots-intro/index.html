What is Introduction to Autonomous Robots?

Autonomous robots are machines that constantly move around without receiving commands.

Definition and Word Origin

Autonomous means autonomous. The triple structure: Perception (sensor), processing (artificial intelligence) and action (motor). The robot does not memorize the path, it overcomes the obstacle and reaches the target.

Analogy: It is like telling a toy car where it will go and expecting it to find its way instead of driving it with a remote control.

How to Know and Use in Daily Life?

Warehouse: Transport between shelves.
Factory: Assembly line.
Vehicle: Driver support systems.

Technical Depth and Architecture

Loop:

algıla → planla → hareket et → denetle

Parts:

Perception: Camera, lidar and radar.
Location: Positioning on the map.
Plan: Route around obstacle.
Control: Error stop and human takeover.

ROS is the common framework. Safety rule: Speed and power are limited near humans.

Frequently Mixed Things

It is thought to be a remote-controlled robot. It is entirely in human hands, face to face with this goal. One is a puppet, the other is an apprentice.

Use in Different Disciplines

Toy car: The model that finds its own way.
Elevator: Floor selection according to the button.
Autopilot: The system that maintains the route.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do they learn?

Through data analysis and trial and error. Rehearsals are done in simulation, fine-tuning in the field.

Is it safe?

Yes, within the limits it is designed for. Error stops and human takeover are mandatory.

Where is it used?

In the warehouse, factory, and on the road. It is at the forefront in repetitive and dangerous tasks.

When will it become widespread?

Cost and regulations dictate. Indoor areas come first, outdoor paths come later.
