# What is VRAM?

> Video Random Access Memory

It is a special memory unit on the graphics card that accelerates intensive graphics and artificial intelligence operations.

## Overview
VRAM is a fast storage area on the graphics card itself, independent of the main memory of the processor. While artificial intelligence models are running, the model's parameters and current calculation data are loaded into this area. The higher the amount of memory, the faster your computer can process larger and more complex models.

*Analogy: While regular RAM is on a desk, VRAM is like a very quickly accessible special drawer right next to the graphics card; The most needed files are kept here.*

## How it works
When the AI ​​model is run, the software retrieves data from the system RAM and copies it to VRAM; The graphics card also processes this data very quickly and returns the result.

## Where it is used
It is used in games, video editing programs, and systems that run native artificial intelligence models.

## Commonly confused with
It is often confused with system RAM; RAM is used for general tasks, while VRAM is used for intensive graphics and calculation tasks of the graphics card.

## Frequently asked questions
**What happens if VRAM is not enough?**
The AI ​​model becomes too slow or stops working with an 'out of memory' error.
