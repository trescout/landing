# What is Action?

It is the concrete action that an artificial intelligence or software performs in the outside world or within the system upon the command given to it.

## Overview
Action is the step of an AI model that not only produces text but also leads to a physical or digital change, such as sending an email, deleting a file, or entering data into a website. It is the mechanism that turns artificial intelligence from a 'thinker' into a 'doer'.

*Analogy: A cook does not just read a recipe, but goes into the kitchen, turns on the stove and chops the ingredients.*

## How it works
Artificial intelligence sets a goal using the tools at its disposal. It then triggers the technical command necessary to achieve this goal and makes a change to the system.

## Where it is used
It is frequently seen in AI Agent systems, automation tools, and application integrations.

## Commonly confused with
It should not be confused with the mere thinking or planning process; action is an actual action that leaves a mark on the system.

## Frequently asked questions
**Does Action always succeed?**
No, it may fail due to lack of authorization or incorrect command usage.

**What is the difference between action and prompt?**
Prompt is a request, action is the concrete action that takes place as a result of this request.
