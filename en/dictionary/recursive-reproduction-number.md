# What is Recursive Reproduction Number?

It is the growth rate of an artificial intelligence system during its self-improvement and replication process.

## Overview
This term measures how quickly an AI becomes the next generation by writing and improving its own code. As the system creates copies of itself, this ratio determines the speed and efficiency of technological progress. It is a metric used to understand the future potential of self-improving systems.

*Analogy: It is similar to the speed at which a plant doubles every day to cover a garden.*

## How it works
The system first analyzes its current performance, then makes improvements, and transfers these improvements to its new copy. When this cycle is repeated continuously, the rate of increase in the system's capabilities is expressed by this number.

## Where it is used
It is used in advanced artificial intelligence safety and system architecture research.

## Commonly confused with
It should not be confused with simple software copying or backup processes.

## Frequently asked questions
**Why is this number important?**
It allows us to understand how quickly the system is 'becoming intelligent' or changing.

**What happens if this number is high?**
The system can reach an unpredictable level very quickly.
