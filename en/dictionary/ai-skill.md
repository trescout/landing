# What is AI Skill?

It is the set of specialized abilities that artificial intelligence has to successfully complete a certain task or process.

## Overview
AI skill is the set of tools and methods that an artificial intelligence system uses to carry out a specific command. These capabilities enable AI to do concrete tasks, such as editing a file or searching the internet, and not just talk. An artificial intelligence agent can solve complex problems using these skills.

*Analogy: It's like a cook not only knowing recipes, but also having different areas of expertise, such as using a knife, managing an oven, or preparing sauces.*

## How it works
These capabilities are usually given to artificial intelligence models by granting special commands or access to external tools. When you give him a task, the model chooses which skill he needs to use and activates that skill. In this way, the model turns from a general chatbot into a helper that gets the job done.

## Where it is used
It is used in platforms that manage AI agents, automation tools, and specialized AI applications.

## Commonly confused with
Can be confused with AI-agent; The agent is the whole that does the job, and the AI ​​skill is each of the tools that make up this whole.

## Frequently asked questions
**Does every artificial intelligence have the same ability?**
No, capabilities can be personalized based on the tools the model is trained on or later integrated with.

**Does AI skill develop on its own?**
It is usually defined by humans, but in advanced systems the model can better learn which ability to use and when to use it over time.
