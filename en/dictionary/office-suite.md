# What is Office Suite?

It is a software package put together to perform daily office work in a digital environment.

## Overview
It is a group of programs that offer basic tasks such as writing documents, creating spreadsheets and preparing presentations under one roof. They work in harmony with each other and enable data to be easily transferred from one to another.

*Analogy: It's like a stationery store; pen, paper, calculator and flipchart are in one bag.*

## How it works
It is usually installed on your computer with a single installation file. Switching between files is very easy because they use a common interface and file format.

## Where it is used
It is the standard in business, education and personal computing.

## Frequently asked questions
**Are there cloud-based ones?**
Yes, today most office suites can work online via a browser.

**Are they all paid?**
No, there are many options, both free and paid.
