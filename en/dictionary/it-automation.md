# What is IT Automation?

*Dictionary · Dev · Last updated: July 31, 2026*

Routine and repetitive tasks in computer systems are carried out by software without the need for humans.

## Overview

IT automation is outsourcing boring tasks, such as updating a server or moving data from one place to another, to a robot. It minimizes human error and ensures that systems operate uninterruptedly 24/7. In the modern world, these processes are often managed by artificial intelligence agents.

***Analogy:** Instead of manually arranging the same parts every day in a factory, it is like deploying a robotic arm that does the job without error.*

## How it works

By using predefined scripts or smart algorithms, the system is ensured to operate automatically when a certain trigger occurs.

## Where it is used

It is used extensively in server management, software distribution and big data processing processes.

## Commonly confused with

It can be confused with artificial intelligence; Automation is rule-based, AI learns.

## Frequently asked questions

**Will automation take my job?**

No, automation frees you from boring tasks and allows you to focus on more valuable tasks.
