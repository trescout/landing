# What is Stealth Headless Browser?

An invisible browser that bypasses firewalls on websites and prevents detection as a bot.

## Overview
These are software programs that do not have a screen like standard browsers but visit sites by mimicking the behavior of a human user. Thanks to its stealth feature, it can bypass the bot-blocking systems used by websites. This allows data collection processes to continue uninterrupted by appearing as a normal user.

*Analogy: It is like someone trying to bypass a security guard to enter a building by disguising themselves and acting like a regular employee to sneak inside.*

## How it works
The browser is configured to make details such as screen resolution, mouse movements, and software signatures appear like human behavior.

## Where it is used
It is used in processes such as web scraping, automated testing, and price tracking.

## Commonly confused with
Headless Browser

## Frequently asked questions
**Why don't we use a normal browser?**
Normal browsers consume too many resources and cannot perform thousands of operations in the background simultaneously.
