# What is Home Automation?

It is the automatic control of the devices in your home via artificial intelligence or the internet.

## Overview
It is the management of lights, locks, heating systems or white goods from a center or through sensors. When artificial intelligence is added, the house can learn your habits and heat the house before you come home or turn off the lights while you sleep.

*Analogy: It's like having a helper in your home who thinks and handles small tasks for you.*

## How it works
You install smart devices in your home and connect them via an app. For example, by defining rules such as 'turn on the lights at 7pm', you enable the house to work on its own.

## Where it is used
It is used in smart home systems, security cameras and energy-saving smart thermostats.

## Commonly confused with
Not to be confused with just a remote control; Automation involves the system making decisions on its own without your intervention.

## Frequently asked questions
**Is it safe?**
It is secure in a well-configured system, but it is important to keep devices up-to-date.
