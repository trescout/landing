# What is Attention Mechanism?

A method that enables artificial intelligence to better grasp meaning by focusing on the most important words within a text.

## Overview
It is a fundamental mechanism that enables artificial intelligence models to establish relationships between words, especially when processing long sentences. Just as humans mentally underline important words while reading, this structure weighs the impact of words on one another to capture the correct meaning. It is one of the biggest secrets behind the success of modern language models.

*Analogy: It is like being in a crowded room where multiple people are speaking, yet you are able to focus solely on the voice addressing you.*

## How it works
Mathematical weights are assigned to each word in the sentence, and it is calculated which word is most related to the others. Thus, the model preserves semantic integrity without losing the connection between a subject at the beginning of a sentence and a verb at the end.

## Where it is used
It is used in the core architectural layer of all contemporary language models and translation systems today.

## Commonly confused with
It might be confused with general memory or simple word-counting methods, but the core phenomenon here is the capability to focus.

## Frequently asked questions
**Why is it so important?**
Because it prevents artificial intelligence from missing the context of the sentence.
