What is Knowledge Graphs?

It is a type of database that presents information and the relationships between this information by connecting them in a network structure.

Overview

Infographics organize data not just into lists but also show connections between concepts. This structure allows artificial intelligence to better understand complex issues and make logical inferences between data. It works like a digital brain map.

Analogy: Instead of randomly arranging the books in a library, imagine a giant spider web where each book is threaded together according to its subject, author, and connection to other books.

How it works

Data is defined as nodes (objects) and edges (relationships). For example, 'Istanbul' is a node, 'Türkiye' is a node, and 'is the capital' between them is a relationship.

Where it is used

It is used in search engines, recommendation systems, and situations where artificial intelligence needs to verify real-world information.

Commonly confused with

It can be confused with classic table-based databases; While they use rows and columns, this system puts relationships at the center.

Frequently asked questions

Why should I use infographics?

Ideal for discovering hidden connections between data and making smarter queries.

What is its relationship with artificial intelligence?

It provides support with real-world data to prevent artificial intelligence from hallucinating.
