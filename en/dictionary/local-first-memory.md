# What is Local-first Memory?

An approach to information storage where data is stored directly on your device and can be accessed at any time, without the need for the Internet.

## Overview
Your information is kept directly on your computer or phone instead of cloud servers. In this way, you can access and edit your data even if your internet is disconnected. It is a very safe method in terms of privacy and speed.

*Analogy: It's like storing your information in a locked drawer in your own home, rather than in a bank vault.*

## How it works
Data is kept in a local database, and apps on your device talk directly to these local files.

## Where it is used
It is used in privacy-oriented note applications and local artificial intelligence systems.

## Commonly confused with
It may be confused with working only offline, but in this approach, you have complete ownership of the data.

## Frequently asked questions
**If my device breaks down, will my data be lost?**
Since it is stored locally, you need to back up the device; as in cloud systems, extra settings may be required for automatic backup.
