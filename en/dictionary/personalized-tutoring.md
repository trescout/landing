# What is Personalized Tutoring?

*Dictionary · AI · Last updated: July 16, 2026*

Artificial intelligence-supported special education adapted to the student's understanding speed and deficiencies.

## Overview

Personalized education is when artificial intelligence detects where the student has difficulty and shapes the curriculum accordingly. Unlike a standard classroom lecture, if the student does not understand a topic, the system simplifies the topic or explains it with different examples. In this way, the learning process progresses at a completely personalized pace.

***Analogy:** Instead of a teacher teaching 30 people in the classroom at the same time, it is like a private teacher who only cares about you and repeats the parts you do not understand in different ways.*

## How it works

The system analyzes the student's answers and the time spent. If a mistake is made on a subject, artificial intelligence finds the underlying deficiency of that issue and offers a new work plan focusing on that issue.

## Where it is used

It is used in language learning applications, online education platforms, and school support software.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does it completely replace the teacher?**

Usually no; It is positioned more as a tool that makes the teacher's job easier and allows the student to practice individually.
