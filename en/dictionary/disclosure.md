# What is Disclosure?

The process of disclosing a security vulnerability or confidential information in software to relevant parties.

## Overview
Disclosure is a protocol that determines how and to whom a security vulnerability is announced when it is discovered. Responsible disclosure involves notifying the software owner first and giving them time to fix it. Patent disclosure is the public submission of all technical details of an invention for legal protection.

*Analogy: It is like noticing a fire exit in a building and telling the building management first to give them the opportunity to warn everyone.*

## How it works
When a security vulnerability is found, it is kept confidential and reported to the manufacturer. Once the manufacturer prepares the patch, the situation is announced to the public.

## Where it is used
It is involved in cybersecurity reports, legal patent processes, and software development cycles.

## Commonly confused with
It is not just revealing something, but a process usually carried out within a framework of legal or ethical responsibility.

## Frequently asked questions
**What is responsible disclosure?**
It is the process of giving the manufacturer a chance to fix the vulnerability before announcing it to the public.

**Why is patent disclosure done?**
It is done to prove that the invention legally belongs to you and to secure it under protection.
