# What is Autonomous Robotics?

They are machines that can act by sensing their environment and making their own decisions, without human intervention.

## Overview
These robots see the world through their sensors, create maps, and calculate for themselves the best path to reach their destination. They don't just follow commands, they change strategies according to changing situations.

*Analogy: It is not a remote-controlled car, but rather a driverless vehicle that knows its destination on its own and changes its path according to traffic.*

## How it works
Artificial intelligence models operate the robot's decision-making mechanism by processing camera images and sensor data.

## Where it is used
It is used in warehouse logistics, agriculture, reconnaissance missions and production lines.

## Commonly confused with
The difference from programmed robots is that they can produce solutions on their own even in uncertain situations.

## Frequently asked questions
**Don't these robots make mistakes?**
They can make mistakes, so they are backed by layers of security and continuous learning algorithms.
