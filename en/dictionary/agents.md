# What is Agents?

They are autonomous artificial intelligence programs that use tools and make decisions on their own in line with the goals they receive from the user.

## Overview
Agents are different from a passive chatbot that just answers a question. They decide what tools they need to use to accomplish a given task and manage the process on their own. For example, when you want to find a flight ticket, they can not only search the site, but choose the appropriate one and proceed to the booking stage.

*Analogy: An ordinary AI is like a librarian who only answers the questions you ask. Agents are like personal assistants who do research, make phone calls and finalize work on your behalf.*

## How it works
Agents break down the goals given to them into small steps. At each step, they think about what they need to do, choose the necessary tools and control the result. If they make a mistake, they realize the situation and try to complete the task by changing their strategy.

## Where it is used
They are used for multi-step tasks in software development processes, such as writing code, performing complex data analysis, or personal calendar management.

## Commonly confused with
It is often used interchangeably with the terms AI Agent or Autonomous Agent, so these concepts are interchangeable.

## Frequently asked questions
**Do agents always make the right decision?**
No, sometimes they can choose the wrong tool or get into a loop, so human supervision is still important for critical work.

**Can agents go online on their own?**
Yes, they can make transactions over the internet using web browsers or APIs if they are authorized.
