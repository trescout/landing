# What is Deepfake?

The technology of adapting one person's voice or image to another person in a way that is indistinguishable from the real thing, using artificial intelligence.

## Overview
Deepfake uses deep learning methods to take an existing image or audio recording and manipulate it on another content. This technology can make it appear as if a person is having a conversation they never had. While it can be used for entertainment purposes, it is also carefully monitored for security due to the risk of misinformation.

*Analogy: It's like wearing a digital mask; However, this mask is so advanced that it is indistinguishable from your real face.*

## How it works
The artificial intelligence model learns the target person's facial expressions and speech patterns by examining thousands of images or hours of audio recordings, then applies this data to a new video.

## Where it is used
It is used in effects production in the film industry, entertainment content on social media and cyber security training.

## Commonly confused with
Not to be confused with simple photo editing; deepfake covers much more complex and moving content.

## Frequently asked questions
**How do I know if a video is deepfake?**
Eyeblink abnormalities, incoherence in mouth movements, or background distortions may be clues.

**Is deepfake legal?**
It varies depending on the purpose of use; Unauthorized and malicious use causes legal problems in many countries.
