# What is Voice Agents?

They are artificial intelligence assistants that communicate with the user by voice, answer questions and carry out commands.

## Overview
Instead of text-based chat, voice agents hear and understand your voice and respond to you by voice. Not only can they talk, but they can also perform tasks like controlling smart home devices or taking action on your calendar. Thanks to their natural language processing capabilities, they accompany you without disrupting the flow of conversation.

*Analogy: It's like a friend you've only been texting with now calls you and chats live with you.*

## How it works
Your voice is converted into digital data through your microphone, artificial intelligence processes it and produces a response, converting it back into sound and transmitting it to you.

## Where it is used
It is used in smartphones, customer service systems and smart home assistants.

## Commonly confused with
Can be confused with simple voice command systems; agents can have much more complex and contextual conversations.

## Frequently asked questions
**Can they speak any language?**
Yes, they can communicate fluently in supported languages.

**Is it safe?**
Voice data is often anonymised, but it's always important to check your privacy settings.
