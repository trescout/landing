# What is Texture Painting?

It is the process of adding color, texture, and detail to the surface of three-dimensional models by painting them with digital brushes.

## Overview
When you create a three-dimensional object, it is usually colorless or uniform at first. Texture painting is like dressing this object in digital skin; you make it look realistic by painting details such as rusted metal, leather texture, or fabric directly onto the model.

*Analogy: It is similar to bringing a sculpture to life by painting its clothes and facial details after creating a bare sculpture.*

## How it works
You open your model in 3D design software, then use brush tools to apply colors and patterns directly to the surface of the model.

## Where it is used
It is used in game development, animated films, and digital art projects to make characters or objects look realistic.

## Commonly confused with
It is not the same as just drawing a 2D picture, because what is being painted is a 3D model that has volume.

## Frequently asked questions
**Which programs are used?**
Professional 3D design tools like Blender and Substance Painter are the most common for this task.

**Why is it important?**
It is the most important visual stage that ensures models look realistic and convincing in the digital world.
