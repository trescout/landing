# What is AI-generated Content?

It is all kinds of digital content created by artificial intelligence models without human intervention.

## Overview
Everything produced by artificial intelligence, from texts to images, from music to videos, falls into this category. These contents are created by artificial intelligence creating new and unique pieces using patterns in the huge data sets it has learned.

*Analogy: Just as a painter examines thousands of paintings and develops his own style, artificial intelligence examines millions of contents and creates a new work.*

## How it works
The user gives a command, the artificial intelligence model calculates the probabilities within itself and produces the result. You just confirm or edit the result.

## Where it is used
It is used in social media content, advertising texts, stock images and software codes.

## Commonly confused with
It can be mixed with AI-assisted content; Here, production belongs entirely to artificial intelligence.

## Frequently asked questions
**Who owns the copyright of these contents?**
Although it is still a legally controversial issue, it generally belongs to the person or platform using it.

**Is it always true?**
No, artificial intelligence can sometimes produce false information (hallucinations).
