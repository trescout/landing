# What is Agent?

An intelligent software unit that can make decisions on its own and use tools to perform the targeted task.

## Overview
An artificial intelligence agent not only responds to what you type; It can search the Internet, edit files, or control other software on your behalf. These structures proceed step by step by breaking complex goals into small parts. Thanks to his ability to make his own decisions, he can bring things to fruition even if you are not watching over him.

*Analogy: An ordinary AI model is like an encyclopedia that you can only ask questions to. The agent is like an assistant who reads that encyclopedia, goes to the library, does research for you, and leaves the report on your desk.*

## How it works
Agents begin by analyzing a primary target they are given. They choose which tools they should use (for example, a calculator or web browser). If they get an error while performing the operation, they try again and finally present you the final result.

## Where it is used
They are frequently used in customer service bots, automated software development tools, and complex data analysis processes.

## Commonly confused with
Not to be confused with standard AI models that only produce text; agents go beyond producing text and take action.

## Frequently asked questions
**Do agents always make the right decision?**
No, sometimes they can choose the wrong tool or stray from the target, so it is important to keep them under control.

**Can agents check my computer?**
Yes, they can manage your files or run your applications if you give them the necessary permissions and tools.
