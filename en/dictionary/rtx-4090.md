# What is RTX 4090?

> GeForce RTX 4090

It is a flagship desktop graphics card with 24 GB of video memory, offering high graphics and artificial intelligence processing power.

## Overview
The RTX 4090 is a high-end graphics processing unit manufactured by Nvidia and powered by the Ada Lovelace architecture. Thanks especially to its 24 GB VRAM capacity, it is frequently preferred for running large language models and deep learning algorithms on local computers. It delivers exceptionally high computational performance in both graphically intensive applications and professional AI development processes.

*Analogy: You can think of this as a giant fleet of trucks on a highway that can carry dozens of heavy loads simultaneously at top speed, instead of standard vehicles.*

## How it works
It executes complex mathematical computations in parallel with thousands of independent processing cores. The dedicated tensor cores inside it accelerate matrix multiplications, allowing artificial intelligence model inference processes to be completed extremely quickly. Thanks to its high-speed memory bandwidth, it processes large data blocks without experiencing bottlenecks.

## Where it is used
It is used in local artificial intelligence laboratories, professional 3D modeling studios, and high-performance personal computers.

## Commonly confused with
It can be confused with data center-focused server cards; however, the RTX 4090 is consumer-grade hardware developed for individual users and workstations.

## Frequently asked questions
**Is the RTX 4090 sufficient to run local artificial intelligence models?**
Yes: Thanks to its large video memory, you can run many open-source large language models and image generation tools locally.

**What is the difference from a standard graphics card?**
Its high number of tensor cores and memory capacity make it many times faster in artificial intelligence computations compared to standard cards.
