# What is Intelligent UI?

*Dictionary · AI · Last updated: October 8, 2026*

An intelligent interface that learns the user's habits, adapts accordingly, and simplifies tasks.

## Overview

Intelligent UI is an interface approach that goes beyond classic static screen designs by predicting what the user might need at that moment. Thanks to artificial intelligence models, it analyzes the user's past actions and highlights the most necessary buttons or information.

***Analogy:** It is like a smart waiter who puts your favorite coffee and newspaper in front of you before you even sit at the table.*

## How it works

The interface communicates with the artificial intelligence components in the background, monitors user behavior, and dynamically rearranges the components on the screen.

## Where it is used

It is starting to be featured in modern mobile applications, smart assistant panels, and software development tools.

## Commonly confused with

It is different from classic dynamic interfaces; here, decisions are made by artificial intelligence models, not fixed rules.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does Intelligent UI look different for every user?**

Yes, it offers a personalized experience based on each individual's usage habits.
