# What is Text Generation Model?

*Dictionary · AI · Last updated: August 17, 2026*

It is an artificial intelligence model that produces meaningful texts by following a given starting sentence or topic.

## Overview

These models are trained on massive amounts of text data, learning the structure, logic, and knowledge of the language. When you give him a topic, he can make sentences and complete a paragraph or article by guessing what the next word should be. Not only can he write text, but he can also translate or perform tasks such as summarizing.

***Analogy:** It is like a very knowledgeable friend of yours who constantly reads books and is more or less aware of every topic in the world, and who tries to complete your next sentence while chatting with you.*

## How it works

The user enters a command, and the model creates the most logical continuation text using its own statistical probabilities.

## Where it is used

It is used in chatbots, content writing tools, and email drafting systems.

## Commonly confused with

It is almost identical to LLM (Large Language Model), but this term emphasizes that the focus is solely on text production.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does he always write correctly?**

No, sometimes he can make up unrealistic information, this is called hallucination.

**Does he only write in Turkish?**

It can produce text in many languages ​​of the world, depending on the data it is trained on.
