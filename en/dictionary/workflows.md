# What is Workflows?

*Dictionary · Dev · Last updated: September 22, 2026*

Workflow (referred to in Turkish as iş akışı) is the structured sequence of steps in a process.

## Definition and Word Origin

Flow means flow. The steps leading to the goal are ordered, and complex work is broken down into small pieces. The right flow reduces errors and increases speed.

***Analogy:** It is like a recipe; all the steps of ingredient preparation, cooking, and serving make up the workflow.*

## How to Know and Use in Daily Life?

**Development:** Code review pipeline.
**Support:** Ticket handling order.
**Office:** Approval chain.

## Technical Depth and Architecture

Flow description:

**Trigger:** Initiating event.
**Step:** Sequential tasks.
**Condition:** Branching rule.
**Output:** Result and log.

Trigger example:

```
on: [push]
```

This line tells the flow to run on every commit to the code. Automation rule: The second manual task is written to the candidate.

## Frequently Mixed Things

Mistaken for a pipeline. A pipeline is a technical data flow, while a workflow is a more general business process. Every pipeline is considered a workflow, but not vice versa.

## Use in Different Disciplines

**Specification:** Preparation, cooking, and service.
**Assembly:** Part order.
**Counter:** Registration and routing.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why automate it?**

Repetition breeds errors and wastes time. Automation cuts down on both.

**Can workflows change?**

Yes. It is updated as needs change, and its version is tracked.

**Where to start?**

Start with the most frequently repeated task. The steps are written down, and one of them is automated.

**Is a tool necessary?**

No. A paper list is also a workflow. A tool becomes necessary as volume grows.
