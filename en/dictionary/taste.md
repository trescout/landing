# What is Taste?

*Dictionary · AI · Last updated: August 7, 2026*

It is the ability to distinguish the aesthetic and functional quality of a technological product or design.

## Overview

Taste in technology is not just about how something works, but also about how elegant, consistent and effective the experience it provides to the user is. Having good taste means being able to present a complex artificial intelligence system with a simple and understandable interface or ensuring that a code is not only correct but also clean and maintainable.

***Analogy:** It's like a good chef knowing that food should be presented with a balance of flavors, not just to fill you up.*

## How it works

It is developed by analyzing user feedback, following design principles and constantly asking 'how can it be better?'

## Where it is used

It plays a critical role in product design, software development and creation of AI interfaces.

## Commonly confused with

It can be confused with just design, but it is a deeper vision where taste, functionality and aesthetics combine.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can taste be improved?**

Yes, it improves over time by studying different technologies, analyzing good examples and constant practice.
