# What is Benchmarks?

These are standard tests used to measure the performance, speed and accuracy of artificial intelligence models or software.

## Overview
Benchmarks are like a report card that allows us to compare how different models perform on the same tasks. Predetermined questions are used to measure a model's math ability, grammar, or coding ability. These tests help us understand which model is more suitable for which job.

*Analogy: It is like standard exams to measure the level of students; Everyone solves the same questions and who is better is determined by the score.*

## How it works
The model answers thousands of questions in the test set. These answers are scored as correct or incorrect and a success percentage is obtained.

## Where it is used
When new artificial intelligence models are published, they are used in academic research and in companies' model selection processes.

## Commonly confused with
May be confused with Inference; Inference is the work of the model, while benchmark is the measurement of the success of this work.

## Frequently asked questions
**Is the model with the higher benchmark score the best?**
Sometimes, but not always, models may have memorized the test questions.

**Can we do our own benchmark testing?**
Yes, you can test models with data specific to your own business.
