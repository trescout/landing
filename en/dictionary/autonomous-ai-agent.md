# What is Autonomous AI Agent?

It is a software system that can set its own goals and perform complex tasks end-to-end without human intervention.

## Overview
Autonomous artificial intelligence agents not only carry out given commands; At the same time, he decides for himself what steps he needs to take to achieve the goal. He chooses the tools necessary to solve a problem, realizes his mistakes, updates his strategy, and manages the process until he gets results. These systems execute workflows independently, much like an employee manages a project from start to finish.

*Analogy: An ordinary artificial intelligence is like a food processor, it just works when you press the button. An autonomous agent is like a professional chef who manages his own kitchen, goes to the market and buys ingredients if they are missing, and cooks the meal according to his plan.*

## How it works
You just tell the system the end goal. The agent breaks this target into smaller pieces. At each step, he checks the result of his work and decides whether to move on to the next step. If he encounters an obstacle, he tries to reach his goal by trying a different method.

## Where it is used
It is used in complex software development projects, market research processes and jobs that require multi-step data analysis. It is especially preferred in operational processes that require constant monitoring.

## Commonly confused with
It can be confused with an ordinary AI agent; however, autonomous agents have the ability to make decisions on their own without waiting for human approval.

## Frequently asked questions
**What happens if autonomous agents make mistakes?**
Quality autonomous systems can realize that they have made a mistake and go back or report the situation to the user and ask for guidance.

**Does he do everything by himself?**
Yes, it works within the defined limits and authorities until it terminates the process without the need for external intervention.
