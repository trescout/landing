# What is Playlist?

A playlist (or "çalma listesi" / "oynatma listesi" in Turkish) is a sequential collection of digital audio, video, or data content brought together to be played back consecutively according to a specific order, theme, or algorithmic logic.

## Definition and Word Origin
The term "playlist" is derived from the combination of the English words "play" and "list." Its most common and established equivalent in Turkish is "çalma listesi" or "oynatma listesidir." Its primary purpose is to ensure that the stream continues uninterrupted and appropriately tailored to the user's intent, without requiring the user to bother selecting a new file every time a piece of content ends.

## Frequently asked questions
**What does a playlist mean, what is its Turkish equivalent?**
Derived from the English words 'Play' and 'List', its exact equivalent in Turkish is 'çalma listesi' or 'oynatma listesi'.

**What is a Collaborative playlist?**
It is a shared playlist where multiple people can add and edit songs, podcasts, or videos to the same list via a common link.

**How to create a playlist on Spotify or YouTube?**
It is enough to go to the 'Your Library' section in the app, press the '+' (New Playlist) button, enter a title, and save your desired tracks using the 'Add to Playlist' option from the search bar.

**What is an M3U playlist file and how is it opened?**
It is a plain text-based index file containing internet addresses (URLs) of media streams and track names; it can be easily run by dragging it into VLC Media Player or IPTV players.
