# What is Local?

It means that programs or data run entirely on your device, without the need for the Internet.

## Overview
Most applications today send data to the cloud. In the local working principle, everything ends in the processor of your computer or phone. This means that your data does not go out.

*Analogy: It's like cooking in your own kitchen with your own ingredients, rather than eating at a restaurant.*

## How it works
Instead of using the software over the cloud, you download the installation files to your device. Even if you turn off the internet, the program continues to work with all its features.

## Where it is used
It is used in privacy-focused artificial intelligence tools, personal file management systems, and secure software development environments.

## Commonly confused with
It is similar to self-hosting, but local generally refers to a single device, while self-hosting refers to a server.

## Frequently asked questions
**What is the biggest advantage of working locally?**
Its biggest advantage is privacy; Your data never leaves your device.
