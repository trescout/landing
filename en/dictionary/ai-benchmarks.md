# What is AI Benchmarks?

*Artificial Intelligence · Last updated: October 17, 2026*

Performance tests that measure the reasoning, speed, and accuracy capabilities of artificial intelligence systems using standard scales.

## Overview

Test suites that evaluate the capabilities of artificial intelligence models, such as logical reasoning, code generation, language comprehension, or solving mathematics, on unbiased and standardized datasets. They provide developers and researchers with the opportunity to compare the strengths and weaknesses of different models. They play a critical role in tracking whether model updates bring progress or regression.

***Analogy:** It is like different cars being subjected to acceleration, braking, and cornering tests on the same track and under the same weather conditions to receive a report card.*

## How it works

Pre-determined question collections with known answers are posed to the model. The responses generated by the model are scored based on accuracy rates, latency times, and token consumption. The obtained scores are listed on global leaderboards and made available for comparison.

## Where it is used

It is used in technical reports when a new foundation model is released, on Hugging Face leaderboards where open-source models are ranked, and in enterprise model selection processes.

## Commonly confused with

It should not be confused with general hardware benchmarks. While classic tests measure processor and memory speed, artificial intelligence benchmarking tests measure the model's comprehension and logical reasoning quality.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can models memorize test questions?**

Yes. This situation, known as data contamination, can cause the model's score to appear artificially high by having seen the test questions in the training data beforehand.

**What are some examples of popular artificial intelligence tests?**

MMLU for reasoning, HumanEval for coding, and SWE-bench for the ability to solve real-world software bugs are frequently used.
