# What is AI-powered Note Analysis?

The important information in the notes is found and summarized or organized by artificial intelligence.

## Overview
It is the process of using artificial intelligence to draw meaningful conclusions from messy and long notes. AI automatically detects keywords, tasks, and important dates in your notes. In this way, your notes turn from a pile of information into actionable data.

*Analogy: It's like a personal assistant who reads a messy notebook, marks the parts that are important to you with colored pencils, and makes a summary.*

## How it works
You upload your notes to the system, or the system connects to your notes application. Artificial intelligence scans the text, extracts the important points and presents them to you in the form of a list or summary.

## Where it is used
It is used in organizing meeting notes, academic studies and analyzing personal diaries.

## Commonly confused with
Unlike tools that simply copy text, it understands the content of the text and gives meaning to it.

## Frequently asked questions
**Is my privacy protected?**
It depends on the data policy of the vehicle you use, locally working models may be preferred.

**Can it analyze handwritten notes?**
When combined with OCR technology, yes, it can.
