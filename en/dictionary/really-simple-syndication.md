# What is Really Simple Syndication?

> RSS

It is a content distribution system that allows updates or news on websites to be followed from a single place without the need to enter the sites one by one.

## Overview
RSS turns content on the internet into a 'feed'. When you use an RSS reader, this system automatically notifies you when the sites you follow publish new content. This significantly reduces the time you spend browsing the internet.

*Analogy: It is like a newspaper deliveryman who delivers the newspaper to your door every morning. Instead of buying newspapers one by one from the newsstand, they are all delivered to your door.*

## How it works
Download an RSS reader app. Add the RSS links of the sites you want to follow to this application. Now all updates will appear on a single screen.

## Where it is used
It is used in blog tracking, news sites and content curation platforms.

## Commonly confused with
It may be confused with Newsletter, but RSS is completely under your control and does not require email.

## Frequently asked questions
**Is RSS dead?**
Although its popularity has decreased, it is still one of the most efficient and clean methods for tracking content.

**Is it paid?**
No, it is a completely open and free standard.
