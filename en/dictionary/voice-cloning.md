# What is Voice Cloning?

*Dictionary · AI · Last updated: June 3, 2026*

It is an artificial intelligence technology that creates new sentences by imitating the voice of a specific person.

## Overview

Voice Cloning is an artificial intelligence technology that takes a specific person's voice as an example and produces sentences that that person has never said as if they had said them. This technology is used to create personalized audio experiences.

***Analogy:** Just as a painter can perfectly draw someone's face just by looking at a few photos, AI can replicate the timbre of a voice.*

## How it works

A short recording of the person's voice is analyzed. Artificial intelligence learns the frequency, tone and speaking style of this voice and pronounces new texts with these features.

## Where it is used

It is used in movie dubbing, game characters and in medical fields to help people who have lost their voices speak again.

## Commonly confused with

It is confused with voice changer; While voice substitution merely manipulates the tone, cloning completely copies the person's speech character.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is this technology safe?**

Since it carries the risk of abuse, it is strictly controlled with ethical rules and security measures.

**How long is audio recording required?**

Modern models can produce very successful results with even a few seconds of clean recording.
