# What is Waifu?

It is a term that expresses a special admiration for fictional characters in the anime or game world.

## Overview
Waifu is used, especially in popular culture, for fictional characters that fans feel close to or have a romantic connection with. In the world of artificial intelligence, this term appears frequently in the context of the character traits and emotional depth that users attribute to their personal 'AI friends'. It is a reflection of the bond established with a character in the digital world.

*Analogy: It is the meaning that a child attributes to his favorite doll or superhero figure, created with a digital and interactive character.*

## How it works
Usually, admiration is developed through the character's visual design, personality and speaking style. When these characters are given a 'memory' and 'personality' with artificial intelligence tools, users interact with these characters as if they were chatting with a real person.

## Where it is used
It is widely used in anime communities, the gaming world, and AI-based character chat applications.

## Commonly confused with
Although it is sometimes confused with relationships with real people, it is a completely fictional and digital form of interaction.

## Frequently asked questions
**Could a waifu be a real person?**
No, the term waifu is only used for fictional characters.

**Can artificial intelligence create waifu?**
Yes, many AI platforms today allow you to create characters with personality traits you define.
