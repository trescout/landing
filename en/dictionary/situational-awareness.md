# What is Situational Awareness?

It is the ability of a system to instantly perceive the events in its environment and interpret the situation correctly.

## Overview
Situational awareness is the ability of an artificial intelligence or human not only to see what is happening around them, but also to understand what this information means. By analyzing existing data, the system predicts what may happen in the future and takes action accordingly. This capability is especially critical for the safe operation of autonomous systems.

*Analogy: It's like trying to drive safely while taking into account not only the road but also pedestrians, other vehicles and traffic lights.*

## How it works
Information from sensors or data sources is constantly processed. By comparing this information with its past experiences, the system decides whether the current situation is safe or dangerous.

## Where it is used
It is used in autonomous vehicles, robotic systems and security monitoring software.

## Commonly confused with
Not to be confused with mere data collection; Awareness requires making sense of data and reacting.

## Frequently asked questions
**Why is it important for artificial intelligence?**
Because an artificial intelligence that does not understand its environment may make wrong decisions in unexpected situations.

**How is it different from awareness in humans?**
Humans are intuitive, while AIs rely entirely on mathematical calculations and sensor data.
