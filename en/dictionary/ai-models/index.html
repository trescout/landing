What is AI Models?

They are mathematical structures that gain the ability to make predictions or produce content by being trained with large amounts of data.

Overview

AI models are complex mathematical formulas that enable a computer to recognize patterns in data. These models can make reasonable predictions about new and previously unseen situations, thanks to the data they are trained on. They serve as the engine underlying the artificial intelligence applications used today.

Analogy: It is like a student specializing in a subject by reading thousands of books; Books are educational data, and the knowledge accumulated in the student's mind is the model.

How it works

First, a huge data set is collected and this data is fed into the model's architecture. The model adjusts its weights by learning the relationships in this data. When the training is completed, when the model receives a new input, it produces an output using the relationships it has learned.

Where it is used

It is used in image processing tools, text writing applications, recommendation systems and autonomous vehicle software.

Commonly confused with

It can be confused with all artificial intelligence software; The model is just the 'brain' part of the software.

Frequently asked questions

Can every model do every job?

No, models are often designed to specialize in certain areas (text, images, audio).

How long does model training take?

Depending on the size of the model and the size of the data, it may take days or even months.
