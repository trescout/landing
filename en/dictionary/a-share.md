# What is A-share?

*Dictionary · Data · Last updated: September 27, 2026*

A type of stock traded on Chinese stock exchanges and reserved for local investors.

## Overview

They are company shares bought and sold in the local currency on mainland Chinese stock exchanges. Traditionally, foreign investors' access is subject to strict rules and restrictions.

***Analogy:** Similar to tickets in a private marketplace that only specific club members can enter.*

## How it works

Companies offer these shares to the public on local exchanges, and transactions are conducted through special clearing systems.

## Where it is used

You encounter them in global financial analyses and portfolios focused on the Chinese market.

## Commonly confused with

Unlike general international stock concepts, it contains geographical and legal restrictions.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can foreigners never buy them?**

Limited access is now possible thanks to special programs and connection rules.
