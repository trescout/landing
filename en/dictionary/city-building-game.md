# What is City-building Game?

*Development · Last updated: October 11, 2026*

It is a strategy simulation game genre where players build a city from scratch, growing it by managing infrastructure and resources.

## Overview

A city-building game is a video game subgenre that assigns the player the role of a city's manager. The player draws road networks, designates zoning areas, provides infrastructure services such as electricity and water, and manages the budget. These games run complex economic, demographic, and traffic simulation systems in the background.

***Analogy:** It is similar to setting up a miniature city model at a desk and trying to balance its water supply, electricity, and the needs of its living populace in real-time.*

## How it works

The game engine calculates resource flows and road networks across the map. Simulation systems instantly simulate the commuting routes of city residents and disaster dynamics. As the player makes the right decisions, the city grows and new buildings and technologies are unlocked.

## Where it is used

It is used in video games like SimCity and Cities: Skylines, in the entertainment industry, and as urban planning educational tools.

## Commonly confused with

It can be confused with general warfare or real-time strategy games: in city-building games, the primary focus is not defeating the enemy, but making a complex infrastructure and life cycle sustainable.

## Questions and answers

**Why are these games difficult from a software perspective?**

Because they require intensive processing power and optimization since they demand the real-time calculation of thousands of independent vehicles, human movements, and economic variables simultaneously.

**How is artificial intelligence used in these games?**

Simulation algorithms are used in determining the traffic routes of city residents, building development decisions, and emergency routing.
