# What is Dial Keys?

It is a rotating mechanical keypad used to dial numbers on old phones.

## Overview
Dial keys are older generation phone interfaces that require a physical rotating motion, unlike modern touch screens. The user rotates the disk according to the number he wants to search and the signal is transmitted through a mechanical return process. Today it is a reference point for enthusiasts of technological nostalgia and industrial design.

*Analogy: It's like turning a disk left and right to find the combination for a door lock; You need to make a physical effort for each number.*

## How it works
You turn the circular disk on an old phone with your finger up to the corresponding number and release it. As the disk returns to its original position on its own, it sends the necessary signals to the telephone line.

## Where it is used
You will encounter it in antique telephones, museum collections and retro-themed decorative designs.

## Commonly confused with
Not to be confused with modern digital keypads or touch-screen interfaces.

## Frequently asked questions
**Why is it not used anymore?**
Because digital keypads offer a much faster, more practical and error-free calling experience.
