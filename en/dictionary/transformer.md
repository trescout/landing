# What is Transformer?

*Dictionary · AI · Last updated: June 3, 2026*

It is a powerful architectural structure that forms the basis of modern artificial intelligence models.

## Overview

Transformer is a revolutionary architecture that enables today's artificial intelligence models to understand sentences as a whole. Thanks to its attention mechanism, it simultaneously analyzes the relationship between words in a sentence.

***Analogy:** The old methods were like a student reading a sentence by looking at individual words. Transformer, on the other hand, is like a genius who focuses on the entire page at a glance and instantly sees which words complement each other.*

## How it works

The model gives an 'attention' score to each word in the text. For example, in the sentence 'I went to the bank because I ran out of money', it mathematically calculates the strong bond of the word 'money' with the word 'bank' and grasps the context in this way.

## Where it is used

It is at the heart of modern language models, translation tools, and text summarization systems such as ChatGPT.

## Commonly confused with

It is not just a model, it is a basic architectural structure on which models are built.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why is it so popular?**

Because it has the ability to process even very long texts without losing their context.

**Is it only used for text?**

No, today it is also used successfully in image and audio processing fields.
