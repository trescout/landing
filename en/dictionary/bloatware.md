# What is Bloatware?

They are software pre-installed on the computer or phone, which usually take up unnecessary space and slow down the system.

## Overview
Bloatware are applications that are included with the device when you buy it, but you do not use it most of the time. These software can run in the background, occupying the processor, draining the battery and filling up storage space. These software, which manufacturers add to the device due to advertising or pre-installation agreements, often negatively affect the user experience.

*Analogy: It's like having heavy unnecessary boxes that fill the trunk of a new car you bought, placed by the manufacturer even though you will never use it.*

## How it works
It comes with the operating system of the device and sometimes it is not allowed to be easily deleted by the user.

## Where it is used
It is found in smartphones, laptops and pre-installed software packages.

## Commonly confused with
Not to be confused with required system applications; Bloatware is software that is not optional and creates inefficiency.

## Frequently asked questions
**How do I get rid of bloatware?**
You can get rid of it by disabling the app from the settings menu or reinstalling the device with a clean operating system.
