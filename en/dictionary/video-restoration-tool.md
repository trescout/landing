# What is Video Restoration Tool?

*Artificial Intelligence · Last updated: October 19, 2026*

Software tool that repairs old or low-quality videos and enhances image clarity.

## Overview

A video restoration tool is software that improves corrupted, shaky, low-resolution, or worn-out old video recordings using artificial intelligence algorithms. These tools intelligently predict missing details to bring the image closer to modern standards.

***Analogy:** It is like giving a blurry and yellowed old family photo to a master painter and having it sharpened as if it were taken yesterday.*

## How it works

The artificial intelligence model analyzes each frame in the video one by one and detects distortions between pixels. It redraws missing or erased details by looking at surrounding information, stabilizes jitters, and revitalizes colors.

## Where it is used

It is used in archival work, digital transfers of old cinema films, and personal video editing projects.

## Commonly confused with

It can be confused with video editor tools, but video editors perform cutting and merging, whereas these tools directly repair pixels using artificial intelligence.

## Questions and answers

**Can these tools make black and white videos color?**

Many modern video restoration tools also have the ability to automatically colorize black and white content with artificial intelligence support.

**Does the processing take a long time?**

Due to the number of frames in the video and the intensity of the artificial intelligence's detail processing, it generally requires high processing power and time.
