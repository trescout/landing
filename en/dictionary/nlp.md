# What is NLP?

> Natural Language Processing

It is the ability of computers to understand and process human language.

## Overview
NLP (Natural Language Processing) is a branch of artificial intelligence that enables computers to understand, interpret and produce human language. Thanks to this technology, machines try to understand not only the words but also the meaning and emotion behind the sentences.

*Analogy: It's like giving someone who doesn't know a foreign language the rules and dictionary of that language, and helping him or her to converse fluently with you over time.*

## How it works
First, the texts are divided into small pieces. These pieces are then converted into mathematical values ​​and the model begins to predict the next word by learning patterns within the data.

## Where it is used
It appears every day in autocorrect features, customer service chatbots, and translation apps on smartphones.

## Commonly confused with
It is often confused with just language translation, but NLP covers much broader processes such as text summarization and sentiment analysis.

## Frequently asked questions
**Can NLP understand every language?**
It depends on the dataset on which the model is trained, but it has very high success rates in common languages ​​today.

**Do machines really understand language?**
No, machines actually operate on statistical probabilities, so there is sophisticated guessing rather than actual consciousness.
