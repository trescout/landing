# What is Instincts?

These are fast and automatic reactions that artificial intelligence gives without thinking, using the patterns it has learned during the training process.

## Overview
Artificial intelligence systems can act 'intuitively' in some situations because they are trained on millions of examples. This means that the model instantly produces the most probable result based on the experience it has gained, without making a complex analysis. Just like a person shows reflexes, the model makes quick decisions using the patterns it has learned.

*Analogy: It is like a pianist playing a piece without looking at the notes, through years of habit; He doesn't think, he just does.*

## How it works
The model remembers similar situations in the data set on which it was trained and predicts what kind of response is usually correct in those situations. This process is a statistical gain in speed rather than conscious reasoning.

## Where it is used
It is used in chatbots that need to respond quickly and in instant decision-making processes.

## Commonly confused with
It should not be confused with a conscious reasoning process; it's just a statistical speed.

## Frequently asked questions
**Can artificial intelligence's instinct make mistakes?**
Yes, because it is a predictive mechanism and can sometimes mistake wrong patterns for the right one.

**How to improve this feature?**
By training with higher quality and more diverse data, the model's ability to respond correctly is increased.
