# What is Automatic Tagging?

*Dictionary · Data · Last updated: September 22, 2026*

Automatic tagging is the process of reading the content and pasting a tag.

## Definition and Word Origin

"Tag" means label. The model scans the data, recognizes objects and concepts, and processes the appropriate tag from the defined list into the file. The archive becomes searchable.

***Analogy:** He is like the fast librarian who reads thousands of books and writes the category on the cover.*

## How to Know and Use in Daily Life?

**Photograph:** Object and face labels.
**Document:** Subject classification.
**Social:** Content layout.

## Technical Depth and Architecture

Order:

**Classification:** Assigning content to the cluster.
**Threshold:** Trust score below remains unlabeled.
**Control:** Human approval is critical.

Sample output:

```
{"etiketler": ["doğa", "deniz"], "güven": 0.92}
```

Rule: If the threshold is high, it is missing, if it is low, noise increases. It is adjusted according to measurement.

## Frequently Mixed Things

It is mistaken for manual labeling. That's the human hand, that's the model output. Speed ​​is in the machine, judgment is in the human.
