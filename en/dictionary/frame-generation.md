# What is Frame Generation?

*Artificial Intelligence · Last updated: November 14, 2026*

It is a method of adding new frames between existing frames using artificial intelligence to make the video stream smoother.

## Overview

It is used to increase the frame rate, especially in video games and video processing workflows. The AI, calculating the motion between two real frames, inserts entirely artificial intermediate frames in between.

***Analogy:** It is like ensuring fluidity by adding in-between transition drawings between two key animation drawings when making a cartoon.*

## How it works

It analyzes where the objects on the screen are moving and generates an intermediate image accordingly, displaying it on the screen.

## Where it is used

It appears in modern graphics card technologies and game performance enhancement software.

## Commonly confused with

It is confused with hardware upgrades; instead of increasing hardware power, it provides a sense of fluidity through a software trick.

## Questions and answers

**Does it cause latency?**

Since it requires extra computation, it can introduce a slight input lag, but it increases fluidity.
