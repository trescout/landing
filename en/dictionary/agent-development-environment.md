# What is Agent Development Environment?

*Dictionary · AI · Last updated: June 25, 2026*

It is a specialized software development environment used to create, test and manage artificial intelligence assistants.

## Overview

Developing AI agents is a bit more complicated than developing regular software because you need to monitor the agents' decision-making processes. These environments allow you to see how agents think and correct their mistakes.

***Analogy:** It is like a special laboratory used to train a robot's brain, where you can simulate its every move and watch the results.*

## How it works

In this environment, developers define what information the agent can access and what tools it can use. Then, they follow the decisions made by the agent step by step, detect logical errors or misdirections and make improvements.

## Where it is used

It is used in artificial intelligence-focused software projects and on the computers of teams developing agent-based systems.

## Commonly confused with

Not to be confused with standard text editors that just write code; There are also tools here to monitor the agent's 'thought' process.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why is a special environment needed?**

Because artificial intelligence agents can sometimes make unexpected decisions; These environments allow us to understand the reason for these decisions.
