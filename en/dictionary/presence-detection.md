# What is Presence Detection?

It is a technology that detects human presence in an environment with the help of sensors or software.

## Overview
Presence detection allows you to understand whether there is movement in a room or a certain area or whether someone is present. These systems often work using data such as camera images, temperature sensors, or signals from networked devices. It is used in many areas from smart home systems to security applications.

*Analogy: It's like an observer who notices you when you enter a room so that the lights turn on automatically.*

## How it works
The system constantly monitors data in the environment and is triggered when it detects a predefined movement or signal change. For example, when a software sees your phone connected to the network, it understands that you are in the room and changes the settings accordingly.

## Where it is used
It is frequently used in smart home automations, office energy saving systems and security alarms.

## Commonly confused with
It can be confused with motion sensors, but this system can track not only movement but also the entity itself.

## Frequently asked questions
**Will this system violate my privacy?**
It depends on how the system works; Some only detect motion, some do image processing.

**Which sensors are used?**
Various methods can be used, such as infrared, camera, Bluetooth or Wi-Fi signals.
