What is Scene Reconstruction?

It is the process of creating three-dimensional digital models of physical environments based on images.

Overview

This technology calculates the depth and position of objects in the environment by analyzing photos or videos taken from different angles. With the data obtained, a digital copy that is exactly the same as the real world is constructed. It is a critical method for machines to understand their environment, especially in the fields of robotics and virtual reality.

Analogy: It is like making a sculpture of an object on the table by combining the pieces of a puzzle; You recreate that object in the digital environment by looking at it from every angle, not just with your eyes.

How it works

First, camera data is collected, then the software creates a depth map by finding common points in these images and finally creates a 3D model by combining these data.

Where it is used

It is frequently used in autonomous robots, virtual reality applications and digital twin creation processes.

Commonly confused with

It is similar to 3D scanning, but this method is usually done with software via standard cameras rather than special scanning devices.

Frequently asked questions

Can it be done with just one photo?

Generally no, multiple images taken from different angles are needed to understand the depth of the environment.
