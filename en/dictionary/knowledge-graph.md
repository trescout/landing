# What is Knowledge Graph?

It is a structured information network that connects relationships between data and concepts.

## Overview
A knowledge graph is a structured data model that connects the relationships of data together like a map. In this way, artificial intelligence can easily discover hidden connections between seemingly independent information.

*Analogy: It's like unraveling a web of crime by connecting photos and names with red strings on a detective board.*

## How it works
Information is recorded in the form of nodal points and lines connecting these points. For example, an employee relationship is established between a person node and a company node.

## Where it is used
It is used to deliver more accurate results in search engines and to analyze complex corporate data.

## Commonly confused with
It is not an ordinary Excel table or list because the relationship between the data is important here rather than the data itself.

## Frequently asked questions
**What is the difference between knowledge graph and RAG?**
When searching RAG documents, the knowledge graph maps the logical relationships of concepts in those documents.

**Why is it called graphics?**
It is called this name because it is mathematically a network structure consisting of points and lines.
