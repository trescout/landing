What is Regulatory Restriction?

It is the restriction of technological activities, products or software by legal rules by states or institutions.

Overview

These are legal regulations introduced for security, privacy or ethical concerns in the technology world. These restrictions can determine what data an AI model can use or in which countries a software can operate. Developers must comply with these rules when designing their projects.

Analogy: It is like speed limit signs in traffic; Even if your vehicle is capable of going faster, you must slow down according to the rules.

How it works

They are published by governments or sectoral organizations in the form of laws, regulations or standards. Technology companies develop filters or restrictive measures in their software to comply with these rules.

Where it is used

Artificial intelligence ethics appears in data privacy laws and international software distribution processes.

Frequently asked questions

Why are restrictions needed?

To ensure user security, protect personal data and prevent misuse of technology.

How do developers follow these rules?

It is usually audited through legal departments or compliance teams.
