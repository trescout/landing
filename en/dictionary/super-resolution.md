# What is Super Resolution?

*Artificial Intelligence · Last updated: November 14, 2026*

It is a technology that makes low-resolution images clearer and higher resolution with the help of artificial intelligence.

## Overview

It intelligently enlarges blurry photos or videos with few pixels using artificial intelligence models. The model predicts missing details based on previous training data and adds them back to the photo.

***Analogy:** Instead of examining a blurry photo with a magnifying glass, it is similar to a painter imagining the missing details and redrawing them with a brush.*

## How it works

The artificial intelligence model examines the low-quality image, recognizes edges and objects, and then reconstructs their high-resolution counterparts pixel by pixel.

## Where it is used

It is widely used in photo editing software, gaming graphics, and the restoration of old video footage.

## Commonly confused with

It is confused with simple pixel-upscaling methods; while simple methods just enlarge the image blurrily, this method invents details using artificial intelligence.

## Questions and answers

**Is it adding real details or just guessing?**

The model makes logical guesses based on what it has learned from previous data.
