# What is Agent Fleets?

A group of multiple artificial intelligence software that work in harmony with each other to complete complex tasks.

## Overview
Instead of a single artificial intelligence, many small artificial intelligence representatives, each with a different expertise, come together and collaborate. While one agent does research, another writes code, and another checks the results.

*Analogy: It is like an orchestra; Each musician plays his own instrument, but they all form a single symphony under the direction of the conductor.*

## How it works
A centralized system divides the task into parts and assigns each part to the appropriate representative.

## Where it is used
It is used in complex software development projects and large-scale business automation.

## Commonly confused with
It is almost the same as multi-agent systems; The emphasis here is more on large-scale 'fleet' management.

## Frequently asked questions
**How do representatives understand each other?**
They communicate via a common protocol or messaging language.
