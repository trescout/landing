# What is Self-hosting?

It is a method of hosting software on your own servers or computers.

## Overview
Self-hosting is when you manage a service or software on your own hardware or a dedicated server you rent, rather than running it on someone else's server. It ensures data privacy and complete control.

*Analogy: It's like building your own house and taking on all the renovation and management work yourself, instead of living on rent.*

## How it works
You get a server or a powerful computer, install the necessary software there and make it accessible over the internet. All updates and data backup are your responsibility.

## Where it is used
It is used to set up your own cloud storage, run your personal AI models, or manage privacy-conscious websites.

## Commonly confused with
It is confused with cloud computing, but while you are using someone else's server in the cloud, here you have complete control of the hardware and software.

## Frequently asked questions
**Why is self-hosting preferred?**
It is preferred to prevent your data from falling into the hands of third-party companies and to have full control over the system.

**Does it require technical knowledge?**
Yes, it requires basic server administration and networking knowledge.
