# What is Side-by-Side?

*Development · Last updated: November 12, 2026*

> Utility Task Vehicle

A side-by-side is a small motorized off-road vehicle with a side-by-side seating arrangement designed for challenging terrain use.

## Overview

A side-by-side is a compact vehicle category that combines high off-road capability with passenger capacity. These vehicles, where the driver and passenger sit side-by-side, feature safety equipment such as a roll-over protection cage and seat belts. Thanks to their powerful suspension systems and four-wheel-drive structures, they move easily over rugged terrain.

***Analogy:** It is a small off-road vehicle that combines the off-road power offered by ATV engines with the safe seating arrangement of a car.*

## How it works

It is built with high-stroke suspensions placed on a strong chassis and a protective cage structure. Power from the engine is transferred to the four wheels via an automatic or manual transmission. Thanks to its wide track width, the risk of rolling over is reduced, ensuring stability on challenging tracks.

## Where it is used

It is frequently preferred in search and rescue operations, as well as in agricultural and forestry field work. It is also used in extreme sports and off-road excursions.

## Commonly confused with

It can be confused with an ATV. ATV vehicles have handlebars and the rider sits on them like a motorcycle, whereas side-by-side vehicles have a steering wheel, pedals, and side-by-side seats.

## Questions and answers

**Can side-by-side vehicles be used in traffic?**

Depending on local laws and the vehicle's license plate registration status, some models have permission to be driven on public roads.

**Are UTV and side-by-side the same thing?**

Yes, the term UTV refers to the general commercial and work-oriented category of these vehicles.
