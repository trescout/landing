# What is Introduction to Autonomous Robots?

It is the basic working principle of machines that can make their own decisions and move around without receiving constant commands from the outside.

## Overview
This field is based on robots perceiving their surroundings (sensors), processing this data (artificial intelligence) and performing an action (motors). It ensures that the robot not only follows the programmed path but also reaches the target by overcoming obstacles.

*Analogy: It's like telling a toy car where to go and expecting it to find its own way, instead of controlling it with a remote control.*

## How it works
Robots are fitted with cameras and sensors. The incoming data is processed with artificial intelligence models and it is decided what the robot will do.

## Where it is used
It is used in warehouses, factories and autonomous vehicles.

## Commonly confused with
Can be confused with remote controlled robots; they are completely under human control.

## Frequently asked questions
**How do they learn?**
Usually by constantly analyzing the data around them and by trial and error.
