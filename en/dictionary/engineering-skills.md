# What is Engineering Skills?

It is the ability to design and build systems using logic and mathematics to solve complex problems.

## Overview
It's not just writing code, it's the process of planning how to solve a problem most efficiently. This skill aims to create a working whole by bringing parts together. The engineering perspective allows you to make the best use of the limited resources available.

*Analogy: Instead of following a recipe exactly, understanding which ingredients are used and why and creating your own unique dish is like knowing culinary chemistry.*

## How it works
First break the problem into smaller parts, then choose the most appropriate logical solution for each part and combine these parts so that they work together.

## Where it is used
It is used throughout software development, architectural design and general problem solving processes.

## Commonly confused with
It can be confused with just coding skills, whereas coding is just the application part of this process.

## Frequently asked questions
**Can anyone acquire engineering skills?**
Yes, it is a skill that develops over time by practicing systematic thinking.

**Will it be useful to me even if I am not a software developer?**
It definitely makes it easier to solve complex problems in all areas of life.
