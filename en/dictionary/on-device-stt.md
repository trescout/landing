# What is On-device STT?

*Dictionary · AI · Last updated: September 22, 2026*

> On-device Speech-to-Text

On-device STT, or in-device speech recognition in Turkish, is the conversion of speech to text on the device.

## Definition and Word Origin

On-device means on the device. The local model runs without going to the cloud. Privacy is protected, and it is not affected by outages. It is like a translator who carries the dictionary in their head.

***Analogy:** It's like being in the same room with a translator who carries the dictionary in their head.*

## How to Know and Use in Daily Life?

**Notes:** Voice note on a plane.
**Command:** Offline assistant.
**Accessibility:** On-device subtitles.

## Technical Depth and Architecture

Balance:

**Model size:** Reduced (quantized) version.
**Hardware:** NPU and accelerator.
**Battery:** The cost of always listening.

Rule: The cloud is accurate, the device is private. Sensitive work is done on the device, hard work is done in the cloud.

## Frequently Mixed Things

Cloud recognition is assumed. It runs on the server, data goes out. This runs on the device, data stays.

## Use in Different Disciplines

**Dictionary:** The vocabulary carried in one's head.
**Calculator:** Computation without a battery.
**Flashlight:** Light without a network.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why instead of cloud?**

No connection needed, data stays on the device. Preferred for confidential work.

**Does accuracy drop?**

Slightly compared to the cloud. It is sufficient for daily work, critical text is audited.

**Which device runs it?**

New phones and computers. Those with an NPU run smoothly.

**Does it consume battery?**

Yes. Constant listening has a cost, it is turned on when needed.
