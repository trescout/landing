# What is Design Tool?

It is digital design software that allows you to create visual content, graphics or user interfaces.

## Overview
Design tools are like canvases that help you transfer your ideas to the digital world. Thanks to these software, you can create buttons, icons or professional graphics without dealing with complex codes. Today, artificial intelligence-supported design tools highlight your creativity by accelerating the drawing process.

*Analogy: A design tool is like a painter's brush and palette; It only works in a digital environment and when you make a mistake, you can correct everything in seconds with the 'undo' button.*

## How it works
First you choose a vehicle that suits your needs. Then you start working with drag and drop methods or ready-made templates. In the final stage, you export your design to share with others or to use in your software projects.

## Where it is used
It is used when designing website interfaces, preparing social media content or creating application prototypes.

## Commonly confused with
It's often confused with just 'code editor', but design tools are visually oriented.

## Frequently asked questions
**Do I need to be a graphic designer to use a design tool?**
No, modern design tools are simplified so that anyone can easily use them.

**How has artificial intelligence affected design tools?**
Artificial intelligence now allows you to make complex designs with simple commands or automatically improve existing designs.
