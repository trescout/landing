# What is Open Source?

They are software whose source codes are open to everyone to access and develop.

## Overview
Open source is the sharing of a software's source codes in a way that allows anyone to examine, modify and distribute it. This approach ensures that the software is developed and grown transparently by the community.

*Analogy: It's like publishing the recipe for a dish in a public cookbook instead of keeping it secret and allowing everyone to improve that recipe and make it more delicious.*

## How it works
Developers share their code on platforms like GitHub. Volunteer developers from all over the world work on this code, fixing bugs and adding new features.

## Where it is used
It is at the core of modern technology, from operating systems (such as Linux) to artificial intelligence models. Companies often choose it to reduce costs and ensure trust.

## Commonly confused with
It is confused with free software, but open source code is free, being free is a side effect.

## Frequently asked questions
**Is open source safe?**
Yes, because thousands of eyes examine the code, vulnerabilities are often found and closed faster than in closed source software.

**Can anyone change code?**
Yes, but the inclusion of changes in the main project usually depends on the approval of the project principals.
