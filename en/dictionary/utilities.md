# What is Utilities?

Helpful small software that facilitates the maintenance, management or basic functions of the computer.

## Overview
These are not huge applications, but small tools that make your job easier. They perform tasks such as compressing files, cleaning the system, or adjusting network settings.

*Analogy: Like a screwdriver or pliers in a repair kit; Even if you're not doing a big project, these are the tools you need to have on hand to handle small jobs.*

## How it works
They usually focus on a single purpose and work silently in the background to keep the system organized.

## Where it is used
They are located within operating systems or in small utility packages.

## Commonly confused with
Not to be confused with main software; These are tools that support the operation of the main software.

## Frequently asked questions
**Is every utility necessary?**
It depends on your needs, but the basics (cleanliness, security) are important for system health.

**Is utility software an application?**
Yes, but it is a more specific and small-scale application.
