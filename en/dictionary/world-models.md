# What is World Models?

They are systems that enable artificial intelligence to simulate future results by learning the rules and functioning of the physical world.

## Overview
World models are when an artificial intelligence not only processes data but creates an internal model of how the world works. In this way, the system can predict in its own mind what will happen as a result of an action before trying it in the real world. This ability helps artificial intelligence make more logical and realistic decisions.

*Analogy: It's like a chess master visualizing all the possibilities on the board before making a move. Thanks to this model, artificial intelligence can predict what might be behind the door or the consequences of its action before opening it.*

## How it works
The system creates a simulation environment using observations obtained from past experiences. It reduces the margin of error by testing different scenarios in this environment. It constantly updates this inner world with new data and produces more accurate predictions.

## Where it is used
It is used in robotic systems, in the driving decisions of autonomous vehicles, and in artificial intelligence that develops complex game strategies.

## Commonly confused with
It can be confused with models that make predictions based only on historical data; However, the world model comprehends not only the data but also the cause-effect relationship.

## Frequently asked questions
**Why is the World model important?**
It increases safety and efficiency by enabling artificial intelligence to predict results before performing trial and error.

**Does every artificial intelligence have a world model?**
No, most models just follow text or data patterns; The world model focuses on understanding the functioning logic of the physical world.
