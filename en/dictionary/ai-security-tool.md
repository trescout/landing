# What is AI Security Tool?

Software that protects AI models against cyber attacks, data leaks and malicious manipulation.

## Overview
Artificial intelligence systems can be fooled by unfamiliar commands (prompt injection) or misled by incorrect data. These tools instantly detect and prevent situations that threaten the security of the system by checking the data entering and exiting the model.

*Analogy: It is like a smart security guard standing at the door of an artificial intelligence model, checking the identity of every incoming visitor (data) and not letting suspicious ones in.*

## How it works
These tools, placed at the entry and exit points of the system, scan incoming commands, check for malicious content and mask the response if necessary.

## Where it is used
It is used in enterprise artificial intelligence applications, customer service bots and data processing centers.

## Commonly confused with
It is confused with general cybersecurity tools; However, these tools focus specifically on the working logic of artificial intelligence models.

## Frequently asked questions
**Does it provide one hundred percent protection?**
No security tool provides complete guarantees, but it significantly reduces risks.

**Does it cause the model to slow down?**
Yes, there may be a slight delay as every data is audited.
