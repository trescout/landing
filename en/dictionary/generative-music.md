# What is Generative Music?

*Dictionary · AI · Last updated: September 28, 2026*

It is a musical genre dynamically composed and continuously renewed through artificial intelligence and algorithms.

## Overview

Instead of playing a pre-recorded static track, generative music enables the real-time creation of music accompanied by specific rules and artificial intelligence models. These systems learn the rules of melody, rhythm, and harmony to create unique sound sequences every time. The listener encounters a different variation of the piece with every listen.

***Analogy:** Imagine a music box; however, every time the gears inside turn, it continuously plays a brand-new melody that has never been heard before, following specific compositional rules rather than random ones.*

## How it works

Artificial intelligence models learn the relationship between notes and instruments from thousands of hours of music data. They generate real-time sound waves using mathematical patterns based on the mood, tempo, or instrument preferences specified by the user.

## Where it is used

It is frequently used in background music in video games that changes according to the player's actions. It also has a wide range of applications in focus apps, royalty-free content creation, and digital art projects.

## Commonly confused with

It should not be confused with traditional composition. In composition, the sequence of sounds is fixed, whereas in generative music, the process is dynamic and constantly changing.

## Frequently asked questions

**To whom do the copyrights of the generated music belong?**

It varies depending on the terms of use of the platform utilized and the licensing structure of the artificial intelligence model.

**Does it lead to a decrease in performance in games?**

Thanks to optimized lightweight audio engines, it can generate real-time sound without straining system resources.
