# What is Rendering?

The process of a computer processing raw data and turning it into a visual or graphic that we see on the screen.

## Overview
Computers keep data in numbers. Rendering calculates the properties of this digital data such as light, color and shape and turns it into an image that we can see. This process requires complex mathematical calculations.

*Analogy: It is like a chef using the raw ingredients (data) he has and transforming them into a plate (visual) ready for presentation.*

## How it works
The processor or video card receives the data, processes it according to rules and colors the pixels of the screen.

## Where it is used
It is used in games, video editing programs, and viewing web pages in the browser.

## Frequently asked questions
**Why might rendering be slow?**
If the amount of data to be processed is too large or the computer's hardware cannot keep up with these calculations, the process slows down.
