# What is Clones?

It is the copying of a software project or data to another computer to work on it.

## Overview
In the software world, downloading a project with all its history and files to your local computer is called cloning. In this way, you can make changes to your own copy of the project without damaging the original.

*Analogy: It's like photocopying a book and taking notes on it; The original book remains intact.*

## How it works
Usually, using version control systems such as Git, the entire project directory is downloaded to your computer with a single command.

## Where it is used
It is used in software development, contribution to open source projects and backup processes.

## Commonly confused with
It's not just copying the files, it's bringing the entire development history of the project with it.

## Frequently asked questions
**If I change the project I cloned, will the original be affected?**
No, a clone is just a copy on your computer.
