What is Offline?

The system works with the device's own resources, without the need for an internet connection.

Overview

It is a system that works with the device's own resources, without the need for an internet connection. Since the data does not go to the cloud, it provides a great advantage in terms of privacy and speed.

Analogy: It's like having all the books in your own library at home, instead of going to a library that requires internet.

How it works

The artificial intelligence model is loaded into the device's processor and memory. All calculations are made on the device.

Where it is used

It is used in medical devices that require confidentiality, in regions where there is no internet or on personal computers.

Commonly confused with

It was thought that it could only work with simple models, but today powerful models can also work on the device.

Frequently asked questions

Will my computer get too hot?

Yes, running heavy models may tire the device's processor.

How does it get updates?

It is updated manually or through the system when you connect to the Internet.
