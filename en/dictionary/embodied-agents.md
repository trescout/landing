# What is Embodied Agents?

*Artificial Intelligence · Last updated: October 25, 2026*

These are robotic artificial intelligence systems that can move in the physical world, perceive their environment, and react accordingly.

## Overview

Embodied agents are artificial intelligence systems with a physical body that do not just produce text or code on a digital screen. They perceive the outside world through their sensors and perform physical actions using motor mechanisms. Autonomous robots, humanoid robots, and smart self-driving vehicles are among the clearest examples of this concept.

***Analogy:** While a digital AI consultant is like an advisor giving word-of-mouth recommendations, a chef who actually goes into the kitchen and cooks is an embodied agent.*

## How it works

They make instant decisions by processing visual, auditory, and tactile sensor data with artificial intelligence models. They transmit the decisions they make as commands to robotic arms or wheels, enabling movement in the physical world. During this process, they continuously map their environment and localize themselves.

## Where it is used

They are used in industrial automation, autonomous delivery robots, and surgical assistants in the healthcare sector. They are also preferred for hazardous search and rescue missions.

## Commonly confused with

They should not be confused with software-based autonomous agents; while software agents operate only in a digital environment, embodied agents have a physical presence.

## Questions and answers

**Can embodied agents replace human labor?**

They increase efficiency by supporting humans in hazardous, repetitive, and high-precision physical tasks.

**What technologies are used in these systems?**

Computer vision, deep learning, sensor fusion, and robotic control algorithms are used together.
