# What is Quantum Physics?

*Development · Last updated: October 25, 2026*

It is the branch of physics that examines the behaviors and energy levels of subatomic particles.

## Overview

Quantum physics is the fundamental science field that explains the behaviors of matter at the atomic and subatomic levels. It reveals that matter exhibits both particle and wave characteristics at microscopic scales where classical physics falls short. Today, it plays a critical role in the development of quantum computers and artificial intelligence hardware.

***Analogy:** If you compare classical physics to cars moving according to traffic rules, quantum physics is an extraordinary rulebook that examines the probability of that same car being in the garage and on the highway at the same time.*

## How it works

Systems process information by utilizing quantum principles such as superposition and entanglement. Thanks to quantum bits, or qubits, they can complete complex calculations in seconds that traditional computers would take billions of years to solve. This approach fundamentally transforms simulation and data analysis processes that require high processing power.

## Where it is used

It is frequently used in the design of quantum computers, cryptography systems, and materials science simulations. It is also utilized in drug discovery and complex data analysis projects.

## Commonly confused with

It differs from classical physics; while classical physics examines large objects in the everyday world, quantum physics covers the subatomic world.

## Questions and answers

**How does quantum physics affect artificial intelligence?**

Quantum processors allow artificial intelligence models to train massive datasets much faster and solve complex optimization problems.

**What is a qubit?**

Unlike bits in classical computers that take only the value of 0 or 1, it is the quantum unit of information that can exist in both 0 and 1 states at the same time.
