# What is Pruning?

*Dictionary · AI · Last updated: September 27, 2026*

It is the process of making an artificial intelligence model more efficient by deleting unnecessary connections that do not affect its performance.

## Overview

Pruning literally means trimming. Artificial intelligence models contain millions or even billions of connections (neurons). Some of these have almost no impact on the outcome. When these redundant parts are cleared, the model shrinks, consumes less memory, and runs much faster.

***Analogy:** It is like pruning the unproductive branches of a large fruit tree with too many branches to help it grow more lush and fast.*

## How it works

The weights within the model are analyzed, and the weakest and most ineffective ones are identified and completely removed from the system.

## Where it is used

It is used in optimizations performed to fit artificial intelligence models onto mobile phones or small devices.

## Commonly confused with

Unlike quantization, which simplifies numbers to reduce the model's size, pruning directly deletes unnecessary connections.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does pruning decrease the intelligence of AI?**

When done correctly, performance drops almost not at all; on the contrary, speed increases significantly.
