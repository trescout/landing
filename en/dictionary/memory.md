# What is Memory?

It is the ability of artificial intelligence to store past interactions and personalize its future responses based on this information.

## Overview
Artificial intelligence models are actually inherently forgetful; They start each new conversation from scratch. The Memory feature is like the model saving your preferences, information you have given in the past, or a summary of previous conversations in a 'notepad'. This way you don't have to introduce yourself from scratch every time.

*Analogy: It's like writing down the name of someone you just met in a notebook and calling them by their name the next time you meet.*

## How it works
The system saves the information it finds important during the conversation in a database and updates its answer by looking at this database with each new question.

## Where it is used
It is used in personal assistants, tools that track long-term projects, and customer service bots.

## Commonly confused with
It should not be confused with the model's own training data; This feature covers your private history with you.

## Frequently asked questions
**Is this information shared with others?**
It's usually associated only with your account, but it's important to check your privacy settings.

**How can I clear the memory?**
On most systems you can delete this data with the 'clear memory' or 'forget' command.
