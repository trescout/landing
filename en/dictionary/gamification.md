# What is Gamification?

It is the use of game mechanics to motivate the user in non-game areas.

## Overview
It is a method of adding game elements such as collecting points, leveling up, leaderboards or earning badges to daily applications so that people do not get bored while doing a task. The goal is to increase user loyalty.

*Analogy: To get a child to eat vegetables, it is like giving shapes to his plate or sticking a star on him for every meal he finishes.*

## How it works
When the user completes a task, you trigger the reward mechanism in his brain by giving him a virtual reward.

## Where it is used
It's very common in language learning apps, fitness trackers, and corporate training software.

## Frequently asked questions
**Can everything be gamified?**
Technically yes, but if it is not constructed correctly, it may annoy the user instead of motivating him.
