# What is CRISPR?

> Clustered Regularly Interspaced Short Palindromic Repeats

It is a sensitive biotechnology tool that helps modify the genetic codes of living things, just like editing text with a word processor.

## Overview
CRISPR is a technology that finds faulty parts of the DNA sequence and cuts or replaces them. It works similar to deleting an error in computer code and replacing it with the correct one. Today, it is frequently used for the analysis of genetic data in areas where biotechnology and artificial intelligence intersect.

*Analogy: It is like a 'find and replace' tool that finds a misspelled word in a document, deletes it and replaces it with the correct one.*

## How it works
Using a special protein and guide RNA, it focuses on a specific point of the DNA and makes the necessary editing there. Artificial intelligence models analyze genetic data to perform this process in the safest and most effective way.

## Where it is used
It is used in medical research, agricultural technologies and the treatment of genetic diseases.

## Frequently asked questions
**What is the relationship between CRISPR and artificial intelligence?**
By analyzing genetic sequences, artificial intelligence determines where CRISPR should intervene more quickly and accurately.

**Is this technology safe?**
It is a very sensitive tool, but it is subject to very strict controls due to ethical debates and margin of error.
