# What is Image-to-3D?

*Artificial Intelligence · Last updated: November 10, 2026*

It is an artificial intelligence technology that analyzes a two-dimensional photograph and converts it into a three-dimensional digital model with added depth.

## Overview

These are deep learning models that predict the hidden geometric structure behind a single image standing on a flat screen. The artificial intelligence calculates the object's light reception, shadows, and possible rear angles to construct a virtual sculpture. In this way, hours of manual modeling work are completed in minutes or even seconds.

***Analogy:** It is like looking at a single facade photo, accurately predicting what the other sides of the house look like, and creating a model of it.*

## How it works

The model scans the image, converts pixel colors into spatial coordinates using patterns learned from millions of object data points, and generates a 3D mesh file.

## Where it is used

It is used in game development, e-commerce product catalogs, augmented reality, and architectural visualization projects.

## Commonly confused with

It might be confused with panoramic shooting in classical photography, but here, a completely digital object production is involved.

## Questions and answers

**Does every photo successfully convert into a 3D model?**

Results are much more successful, especially in clear, bright photos where a single object is clearly visible.

**In which file formats can output be obtained?**

It generally provides output in common three-dimensional design formats such as OBJ, FBX, or GLTF.
