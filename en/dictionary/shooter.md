# What is Shooter?

*Development · Last updated: October 20, 2026*

It is a video game genre where players control a character and hit targets using firearms or energy weapons.

## Overview

Shooter is an action game category that focuses on the player's aiming ability, reaction time, and strategic positioning. Players control the character in the game from a first-person or third-person perspective. This genre constitutes one of the most popular and competitive areas of today's digital gaming industry.

***Analogy:** It is the computer-adapted and story-enriched version of the target-shooting game at a shooting range.*

## How it works

The game engine translates the player's mouse or controller movements into the crosshair on the screen. When the fire command is given, the trajectory of the bullet is calculated, and damage points are applied upon contact with the target.

## Where it is used

It frequently appears in e-sports organizations, online multiplayer platforms, and console games.

## Commonly confused with

It can be confused with action and adventure games. General action games may focus on puzzle-solving or melee combat, but the core mechanic of the shooter genre is aiming and shooting.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the main difference between FPS and TPS shooters?**

In FPS games, you see the world through the character's own eyes; in TPS games, you keep the camera behind the character.

**How does artificial intelligence work in shooter games?**

Behavior trees are used to enable in-game enemies to take cover, flank the player, and make logical decisions.
