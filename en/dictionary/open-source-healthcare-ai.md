# What is Open Source Healthcare AI?

They are artificial intelligence software that is shared so that everyone can examine and improve it in the field of healthcare.

## Overview
It is the sharing of codes of artificial intelligence models developed in subjects such as medical imaging, diagnosis or patient monitoring with everyone. In this way, doctors and software developers around the world can improve these models. Transparency increases trust in health technologies.

*Analogy: It's like the formula of a drug, instead of being kept secret, is shared in a public laboratory so anyone can study it and make it more effective.*

## How it works
Download open source health models from platforms like GitHub, test them with your local data, and share feedback with the community.

## Where it is used
It is used in hospitals, medical research centers and personal health monitoring applications.

## Commonly confused with
It may be confused with commercial health software, but the inside of these models is visible to anyone.

## Frequently asked questions
**Will these models replace doctors?**
No, it is designed as a helpful tool for doctors.

**Is it safe?**
Since it is open source, anyone can review it, so errors are found faster.
