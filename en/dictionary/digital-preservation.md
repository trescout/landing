# What is Digital Preservation?

*Dictionary · Data · Last updated: September 27, 2026*

It is the process of ensuring that digital data and files remain readable for many years against risks such as format obsolescence or corruption.

## Overview

Going beyond simply storing files on a disk, it aims to guarantee that future technologies will also be able to open these files. Converting aging file formats into new formats over time and monitoring the lifespan of physical storage media are part of this process. It prevents cultural heritage, corporate archives, and personal memories from disappearing in the digital era.

***Analogy:** It is similar to storing old paper parchments in special rooms to protect them from moisture and continuously teaching them to new generations so their languages are not forgotten.*

## How it works

Data is regularly checked, copies are stored in different geographical locations, and aging file formats are converted into modern standards.

## Where it is used

It is applied in digital libraries, government archives, museum databases, and large companies with long-term data storage needs.

## Commonly confused with

It is confused with a simple backup operation. Backup is copying the data as it is today; this process is planning how that data will be opened even on computers 50 years from now.

## Frequently asked questions

**How can I apply this at home for my own photos?**

You can take the first step by storing your photos in common and standard formats and transferring them to new disks at regular intervals.

**Is using cloud storage sufficient for this process?**

Cloud storage is a good tool, but it is important not to be dependent on a single company and to back up data in different formats as well.
