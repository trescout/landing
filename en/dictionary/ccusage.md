# What is CCUsage?

> Compute Consumption Usage

Consumption data that shows how intensively a system or service is used.

## Overview
It is a type of metric or reporting that measures how much a software consumes processor, memory or other resources, especially in cloud systems.

*Analogy: Just as the electricity meter in your home shows how much energy you use at the end of the month, it shows how much 'energy' the system consumes.*

## How it works
The system constantly monitors resource usage in the background and converts it into a consumption report.

## Where it is used
It appears in cloud billing panels or performance monitoring tools.

## Commonly confused with
Not to be confused with just 'number of users', this is more 'machine power' consumption.

## Frequently asked questions
**Why is CCUsage important?**
It is necessary to control costs and understand whether the system is working efficiently.

**What happens if it is high?**
There may be an inefficiency in the software or your system is under heavy load.
