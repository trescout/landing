What is Autonomous Robotics?

Autonomous robotics is the science of machines that operate without external support.

Definition and Word Origin

Robots see with sensors, map the environment, and calculate the path. They execute commands and change strategy according to the situation. They generate solutions in the face of uncertainty.

Analogy: It is not a remote-controlled car, but like a driverless vehicle that finds its way according to traffic.

How to Know and Use in Daily Life?

Warehouse: Transport between shelves.
Agriculture: Row following.
Exploration: Hazardous area scanning.

Technical Depth and Architecture

Parts:

Perception: Camera, lidar and radar.
SLAM: Simultaneous localization and mapping.
Plan: Obstacle-surrounding route.
Control: Error stop.

ROS is the common framework. Artificial intelligence processes image and sensor data, feeding the decision-making mechanism.

Frequently Mixed Things

It is thought to be a programmed robot. The former repeats a fixed path, while the latter generates solutions in the face of uncertainty. One is a conveyor belt, the other is a driver.

Use in Different Disciplines

Driverless vehicle: Pathing according to traffic.
Autopilot: Route maintenance.
Carrier pigeon: Return to target.

Frequently Asked Questions

Doesn't it make mistakes?

It does. It is supported by a safety layer and learning.

Where is it used?

In warehousing, agriculture, and exploration. It leads in repetitive and hazardous tasks.

What does it cost?

There is a cost for sensors and software. It is paid in exchange for labor savings.

What is the difference from the remote-controlled one?

The remote-controlled one is in human hands, while the autonomous one is left alone with its target.
