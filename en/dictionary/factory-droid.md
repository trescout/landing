# What is Factory Droid?

*Dictionary · AI · Last updated: October 8, 2026*

Automation robots designed to perform specific repetitive tasks on production lines.

## Overview

A factory droid is an automation system that performs repetitive physical tasks in industrial environments, such as assembly, welding, transport, or packaging. Unlike traditional fixed robotic arms, they are equipped with artificial intelligence and sensor technologies, allowing them to adapt to changes in their surroundings. They accelerate production processes and minimize human error.

***Analogy:** It is like a factory's tireless metalworker, fitting every part into place perfectly.*

## How it works

It scans its surroundings with advanced cameras and sensors like LiDAR, makes real-time decisions using artificial intelligence algorithms, and takes action with its mechanical arms or wheeled base.

## Where it is used

It is used in automotive factories, electronic assembly lines, massive logistics warehouses, and heavy industrial production facilities.

## Commonly confused with

They may be confused with general-purpose humanoid robots, but factory droids are typically optimized for specific and fixed industrial tasks.

## Frequently asked questions

**Are factory droids replacing human workers?**

They take on dangerous, exhausting, and repetitive tasks that people often find difficult to perform, guiding humans toward more qualified areas.
