# What is Generative Model?

They are artificial intelligence models that can create new and original content.

## Overview
Generative models can produce content such as text, images or audio from scratch by imitating data they have not seen before. These models have the ability to create new combinations using the patterns they have learned. Basically, they work on understanding the structure of the data and creating similar ones.

*Analogy: He is like a creative chef who, instead of just reading recipes, invents delicious new dishes that have never been cooked before, based on those recipes.*

## How it works
These models are trained on huge data sets and learn the hidden rules and relationships within the data. After training, they produce a new output with probability calculations based on an initial command given to them.

## Where it is used
They are used in areas that require creativity, such as artistic visual production, copywriting and music composition.

## Commonly confused with
They are often confused with models that merely classify what exists; whereas these are focused on creating something new.

## Frequently asked questions
**Do productive models always tell the truth?**
No, sometimes they can produce information that seems realistic but is false, this is called hallucination.

**Can these models think like humans?**
No, they just make predictions based on mathematical probabilities.
