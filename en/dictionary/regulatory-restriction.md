# What is Regulatory Restriction?

Regulatory restriction is the legal framework that limits the development, data use, distribution or export of technologies by governments, international authorities or sectoral regulatory bodies with binding rules.

## Definition and Meaning (Regulatory Restrictions Meaning)
The concept of "Regulatory restrictions" is called regulatory restrictions, legislative limitations or legal regulation obstacles in Turkish. It is especially applied to ensure user rights, data privacy and public security in critical areas such as artificial intelligence (AI), financial technologies (FinTech), health and cryptography.

## Major Areas of Regulation and Restriction
Data Privacy and Sovereignty (KVKK, GDPR, CCPA): Prohibits the collection, processing, or unauthorized transfer of users' personal data to cross-border cloud servers without consent.

## Importance for Developers: Compatibility from Design
Software teams must observe security and regulatory standards when designing the architecture (Compliance by Design / Privacy by Design), not at the final stage of the project. Otherwise, there is a risk that products will be removed from application stores or blocked from access to target markets.

## Commonly confused with
Internal platform policies (Terms of Service / Acceptable Use Policy) should not be confused with legal regulatory restrictions. Platform rules are companies' own business preferences; Regulatory restrictions are state laws that, if violated, result in huge fines and legal sanctions relative to the company's turnover.

## Frequently asked questions
**Regulatory restrictions meaning?**
These are legal restrictions imposed by states or authorized independent institutions on technological products for the purposes of public security, consumer rights and data privacy.

**How to ensure regulatory compliance in software projects?**
Data encryption is achieved through role-based access control, regular security audits, and architectural designs coordinated with legal experts.

**What does the European Union Artificial Intelligence Act (EU AI Act) restrict?**
It restricts cognitive systems that manipulate human behavior, biometric classification, and opaque black box algorithms in high-risk areas such as recruitment.

**What is the penalty for non-compliance with regulatory restrictions?**
Under GDPR, fines of up to 4% of annual global turnover, complete bans of the product in certain countries and serious reputational losses may occur.
