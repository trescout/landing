# What is CNN?

*Artificial Intelligence · Last updated: October 26, 2026*

> Convolutional Neural Network

It is a deep learning architecture inspired by biological visual systems, developed to analyze visual data and images.

## Overview

Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) are layered artificial intelligence models that automatically detect patterns such as edges, textures, and shapes in images. They extract prominent features by scanning pixel matrices in images using filters. It is one of the fundamental standards in object recognition, image classification, and medical image analysis.

***Analogy:** It works like a detective that scans an image piece by piece with a magnifying glass, first recognizing lines, then shapes, and finally the entire face.*

## How it works

The input image is passed through convolutional layers to extract small feature maps. Pooling layers downscale the data, and finally, fully connected layers predict what the image is.

## Where it is used

It is widely used in autonomous vehicle lane tracking, face recognition in security cameras, and X-ray analysis in the healthcare sector.

## Commonly confused with

Unlike text-focused Transformer models, it is designed to process 2D and 3D visual data grids directly.

## Questions and answers

**Are CNN models only used for images?**

Although primarily used for images, it also yields successful results on other grid-structured data such as sound waves and time series.

**What is the difference between a CNN and traditional artificial neural networks?**

CNNs use convolution filters to preserve pixel relationships and spatial locations, thereby achieving much higher success rates in images.
