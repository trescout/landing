# What is CRO?

> Conversion Rate Optimization

These are improvements made to increase the rate of visitors to a website becoming customers or subscribers.

## Overview
Conversion rate optimization is the art of using traffic to your site more efficiently. The aim is for visitors to click a button, fill out a form or make a purchase, rather than just looking at the site and leaving. At TreScout, we see this as constantly arranging your store's display to draw customers in.

*Analogy: If only 1 in 100 people entering the door of a store makes purchases, it is like trying to increase this number to 5 by changing the store layout.*

## How it works
User behavior is analyzed, different versions of the site are tested, and the designs or texts that give the best results are made permanent.

## Where it is used
It is used on e-commerce sites, digital marketing campaigns and user experience (UX) improvements.

## Commonly confused with
It is not just about attracting more visitors (SEO), it is about using the visitors efficiently.

## Frequently asked questions
**Does it give fast results?**
It is a process that requires patience as there are testing processes and data collection stages.
