# What is Free Tier?

It is the entry level of a service offered for free with limited features.

## Overview
It is a trial model that companies offer to get users used to the system. It is usually a package in which a certain usage quota or some advanced features are restricted. When the need increases, users switch to paid plans.

*Analogy: It's like being given a small glass of trial drink before buying coffee in a cafe.*

## How it works
The user creates an account and the system automatically defines free limits. When the limit is reached, the system warns you and asks for an upgrade.

## Where it is used
It is common in software services (SaaS), AI APIs, and cloud platforms.

## Commonly confused with
Not to be confused with completely free software; it is part of a business model.

## Frequently asked questions
**Unlimited?**
No, there are usually restrictions based on usage amount or features.

**When do I have to pay a fee?**
You pay when you exceed your limits or need more advanced features.
