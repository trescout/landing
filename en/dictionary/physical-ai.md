# What is Physical AI?

Artificial intelligence systems that can control objects in the physical world, such as robots, and interact with the environment.

## Overview
Unlike AI that merely produces text or images on a screen, physical AI manages real-world actions. It performs an action in the physical world using data from sensors. This technology enables machines to not only understand the world, but to touch and change it.

*Analogy: It is like an artificial intelligence that only theoretically reads a recipe turns into a robot that enters the kitchen and actually cooks food using the ingredients.*

## How it works
The system detects the environment through cameras and sensors. It processes the information it collects like a brain and then gives a physical response via motors or arms.

## Where it is used
It is used in autonomous vehicles, smart robots in logistics warehouses and production arms in factories.

## Commonly confused with
Unlike virtual intelligences that work only in a software environment, they definitely require a physical body or hardware.

## Frequently asked questions
**Can it harm people?**
These risks are tried to be minimized with security protocols and physical restrictors.

**Are every robot physical artificial intelligence?**
No, unlike simple machines that simply execute basic commands, physical AI learns its environment and makes decisions.
