# What is AGI?

> Artificial General Intelligence

It is the goal of AI that exhibits human-level or above intelligence.

## Overview
Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) is the capacity of an artificial intelligence to perform any intellectual task as well or better than a human. While current AIs specialize in certain areas, the AGI goal is a versatile and self-learning intelligence.

*Analogy: Instead of a computer program that only plays chess, imagine an intelligence that can both cook and write poetry.*

## How it works
AGI adapts to new situations by combining different areas of expertise. It works not with a specific set of commands but with general reasoning and problem-solving abilities.

## Where it is used
It is not fully available yet, but it is considered the ultimate goal of artificial intelligence research.

## Commonly confused with
It is often confused with today's advanced language models, but they are still narrow intelligences that specialize only in certain tasks.

## Frequently asked questions
**How do we know when we have achieved AGI?**
We will be close to this when artificial intelligence can perform at an expert level without human assistance, even in an area it has never seen before.

**Is AGI dangerous?**
This is an issue that is at the center of ethical and security discussions, and whose controllability has been given much thought.
