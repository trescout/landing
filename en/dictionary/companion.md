# What is Companion?

*Dictionary · AI · Last updated: July 16, 2026*

It is a digital assistant that chats with the user, helps him or her in daily tasks.

## Overview

They are artificial intelligence-based software that is not only task-oriented but also able to establish a long-term relationship with the user. They remember your personal preferences, provide emotional support or accompany you in your daily planning. These models recognize the user and provide a more personalized experience over time.

***Analogy:** It is not just a secretary who does your work, it is like a digital friend who knows your interests and chats with you when necessary.*

## How it works

You interact in natural language, as if you were talking to a friend. It reminds you, exchanges ideas and lightens your workload in the digital world.

## Where it is used

It is found in personal assistant apps, smart devices, and dedicated AI platforms.

## Commonly confused with

Can be confused with AI Agent; While the agent is more task-oriented, the companion is more focused on companionship and interaction.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the difference between companion models?**

They have a memory that recognizes you and are designed to establish an emotional or social bond with you.

**Can anyone have a companion?**

Yes, today you can create your personal artificial intelligence friend through many applications or use ready-made ones.
