# What is Embed?

*Dictionary · Data · Last updated: September 27, 2026*

The process of converting data into numerical lists that computers can understand the semantic relationship between.

## Overview

It is called the process of turning texts, visuals, or words into number arrays that artificial intelligence can digest. This way, similarities between words can be measured mathematically. For example, the words king and queen are located close to each other in this world.

***Analogy:** It is like dividing thousands of books in a library into neighborhoods on a giant map according to their subjects; similar books always fall side by side.*

## How it works

The data you enter is passed through a special mathematical model and converted into lists of numbers, each corresponding to a point on a coordinate plane.

## Where it is used

It is frequently used in search engines, recommendation systems, and artificial intelligence memory management.

## Commonly confused with

Unlike the term embedding, which is the exact same concept, this is the action or process equivalent of the word.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why do we convert into numerical lists?**

Computers do not know the direct meaning of words, they can only process numbers.
