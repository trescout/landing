What is Agentic Skills?

It is a set of advanced capabilities that autonomous artificial intelligence systems use to manage complex processes and achieve goals.

Overview

Agentic skills are abilities that enable an artificial intelligence not only to answer questions but also to make a plan and implement this plan step by step. These abilities include skills such as decision making, debugging, tooling, and strategic prioritization. Unlike standard artificial intelligence, these systems determine for themselves what steps they need to take to complete a task.

Analogy: If we compare an ordinary artificial intelligence to a librarian, an artificial intelligence with agentic skills is like a library manager who manages that library, completes the deficiencies and guides the readers.

How it works

These capabilities are generally achieved by giving artificial intelligence a goal and defining tools for it, such as accessing the internet, editing files, or executing code. Artificial intelligence divides the task into parts, tests each part and reaches the result by changing its method if necessary.

Where it is used

It is used in complex software development processes, autonomous data analysis projects, and enterprise systems where multi-step workflows are automated.

Commonly confused with

It can be confused with the general concept of 'AI skill'; However, agentic skills refer to the ability to manage an autonomous process rather than a single process.

Frequently asked questions

Does every artificial intelligence have these skills?

No, only systems designed to operate autonomously and equipped with the necessary tools can use these capabilities.

Can these skills be learned?

Yes, artificial intelligence models can improve these abilities with special training processes or correct guidance (prompting).
