# What is Face Swapping?

The process of replacing a face in an image or video with the face of another person using artificial intelligence.

## Overview
Face swapping is a technology that takes the face of a person in a video or photo and seamlessly replaces it with the face of another person. AI matches facial features, lighting and angle to make the change look natural. It is generally used for entertainment and visual effects purposes.

*Analogy: It is like placing a realistic mask on the actor's face in a theater play and making that mask move with all the actor's facial expressions.*

## How it works
Artificial intelligence matches the key points (eyes, nose, mouth) between two faces and renders the new face into the video according to the lighting in the target video.

## Where it is used
It is used in social media filters, film post-production processes and entertainment applications.

## Commonly confused with
It is similar to deepfake, but face swapping generally focuses only on the displacement of the face.

## Frequently asked questions
**Is high hardware required for face swapping?**
Simple applications can run on the phone, but professional results require powerful graphics cards.

**Does it work at all angles?**
It gives the best results at angles where the face is fully visible; It may make mistakes in profile or very sharp angles.
