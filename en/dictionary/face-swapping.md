# What is Face Swapping?

*Dictionary · AI · Last updated: September 22, 2026*

Face swapping is the process of transferring the expression from one face to another face in a video.

## Definition and Word Origin

The model matches the contours, lighting, and angle of the source face to the target video. The result looks natural, but always leaves a mark. It is used in entertainment and effects works; legal problems may arise if used without permission.

***Analogy:** It is like a realistic mask that fits the actor's face in the theater and moves with all facial expressions.*

## How to Know and Use in Daily Life?

**Filters:** Social media face effects.
**Cinema:** Rejuvenation and stunt scenes.
**Entertainment:** Humor applications.

## Technical Depth and Architecture

The line is:

**Point matching:** There are eye, nose and mouth positions.
**Alignment:** The source face is rotated to the target angle.
**Light compatibility:** Color and shade are equalized.
**To mix up:** The edges are softened.

Detection clues: Blink rhythm, edge blur and light inconsistency are examined. The permission rule is clear: Someone else's face cannot be used without permission.

## Frequently Mixed Things

It is similar to deepfake. Deepfake also produces voice and script, face swapping generally focuses on facial transfer. Both require permission.

## Use in Different Disciplines

**Theatre:** Mask that moves with facial expressions.
**Stuntman:** Face change in dangerous scene.
**Photomontage:** Face stitching in frame.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is high hardware required?**

Simple applications run on the phone, professional results require a powerful graphics card.

**Does it work at all angles?**

It is better on the full visible face, the error increases in profile and sharp angles.

**Is it legal?**

Yes, on your own face and on off-duty jobs. Unauthorized use is a violation of personal rights.

**How to tell if it's fake?**

Eye rhythm, edge softness and light adaptation are examined. There are also detection tools.
