# What is NPCs?

*Development · Last updated: October 25, 2026*

> Non-Player Characters

They are characters that are not controlled by the player in games or simulations, moving with artificial intelligence.

## Overview

Non-player characters, namely NPCs, are components that bring the environment to life in digital worlds and interact with the player. While traditionally they followed pre-written scripts, today they can give dynamic responses with advanced artificial intelligence models. These characters drive the narrative and increase the depth of the virtual universe.

***Analogy:** They are like supporting actors in a play who accompany the lead actor and enter or exit the stage according to the flow of the play.*

## How it works

They utilize simple state machines or complex artificial intelligence decision trees. They produce instant reactions based on the player's position, actions, or verbal inputs. Recently, thanks to generative AI integration, they have become able to converse with the player in natural language.

## Where it is used

They are widely used in video games, virtual reality training, and military simulations. They are also utilized in customer service simulations.

## Commonly confused with

They differ from characters controlled by the player; NPCs are entirely managed by computer software or artificial intelligence.

## Questions and answers

**How is generative AI changing NPCs?**

It enables the creation of smart characters that give instant and unique responses to player inputs instead of fixed dialogues.

**Are NPCs only used in games?**

They are also used in training simulations and virtual reality environments to interact as required by the scenario.
