# What is Neural Kana-Kanji Conversion Engine?

*Dictionary · AI · Last updated: July 2, 2026*

It is an artificial intelligence-based software engine that converts Japanese phonetic writing into meaningful characters.

## Overview

Japanese consists of the characters 'kana', which stands for sounds, and 'kanji', which stands for meaning. This engine matches the phonetic sounds you type with the most accurate kanji characters based on context. Thanks to artificial intelligence, it provides a much more natural writing experience by guessing the meaning in the sentence.

***Analogy:** It is like automatic correction in Turkish, considering not only the spelling mistake but also the meaning and context of the word.*

## How it works

The user writes sounds on the keyboard, the engine analyzes these sounds and selects the character that best fits the rest of the sentence with the artificial intelligence model.

## Where it is used

It is used in Japanese input systems, mobile keyboards, and text processing tools.

## Commonly confused with

It is not just a dictionary; It is an artificial intelligence model that understands context.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why use artificial intelligence?**

Since there are so many words in Japanese that sound the same but have different meanings, understanding the context is essential.
