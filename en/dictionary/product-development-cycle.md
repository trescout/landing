# What is Product Development Cycle?

*Dictionary · Dev · Last updated: September 22, 2026*

Product development cycle is the stages of the product's journey from idea to user.

## Definition and Word Origin

Not just writing code; It covers market research, design, development and bug fixing. The word cycle is important: Release is not the end, but the back to square one with measurement and feedback.

***Analogy:** It's similar to the process a car takes from the design stage to being built in a factory, tested on a test track, and sent to the dealer.*

## How to Know and Use in Daily Life?

**Startup:** Quick tour from idea to first user.
**Institutional:** Long tour with approval gate.
**Open source:** Community feedback tour.

## Technical Depth and Architecture

Stages:

**Exploration:** Problem and user interviews.
**Design:** Sketch and prototype.
**Development:** Build piece by piece.
**Test:** Acceptance criteria audit.
**Broadcasting:** Gradual opening.
**Measurement:** Usage and error tracking.

Artificial intelligence shortens the cycle by speeding up the development and testing steps. The decision and responsibility remain with the person.

## Frequently Mixed Things

It can be confused with project management. Management controls the cycle, and the cycle is the business itself. One is the rudder, the other is the journey.

## Use in Different Disciplines

**Automotive:** From design to test track to dealer.
**Agriculture:** Seasonal cycle from planting to harvest.
**Book:** Publication process from draft to print.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How does artificial intelligence affect this cycle?**

It accelerates development and testing steps. Decision, responsibility and user understanding remain with the human.

**How many stages does it consist of?**

Generally, 5-6 stages are counted. The names change, the logic is the same: Understand, do, try, publish, measure.

**How long does it take?**

It varies from weeks to months. Small improvement takes days, new product takes months.

**What is the difference between startup and corporate?**

The startup keeps the tour short and flexible, adding a layer of corporate approval and compliance. They both use the same skeleton.
