# What is Product Development Cycle?

It is the whole of the planning, production and testing stages that a product goes through from idea to reaching the end user.

## Overview
This cycle includes not just writing code; It includes market research, drawing the design, developing the product and fixing bugs. It is a continuous improvement process.

*Analogy: It's similar to the process a car goes through from the design phase, to being built in the factory, tested on the test track, and sent to the dealer.*

## How it works
It usually proceeds in the following steps: 'Plan, Design, Develop, Test, Release'.

## Where it is used
It is applied in software companies, the startup world and engineering projects.

## Commonly confused with
It can be confused with project management; Management controls this cycle, and the cycle is the business itself.

## Frequently asked questions
**How does artificial intelligence affect this cycle?**
It accelerates the development and testing phases, ensuring that the cycle is completed in a much shorter time.
