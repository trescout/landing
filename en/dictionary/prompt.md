# What is Prompt?

*Dictionary · AI · Last updated: September 22, 2026*

Prompt is a written instruction given to the model.

## Definition and Word Origin

"Prompt" means request. It is the basis of communication: Clear instructions bring clear answers. It is written with the trio of role, context and form.

***Analogy:** It is like a task list given to an assistant; As clarity increases, accuracy increases.*

## How to Know and Use in Daily Life?

**Chat:** Giving questions and tasks.
**Visual:** Stage description.
**Code:** Job description.

## Technical Depth and Architecture

Pattern:

```
Rol: çevirmen
İş: metni Türkçeye çevir
```

Rules:

**Context:** Necessary information is given.
**Border:** It is written that it will not be done.
**Example:** A sample is placed.

The bad prompt is short and without context. Good demand requires measurable output.

## Frequently Mixed Things

It is considered engineering. Prompt is the only instruction, engineering is the art of optimization. One is a sentence, the other is a craft.
