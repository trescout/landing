# What is Live Transcription?

It is the process of instantly converting spoken audio into text and displaying it on the screen simultaneously.

## Overview
This technology analyzes sound waves in real time and converts them into written text within seconds. It is especially useful in meetings or live broadcasts so that you do not miss what is said. As TreScout, we see this process as a digital note taker that turns conversation into an instant flow of writing.

*Analogy: It's like someone is doing shorthand for you very quickly and you can read what is being said while listening to it.*

## How it works
Voice data enters the system, artificial intelligence models process this voice and convert it into text data and display the results on your screen. The process is completely automatic and the text is completed as soon as the conversation is finished.

## Where it is used
It is used in video conferencing applications, live broadcast subtitles and voice note taking tools.

## Commonly confused with
Unlike traditional transcription, where audio is recorded and then transcribed into text, speed and instant feedback are essential here.

## Frequently asked questions
**Will there be a delay?**
Depending on the internet speed and the power of the artificial intelligence model used, there may be very small delays of milliseconds.

**Does it work in all languages?**
It depends on the language support in which the model is trained, current systems can now support many languages ​​simultaneously.
