# What is AI Companions?

They are artificial intelligence software that establishes an emotional bond with the user and offers support according to personal needs.

## Overview
AI Companions are artificial intelligence programs designed not just to provide information but to constantly interact like a friend or assistant. These systems provide a more personalized experience over time by learning your past preferences and speaking style. They can take on a wide range of tasks, from daily planning to emotional support.

*Analogy: It's like having a very knowledgeable friend in your pocket who always listens to you, knows your interests and gives you specific advice when you need it.*

## How it works
These software work by combining large language models with the user's personal data and preferences. They develop their character by staying in constant dialogue with you. They can set reminders for you when you need them or make your day by just chatting.

## Where it is used
They are used in personal assistant applications, education platforms, and social interaction-focused artificial intelligence platforms.

## Commonly confused with
They differ from standard artificial intelligence assistants, which only work task-oriented, with their ability to establish emotional bonds and become personal.

## Frequently asked questions
**Can AI Companion think like a human?**
No, they just simulate human-like behavior thanks to advanced algorithms and data processing capabilities.

**Is my data safe?**
Quality AI Companion apps encrypt your data, but you should always check the app's privacy policy when sharing personal information.
