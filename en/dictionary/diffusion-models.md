# What is Diffusion Models?

It is an artificial intelligence technology that produces high-quality images from scratch by cleaning noisy data step by step.

## Overview
These models start with a pile of completely random and meaningless noise. Artificial intelligence learns the patterns in this noise, gradually clarifying it and eventually transforming it into a meaningful image. The process is similar to sharpening a blurry image by slowly focusing it.

*Analogy: It's like you have a pile of sand in your hand and you slowly shape this sand and turn it into a perfect sculpture.*

## How it works
The model first corrupts the data with noise, then learns to recover this noise. Once the training is complete, you can have the model create a clean image from scratch by simply giving a command.

## Where it is used
It is frequently used in visual production tools, artworks and photo enhancement processes.

## Commonly confused with
Unlike traditional visual processing methods, these models do not copy data but create it directly from scratch.

## Frequently asked questions
**Do these models only produce images?**
It is generally visually oriented, but there are also versions that work with similar logic for audio and video production.
