# What is Voice Dictation?

*Artificial Intelligence · Last updated: November 4, 2026*

The process of instantly converting spoken voice into text by a computer.

## Overview

Voice dictation is a technology that allows you to type text into your devices just by speaking, without using a keyboard. Artificial intelligence models that capture your sound waves convert them into words and punctuation marks. Today, thanks to advanced AI infrastructures, this process works with almost zero latency.

***Analogy:** It is like having a professional secretary in the background who flawlessly puts every word on paper as you speak.*

## How it works

Audio signals received from the microphone are first converted into digital data. Then, speech recognition models analyze these audio segments to predict the most likely word sequence and write it on the screen.

## Where it is used

It is used when typing messages on smartphones, dictating long articles on computers, and in note-taking applications.

## Commonly confused with

It is almost identical to the concepts of Speech-to-Text or Transcription. However, dictation usually emphasizes the act of writing into a text field in real time.

## Questions and answers

**Is an internet connection required to dictate?**

Older systems required a cloud connection, but today it can also be done offline using on-device AI models.

**Why does it sometimes misspell words?**

Accents, background noise, and homophones can confuse the model.
