# What is AI Capabilities?

These are all functions that artificial intelligence systems can perform, such as analyzing, learning, producing content or solving problems.

## Overview
AI capabilities define the limits of a model or system and what it can do. These abilities; It covers a wide range of topics, such as writing text, recognizing images, generating code or making complex logical inferences. Knowing what capabilities your artificial intelligence has before starting a project allows you to choose the right tools.

*Analogy: It is like the sum of an athlete's physical skills, such as running, swimming or lifting weights.*

## How it works
Look at the documentation for the model you're using and examine what type of data it was trained with. Observe its abilities by assigning tasks of different difficulty levels to test the limits of the system.

## Where it is used
It is used during the artificial intelligence project planning phase and model selection.

## Commonly confused with
Can be confused with AI Skills; Capability is the innate potential of the model, and skill is the training of this potential for a specific task.

## Frequently asked questions
**Do the capabilities of artificial intelligence increase over time?**
Yes, its capabilities expand as it is trained with new data or augmented with better methods (like fine-tuning).

**Are the capabilities of each model the same?**
No, some models are better at creativity, some at logical analysis or writing code.
