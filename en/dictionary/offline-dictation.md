# What is Offline Dictation?

*Dictionary · AI · Last updated: June 30, 2026*

It is a technology that instantly converts spoken words into text without requiring an internet connection.

## Overview

Generally, voice recognition systems are connected to cloud servers. Offline dictation fits all this intelligence into the device itself. So you can transcribe your voice even on a plane or in places where there is no internet.

***Analogy:** It's like carrying a translator with you; It translates everything from its own memory without any help from the internet.*

## How it works

A lightweight AI model pre-installed on your device analyzes the sound waves coming from your microphone. It guesses the words in the sound and writes them as text on the screen.

## Where it is used

It's used in privacy-requiring note-taking apps, on-the-go professionals' devices, and native voice assistants.

## Commonly confused with

It is the same as voice-to-text but differs in that it does not require the internet.

## Frequently asked questions

**How can it work so smartly if there is no internet?**

It does not need the internet because there is a pre-trained, optimized small model (SLM) inside the device.
