# What is Frontier AI?

Frontier AI refers to the most capable current models.

## Definition and Word Origin
Frontier means boundary. These are systems with high processing power and reasoning capacity that go beyond standards. They run advanced tasks ranging from scientific discovery to strategic planning.

## How to Know and Use in Daily Life?
Research: Literature and experiment design.Engineering: Complex code generation.Analysis: Big data summary.

## Frequently Mixed Things
It is mistaken for AGI. Frontier is the best of today, AGI is the targeted general intelligence. One is the current peak, the other is the horizon.

## Use in Different Disciplines
Formula 1: The fastest car on the track.Everest: The summit route of mountaineering.Experimental aircraft: Frontier test platform.

## Frequently Asked Questions
**Can everyone use it?**
Due to cost, it is generally via a paid service. Open weights are the exception.

**What is the difference with AGI?**
Frontier is the current peak, AGI is the goal of general intelligence. One is today, the other is tomorrow.

**How is access provided?**
Via API and subscription. Quotas and pricing vary by model.

**What is the risk?**
Powerful capabilities carry risks in the wrong hands. Access is tiered and controlled.
