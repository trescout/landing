# What is Frontier AI?

The most advanced and highly capable artificial intelligence systems that push the current technological boundaries.

## Overview
These are AI models that possess very high processing power and complex reasoning capabilities, beyond currently accepted standards. These systems are used in many advanced fields, ranging from scientific discovery to complex strategic planning.

*Analogy: In the world of automobiles, they are like a state-of-the-art Formula 1 car compared to standard passenger vehicles; they represent the fastest and most advanced.*

## How it works
They are trained using massive datasets and the combination of thousands of graphics cards.

## Where it is used
They are used in advanced scientific research, complex problem solving, and large-scale automation.

## Commonly confused with
AGI

## Frequently asked questions
**Can everyone use it?**
Since they are generally very costly, their access is restricted or they are offered through paid services.
