# What is Image-to-Text?

*Artificial Intelligence · Last updated: October 19, 2026*

Technology that analyzes visual content and generates text that defines or describes this content.

## Overview

Image-to-text is the process that allows artificial intelligence to see an image, perceive the objects, people, and events within it, and write descriptions in human language. It is widely used in screen readers for the visually impaired or in visual search engines.

***Analogy:** It is like describing in detail, via voice, what the scenery looks like to someone standing by the bay using a phone.*

## How it works

The image is first scanned at the pixel level and broken down into its numerical features by artificial intelligence. Then, the language model kicks in to turn these visual elements into a logical sentence or paragraph.

## Where it is used

It is found in automatic caption generation systems of social media platforms, AI-powered search engines, and accessibility tools.

## Commonly confused with

It is confused with text-to-image, but it is the exact opposite; while text-to-image generates images from text, this technology generates text from images.

## Questions and answers

**Can it read handwritten texts?**

By working in conjunction with optical character recognition systems, it can also successfully convert text on images into text.

**Can it understand complex diagrams?**

Advanced multimodal models can also summarize what charts and diagrams mean in text form.
