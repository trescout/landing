# What is Cognitive Operating System?

It is an advanced software architecture that processes information and manages decision-making processes by imitating the working principles of the human mind.

## Overview
These systems not only process data; It understands the context, uses its memory, and makes decisions by learning just like a human. It produces solutions to complex problems by evaluating information as a whole, rather than dividing it into parts.

*Analogy: Imagine a 'thought operating system' that thinks and learns like the human brain, rather than the computer's standard Windows or macOS operating system.*

## How it works
It works with a combination of large language models, memory systems and decision-making algorithms.

## Where it is used
It is used in advanced artificial intelligence research and autonomous systems.

## Commonly confused with
Can be confused with traditional operating systems; This system is not software, but a thinking architecture.

## Frequently asked questions
**Can these systems really think?**
It does not have a biological thought like humans, but has a structure that can make complex logical inferences.
