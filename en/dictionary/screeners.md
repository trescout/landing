# What is Screeners?

*Data & Infra · Last updated: November 17, 2026*

Tools that enable investors to find the opportunities they are looking for by filtering financial data according to specific criteria.

## Overview

They are digital sieves that sort out stocks or cryptocurrencies that comply with the rules you set from among thousands of options. They list assets whose price is below a certain level or whose trading volume is high within seconds.

***Analogy:** It is like selecting size, color, and price filters when searching for shoes on the internet to see only the models you want.*

## How it works

The desired filters are selected from the user interface, and the system scans the massive database to display the matching results on the screen.

## Where it is used

It is used on stock exchange platforms, investment websites, and cryptocurrency tracking applications.

## Commonly confused with

It should not be confused with bots that trade directly; these tools only perform searching and listing.

## Questions and answers

**What data can be filtered?**

You can search based on many criteria such as price, market capitalization, trading volume, and technical indicators.
