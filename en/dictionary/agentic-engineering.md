# What is Agentic Engineering?

*Dictionary · AI · Last updated: June 19, 2026*

It is the discipline of designing autonomous software systems that can make their own decisions and achieve their goals.

## Overview

It is the art of building artificial intelligence systems that not only carry out commands, but also make their own plans and use tools towards a goal. This discipline designs how software will make decisions and correct errors when moving from one task to another. Unlike traditional software, the system can take initiative in ambiguous situations.

***Analogy:** It's like the difference between telling a worker 'Do this' and telling a project manager 'Finish this project'; The second one determines its own team and method.*

## How it works

Developers define a goal for the AI ​​and the tools it can use. The system progresses step by step using these tools and updates its strategy according to the results it receives at each step.

## Where it is used

It is used in the automation of complex business processes and autonomous software systems.

## Commonly confused with

Can be confused with AI Agent; This is a discipline, and AI Agent is the product of this discipline.

## Frequently asked questions

**What will be the role of the software developer here?**

The software developer no longer writes code, but becomes an architect who determines the goals and limits of the system.

**Don't these systems make mistakes?**

They can make mistakes, so traceability and security of the system is the most important part of this discipline.
