# What is SuperAgent?

Advanced artificial intelligence assistant that can manage multiple tasks simultaneously and solve complex operations.

## Overview
SuperAgent is more than a standard AI assistant. It not only answers questions but also manages complex workflows end-to-end using different tools. Can plan, correct mistakes and develop strategies to achieve the goal.

*Analogy: It's like the difference between an ordinary secretary and a project manager; One just takes notes, the other coordinates the entire project.*

## How it works
It is equipped with an advanced reasoning engine. It divides the task into parts, chooses the right tool for each part, and combines the results to produce the final output.

## Where it is used
It is used in the automation of corporate workflows, software development processes or complex data analysis.

## Commonly confused with
It may be confused with a regular AI Agent, but the SuperAgent has a much broader scope of authority and coordination.

## Frequently asked questions
**Can SuperAgent make decisions on its own?**
Yes, it can create its own strategy in line with the determined goals.

**Is every artificial intelligence a SuperAgent?**
No, being a SuperAgent requires multitasking and advanced tooling.
