# What is Minimum Viable Product?

*Development · Last updated: November 7, 2026*

> Minimum Viable Product

It is the simplest version of a product that contains its most essential features and is released to gather initial feedback.

## Overview

A Minimum Viable Product is the most basic version developed to understand whether a new idea or project will succeed in the market. Stripped of all unnecessary details, this version allows users to test the product and see its real-world reception. The goal is to validate the fundamentals of the project before spending a lot of time and money.

***Analogy:** You are trying a new recipe. Instead of preparing a whole banquet table, it is like making a single bite-sized taster to check the salt.*

## How it works

Identify the most critical value proposition of your idea. Write the simplest code or design the interface that provides this value. Put it in front of users, observe their reactions, and shape the missing parts based on this feedback.

## Where it is used

It is frequently used in the startup world, at the beginning of new software projects, and in product development cycles.

## Commonly confused with

It is confused with the finished and flawless state of the product; whereas it is merely the most basic skeleton.

## Questions and answers

**When is a Minimum Viable Product good enough?**

It is good enough when it is the first working version that solves the user's core problem.

**Why is the Minimum Viable Product process important?**

It prevents you from wasting months of work and time on the wrong features.
