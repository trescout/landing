# What is Paywall?

It is a digital barrier that requires payment of a fee to access content on the internet.

## Overview
It is the connection of some or all of the content offered by websites, such as articles, videos or reports, to a paid subscription system. The reader must pay or log in to pass this barrier.

*Analogy: It's like entering a library is free but you have to buy a library membership to read some special books.*

## How it works
When your internet browser enters the site, the system checks the payment status of your account and decides whether to grant access.

## Where it is used
It appears frequently on news sites, academic databases and specialized content platforms.

## Commonly confused with
It can be confused with free trials or ad content, paywall is focused on direct payment.

## Frequently asked questions
**Why does Paywall exist?**
It enables content producers to generate income and make quality content sustainable.

**Are all paywalls the same?**
No, some make you read the first few paragraphs, some lock the page completely.
