# What is Self-learning?

*Artificial Intelligence · Last updated: November 12, 2026*

It is the ability of artificial intelligence systems to improve through data or experiences they generate themselves without the need for human intervention.

## Overview

Self-learning refers to algorithms learning without the need for a direct data labeler or constant human feedback. The artificial intelligence model improves its capabilities over time by interacting with its environment or analyzing its own prediction errors. This approach makes it easier for systems to adapt to constantly changing conditions.

***Analogy:** It is similar to a chess player mastering the game without a coach, simply by examining thousands of matches they have played and the mistakes they have made.*

## How it works

The model observes the results of the actions it performs or deciphers hidden patterns in the data. When it makes incorrect predictions, it updates its internal weights to learn how to make more accurate decisions in the next step. The process operates through a cyclical self-evaluation mechanism.

## Where it is used

It is frequently preferred in autonomous vehicle driving systems, in-game intelligent bots, and dynamic decision-making mechanisms.

## Commonly confused with

It can be confused with continuous learning. While continuous learning emphasizes updating information without forgetting it as new data arrives, self-learning focuses on the autonomous execution of the learning process without human guidance.

## Questions and answers

**Can an artificial intelligence that performs self-learning get out of control?**

It can be kept under control because it operates within the framework of strict boundary conditions and safety rules determined by developers.

**Are large datasets necessary for this method?**

Since the model can generate its own data through simulations or self-supervised methods, it is not always dependent on massive external datasets.
