# What is Action Expert?

*Artificial Intelligence · Last updated: October 23, 2026*

It is a specialized artificial intelligence component that optimizes physical movement decisions in robotics and autonomous systems.

## Overview

The Action Expert is a specialized layer for managing complex physical movements in large models based on multi-expert architectures. It evaluates visual or textual inputs to calculate the concrete steps and control commands the system needs to take. This structure separates general decision-making processes from action-oriented motor control steps, enabling autonomous systems to operate with greater precision.

***Analogy:** It is similar to a conductor in an orchestra deciding the overall rhythm, while each instrumentalist executes their own finger movements with specialized muscle memory.*

## How it works

The system processes sensor data received from the environment and turns it into an overall plan. The Action Expert takes this plan and converts it into precise mathematical outputs such as joint angles, speed, or direction. Physical deviations are corrected instantly through continuously running feedback loops.

## Where it is used

It is used in autonomous vehicle driving systems, balancing algorithms of humanoid robots, and industrial robotic arm automation.

## Commonly confused with

It can be confused with the Mixture of Experts (MoE) concept. While MoE is a general architectural approach, the Action Expert is a specialized module of this architecture that focuses solely on physical actions.

## Questions and answers

**Is the Action Expert only used in physical robots?**

Although generally used in robotics, it is also preferred for motion control of virtual agents in simulation environments or digital game characters.

**What is its difference from other artificial intelligence layers?**

Its focus is directly on the triggering commands of mechanical components or control units, rather than language or reasoning.
