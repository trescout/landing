# What is Spatial AI?

*Artificial Intelligence · Last updated: November 2, 2026*

It is an artificial intelligence technology that perceives and makes sense of the physical world and the 3D locations of objects.

## Overview

Spatial AI enables artificial intelligence to work not only with text or two-dimensional visuals, but also with three-dimensional depth, distance, and spatial relationships. It bridges the digital and physical worlds by processing camera and sensor data. It has a wide range of applications from autonomous vehicles to augmented reality.

***Analogy:** It is like someone who has been listening with both eyes closed, suddenly opening their eyes and realizing where the furniture in the room is and exactly where they are standing.*

## How it works

It creates a digital map of the space by combining data from depth cameras, LiDAR sensors, and computer vision algorithms. Artificial intelligence models determine the boundaries, distances, and orientations of objects to make decisions on how to move in this space or manipulate objects.

## Where it is used

It is used in autonomous vehicle navigation, robotic surgery, augmented reality (AR) glasses, and smart warehouse robots.

## Commonly confused with

While the concept of Spatial Reasoning focuses on mental reasoning ability, Spatial AI is the entire system in which the physical environment is perceived and processed.

## Questions and answers

**What is the difference between spatial artificial intelligence and Computer Vision?**

Computer vision primarily analyzes two-dimensional pixel data, whereas Spatial AI makes sense of the environment's three-dimensional volume, depth, and spatial locations of objects.

**Is LiDAR required for Spatial AI?**

Although LiDAR is a common sensor, it is not mandatory; advanced artificial intelligence models can also generate depth maps from two-dimensional images coming solely from standard cameras.
