# What is Smart Meters?

*Dictionary · Data · Last updated: September 30, 2026*

Devices that instantly measure electricity or water consumption and send the data to the center digitally.

## Overview

You may remember the time when officials went door to door to read old-style meters. Smart meters completely digitize this process. It tracks your resource usage on an hourly or even minute basis and reports it wirelessly to the relevant institution.

***Analogy:** It is like a mini reporter attached to the water pipes at home, constantly whispering to the center how much water you spend.*

## How it works

It converts the consumption into digital data through the sensors on it and transmits it to the servers of the relevant energy company via cellular networks or Wi-Fi.

## Where it is used

It is widely used in smart home projects, modern power grids and municipal water management systems.

## Commonly confused with

It should not be confused with simple digital displays that only serve to read meters; smart meters offer two-way data communication and automation.

## Frequently asked questions

**How does it affect bills?**

Since it displays consumption instantly, it allows you to see at which hours you spend more and save money.

**Is my data safe?**

It is usually transmitted via encrypted protocols, thus trying to protect the privacy of your consumption habits.
