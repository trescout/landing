# What is DEX?

*Dictionary · Data · Last updated: September 12, 2026*

> Decentralized Exchange

These are platforms where users can trade their digital assets directly with each other without an intermediary institution or bank.

## Overview

Unlike traditional exchanges, you do not entrust your money to a company on decentralized exchanges. Transactions take place on the blockchain through automated rules called smart contracts. This way, you retain full control, and no one can freeze your account.

***Analogy:** It is similar to two people meeting on the street and trading products directly with each other instead of going to a bank branch.*

## How it works

You connect your digital wallet to the platform, select the assets you want to trade, and confirm the transaction to execute the swap.

## Where it is used

It is used in the trading of crypto assets and the world of decentralized finance (DeFi).

## Commonly confused with

It should not be confused with centralized exchanges (CEX); on centralized exchanges, your assets are under the company's control.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is it necessary to sign up to use a DEX?**

No, you only need to connect a digital wallet; no identity information is required.

**Is a DEX safe?**

Since there is no intermediary, you have control over your assets, but one should be cautious against smart contract vulnerabilities.
