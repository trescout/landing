# What is CFP/CFA?

*Data & Infra · Last updated: November 10, 2026*

> Certified Financial Planner / Chartered Financial Analyst

These are competency certifications held by professionals specializing in financial planning and investment management.

## Overview

These are titles obtained to prove credibility and expertise in the financial world. While CFP is focused on individual financial planning, CFA is built upon in-depth investment analysis and portfolio management. These certifications ensure that professionals work in full compliance with ethical and technical standards in the market.

***Analogy:** It is like carrying badges of expertise that are as valid as if they were carved in stone in the field, in addition to a diploma.*

## How it works

Candidates go through rigorous examination processes, complete relevant work experience, and must adhere to continuous professional development rules. This allows them to provide reliable advisory services in financial markets.

## Where it is used

You will frequently encounter them in the fields of banking, investment funds, asset management, and personal financial consulting.

## Commonly confused with

They may be confused with general economic terms or internal company titles, but these are global certifications issued by official and independent boards.

## Questions and answers

**What is the biggest difference between CFP and CFA?**

While CFP deals with individuals' personal plans such as budget and retirement, CFA performs large-scale investments and corporate analysis.

**Is it difficult to obtain these certifications?**

Yes, it requires years of study and multi-stage exams with low pass rates.
