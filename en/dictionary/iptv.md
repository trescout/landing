# What is IPTV?

> Internet Protocol Television

It is a method of digitally transmitting television broadcasts over an internet connection instead of a traditional antenna or satellite.

## Overview
IPTV allows television content to reach your home using internet protocol (IP) instead of standard cable or satellite broadcasting. In this system, broadcasts are divided into pieces, sent to you over your internet line, and reassembled on your device. Unlike traditional broadcasts, it allows interactive features such as selecting and stopping the content you want to watch.

*Analogy: Traditional broadcasting is like a radio station playing music to everyone at once; IPTV is like a digital music platform where you can choose and listen to the song you want whenever you want.*

## How it works
The data packet coming from your internet service provider is processed through a modem or a special box (set-top box). This box converts the signal coming from the internet into an image format that your television can understand. When you change the channel, the system requests the data stream of that channel in the background and reflects it on your screen.

## Where it is used
It is used in smart televisions, special IPTV boxes or viewing applications on computers and phones.

## Commonly confused with
It may be confused with streaming platforms such as Netflix or YouTube, but IPTV is an infrastructure technology that generally covers the transmission of live television channels.

## Frequently asked questions
**Is high-speed internet required to watch IPTV?**
Yes, a stable and sufficient speed internet connection is required for uninterrupted streaming of high-definition broadcasts.

**Is IPTV legal?**
IPTV is a transmission technology and is legal; However, pirated streaming services that offer content that violates copyrights are illegal.
