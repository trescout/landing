# What is Vector Store?

*Data & Infra · Last updated: October 21, 2026*

A specialized database that stores numerical data to enable artificial intelligence to perform semantic searches.

## Overview

A vector store is a specialized type of database that stores texts or images by converting them into numerical vectors and enables similarity searches among this data. While traditional databases look for exact word matches, these structures capture meaning similarities. This allows artificial intelligence models to find the most relevant information within massive piles of data in seconds.

***Analogy:** It is similar to a smart librarian who, instead of memorizing the contents of thousands of books word for word, groups them by subject and creates a massive emotional map.*

## How it works

Data first goes through a process called embedding, is converted into number arrays, and is saved in the vector store. When a search is performed, the query is also converted into the same format, and the nearest numerical neighbors in the store are found by calculating mathematical distances.

## Where it is used

It is widely used in RAG systems, semantic search engines, recommendation systems, and smart chatbot infrastructures.

## Commonly confused with

It expresses the exact same concept as the given existing term vector-database, it is just a different naming.

## Questions and answers

**Does a vector store replace a normal database?**

Generally no, while traditional databases are used for user information or logs, vector stores operate as a side system only for AI-focused semantic searches.

**What data is stored in a vector store?**

Text snippets, images, audio recordings, and their numerical representations generated by artificial intelligence models are stored.
