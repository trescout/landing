# What is World Simulation?

*Artificial Intelligence · Last updated: October 28, 2026*

It is a simulation technology that mimics the rules of physical or virtual worlds, object interactions, and changes over time using artificial intelligence.

## Overview

World simulation is the digital recreation of real-world physical laws, object movements, and environmental dynamics by artificial intelligence systems with high accuracy. This technology enables AI not only to generate text or visuals, but also to understand how the environment changes over time. Thus, autonomous systems and robots can be trained in safe virtual universes before gaining experience in the real world.

***Analogy:** It is similar to a pilot being trained in an advanced flight simulator that replicates all weather conditions identically before boarding a real aircraft.*

## How it works

Visual, physical, and spatial data are fed into large AI models to teach the rules of the environment. The model maintains the simulation by predicting the next state of the environment in response to given actions.

## Where it is used

It is widely used in testing autonomous vehicles, training humanoid robots, game development, and producing movie effects.

## Commonly confused with

Unlike the term "world model," which is a mental conceptual map, it refers to the complete and interactive virtual environment itself generated by this model.

## Questions and answers

**Why is world simulation important?**

It allows millions of scenarios that are dangerous or costly to perform in the real world to be safely tested in a digital environment.

**What is the difference from traditional game engines?**

While traditional game engines work with hand-coded rules, AI-powered simulations learn the rules directly from real-world data.
