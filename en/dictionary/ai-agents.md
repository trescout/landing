# What is AI Agents?

Intelligent software that uses tools to perform assigned tasks and can make decisions on its own.

## Overview
Artificial intelligence agents are your digital assistants that can not only answer a question but also take action towards a goal. They can choose the tools needed to do a job, correct their mistakes, and make their own decisions along the way. So, rather than a passive chatbot, they are more like an active worker who gets the job done for you.

*Analogy: An ordinary artificial intelligence is like a cooking book that reads the recipe to you. The AI ​​agent is an assistant chef who reads the recipe, enters the kitchen, chops the ingredients and cooks the meal.*

## How it works
Agents first analyze the target. Then they proceed step by step using the tools at their disposal. If they encounter an obstacle, they assess the situation, change their strategy, and continue the cycle until the task is completed.

## Where it is used
They are used in coding processes, customer support systems or complex data analysis.

## Commonly confused with
They differ from standard chatbots that only produce text by their ability to make independent decisions and use tools.

## Frequently asked questions
**What is the main difference between an AI agent and a standard chatbot?**
The chatbot only provides information, while the agent uses tools from the outside world to complete a task on your behalf.

**Can an agent make mistakes on his own?**
Yes, agents can make mistakes, but advanced agents are designed to recognize these errors and correct their methods.
