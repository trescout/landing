# What is Decoding?

*Development · Last updated: November 6, 2026*

It is the process of converting compressed or encoded data into a format that a computer can process.

## Overview

Decoding, or analysis in Turkish, is the process of revealing the original meaning of encrypted or compressed data. It plays a critical role, especially in converting numerical outputs generated by artificial intelligence models into words. Data is first encoded for secure transmission or storage to the other party, and then made readable through this process when it is to be used.

***Analogy:** It is similar to converting hidden encrypted words in a puzzle magazine into normal letters using the special key sheet you have.*

## How it works

Computer systems generally prefer compressed formats over their raw forms to transmit or store data. When this data reaches the target destination, the relevant software or hardware algorithm kicks in. It restores the data to its initial state by applying the encryption or compression rule in reverse.

## Where it is used

It is widely used in reading data packets in network communication, video and audio streams, data security, and text generation stages of artificial intelligence models.

## Questions and answers

**What is the difference between encoding and decoding?**

While encoding converts data into another format for transport or storage, decoding restores this format back to its initial readable state.

**Why is decoding important in artificial intelligence?**

Artificial intelligence models do not understand words directly; they work with numerical token sequences. This process is essential to present the numbers generated by the model to the end user as meaningful sentences.
