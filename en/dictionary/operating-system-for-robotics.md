# What is Operating System for Robotics?

> ROS

It is a special software infrastructure that manages the movements, sensors and complex parts of robots.

## Overview
Robots are not just about hardware; They need a brain that controls them. This operating system coordinates everything from the robot's movement of its arms to its ability to see the surroundings with its eye (camera).

*Analogy: He is like a conductor of an orchestra; It ensures harmonious music by telling the violinist, drummer and pianist what to do and when.*

## How it works
Different pieces of hardware send messages to each other through this system. For example, the camera sends the message 'there is an obstacle in front of me', and the system gives the 'stop' command to the engines.

## Where it is used
It is used in industrial robots, autonomous vehicles and robotic projects in research laboratories.

## Commonly confused with
Not to be confused with regular operating systems (Windows/macOS); These systems are optimized only for managing robotic hardware.

## Frequently asked questions
**Is it necessary to be an engineer to use this system?**
You don't have to be an engineer to learn the basic concepts, but it is recommended to learn it as it is a standard language in the world of robotics.
