# What is Global Intelligence Dashboard?

It is a management screen that collects and visualizes data from different parts of the world at a central point.

## Overview
Global Intelligence Dashboard is a management panel that brings together complex and dispersed data sources in a single visual interface. This panel enables large-scale data to be converted into meaningful graphs and reports. Thus, users can follow what is happening in different parts of the world in real time.

*Analogy: It's like the gauges in the cockpit of an airplane; It allows you to see critical information such as aircraft speed, altitude and fuel at a glance.*

## How it works
Data from different sources are connected to the system. The system processes this data, makes it meaningful and presents it on graphs or maps that the user can easily read.

## Where it is used
It is used in logistics tracking, global market analysis and operational management of large companies.

## Commonly confused with
It should not be confused with just a database; This is not a database, but an interface that presents data.

## Frequently asked questions
**Who uses these panels?**
It is generally used by managers, strategists and operational teams that need to monitor data in real time.

**What is used as a data source?**
Sensors, news feeds, social media data or corporate databases can be used.
