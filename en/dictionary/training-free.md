# What is Training-free?

*Artificial Intelligence · Last updated: November 4, 2026*

A method that can be used directly without needing to retrain the artificial intelligence model.

## Overview

Meaning training-free or requiring no training from scratch, this approach aims to grant new capabilities to an existing, ready-made artificial intelligence model with a new dataset without incurring costs. Weights remain constant, and results are achieved solely through prompt design or external memory methods.

***Analogy:** It is like handing a terminology dictionary specific to that sector to a master translator who already knows a ready language and putting them to work, instead of teaching them a language from scratch.*

## How it works

The model's structure or internal weights are not modified. Instead, the model's existing potential is triggered in different ways using clever command chains, external database connections, or specific logical guidance.

## Where it is used

It is frequently preferred in rapid prototyping processes, projects where cost avoidance is desired, and capability enhancement studies for artificial intelligence agents.

## Commonly confused with

It is confused with fine-tuning or retraining processes. In this method, the model's code or weights are not touched.

## Questions and answers

**If it does not require training, how does it produce smart results?**

Since the model already knows the basic language and logic, it reaches a solution by correctly interpreting externally provided smart guidelines and cues.

**What is its biggest advantage?**

Saving time and high server costs, and being applicable within minutes.
