# What is Newsgroup?

*Dictionary · Dev · Last updated: September 12, 2026*

A messaging and discussion platform centered around specific topics that was popular in the early days of the internet.

## Overview

An old communication network that operated similarly to email lists, where people exchanged ideas by sending text-based messages. This system, which can be considered the ancestor of modern forums and social media, operated on a distributed network of servers rather than a centralized structure.

***Analogy:** It is like a giant bulletin board in a neighborhood coffee shop where everyone leaves notes on a topic and others add their own replies under those notes.*

## How it works

You connect to the relevant server using special news reader software and follow discussion threads.

## Where it is used

It was used for information sharing and technical support in the early days of internet history.

## Commonly confused with

It should not be confused with today's instant messaging applications; it is much slower and text-focused.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is it still in use?**

Although used by a very small number of niche communities, it has been replaced by modern forums and social platforms.

**How is it accessed?**

It requires special client software that operates over the NNTP protocol.
