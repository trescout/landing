# What is AI Coding Clients?

They are artificial intelligence-supported software that accompanies the developer in coding, debugging and project completion tasks during the software development process.

## Overview
These tools work just like an experienced developer sitting next to you. It analyzes the codes you write, completes the missing parts and helps you solve complex errors. They significantly increase your productivity by adding a smart layer on top of traditional editors.

*Analogy: It's like driving a car and having a highly skilled co-pilot who constantly monitors your driving directions, traffic analysis and engine performance.*

## How it works
They are usually installed as a plugin or standalone application in your code editor. When you start writing code, it works in the background, providing suggestions, interpreting error messages, and sometimes writing an entire function for you.

## Where it is used
They are used in popular code editors such as VS Code, terminal-based development environments, and modern software development platforms.

## Commonly confused with
Can be confused with AI coding agents; However, clients provide an interface that is more under your control, while agents tend to complete tasks on their own.

## Frequently asked questions
**Will using an AI coding client prevent me from learning to code?**
No, on the contrary, when used correctly, it can speed up your learning process by allowing you to see complex logic faster.

**Is it safe?**
You should pay attention to whether your code is sent to the cloud; It is safer to choose local running models in projects containing sensitive data.
