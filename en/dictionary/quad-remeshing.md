# What is Quad Remeshing?

It is the process of converting the surface of a complex 3D model consisting of triangles into more regular and rectangular parts (quads).

## Overview
In the world of 3D modeling, especially in models produced with artificial intelligence, the surface structure usually consists of irregular triangles. Quad remeshing converts this structure into a quadrangular mesh structure that is more suitable for animation and editing. This process improves the quality of the model and makes it look smoother.

*Analogy: It's like turning a puzzle of irregular pieces into square pieces that are more easily assembled and organized.*

## How it works
Recalculate the surface of the model using remesh tools in 3D modeling software (such as Blender) or with special AI-supported plug-ins.

## Where it is used
It is used in game development, animation and 3D printing preparation processes.

## Commonly confused with
It should not be confused with mere model reduction (decimation); The aim here is to change the geometric order of the surface.

## Frequently asked questions
**Why not just use triangles?**
Rectangular structures give much more natural results in bending and deformation during animation.
