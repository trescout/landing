What is Dossier?

It is an organized file system in which all documents and information about a particular subject or person are brought together.

Overview

It collects all evidence and data about a topic in one place, whether digitally or physically. In this way, scattered information turns into a meaningful story. It is the primary reference when doing research or managing a project.

Analogy: It's like that big board that a detective hangs on the wall and combines all the photos and notes to catch the criminal.

How it works

Gather data from all relevant sources, arrange them in chronological or logical order, and keep them in a quickly accessible folder structure.

Where it is used

It is used in intelligence analysis, project management and comprehensive research.

Commonly confused with

It may be confused with just a folder, but a dossier is not just a file but an analyzed body of information.

Frequently asked questions

Why is creating a file important?

It allows you to make connections between information and see the big picture.

How to keep it digitally?

It can be easily managed with note-taking applications or databases.
