# What is Inference?

*Dictionary · AI · Last updated: June 3, 2026*

It is the process of a trained artificial intelligence model making predictions on new data.

## Overview

It is the process of a trained artificial intelligence model producing an answer to a new question or input using the information it has learned. This stage is the moment when the model no longer 'learns' but 'applies'.

***Analogy:** It is the process of a chef, after years of learning to cook, going into the kitchen and cooking a meal for you from scratch.*

## How it works

The user asks a question, the model passes this question through its huge digital networks and calculates the most logical answer step by step and displays it on the screen.

## Where it is used

The inference process occurs whenever you chat with ChatGPT or have an artificial intelligence draw a visual.

## Commonly confused with

It is confused with training the model; The education model is the school years, while the inference is graduating and starting to work.

## Frequently asked questions

**How long does the inference process take?**

It may vary from milliseconds to seconds, depending on the size of the model and the power of the computer.

**Does the model learn anything new during Inference?**

No, the model only makes predictions using the available information it has.
