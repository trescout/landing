# What is Jailbreak?

*Artificial Intelligence · Last updated: October 19, 2026*

An attempt to disable the safety filters or restrictions of artificial intelligence models.

## Overview

Jailbreak refers to special command tricks used to make artificial intelligence systems generate harmful or prohibited information that they would normally not provide. Through these methods, attempts are made to bypass the ethical boundaries set by the AI.

***Analogy:** It is like a driver using a secret code to turn off the speed limiter in a car, allowing the vehicle to reach speeds it normally cannot.*

## How it works

Malicious users bypass the firewall by giving the AI role-playing scenarios or writing confusing instructions. While the system would normally not answer a harmful question, it may forget its filter and respond when faced with these cunningly prepared commands.

## Where it is used

It is frequently encountered in artificial intelligence safety testing, red teaming exercises, and processes for closing model security vulnerabilities.

## Commonly confused with

It is very similar to the concept of prompt injection, but while prompt injection aims to directly leak hidden instructions into the system, jailbreak focuses on breaking the model's fundamental moral and safety rules.

## Questions and answers

**Is jailbreaking legal?**

It is generally accepted when done for research and security testing purposes, but malicious use can lead to system violations and security crimes.

**How do AIs prevent jailbreak attempts?**

Developers add additional security layers and filtering mechanisms that recognize harmful requests while training the models.
