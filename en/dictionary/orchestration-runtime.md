# What is Orchestration Runtime?

*Dictionary · AI · Last updated: September 27, 2026*

It is the runtime environment that manages complex artificial intelligence workflows and organizes the sequence of different tasks.

## Overview

An orchestration runtime is the background engine that enables multiple artificial intelligence models or tasks to work together in harmony. It determines which task should be performed first and which result should be passed to the next step. Thus, complex processes are executed flawlessly from a single center.

***Analogy:** It is like a conductor who ensures that all musicians in a symphony orchestra play simultaneously and harmoniously.*

## How it works

It operates according to the rules and workflows defined by developers, sequentially sends data to relevant artificial intelligence components, and collects the results.

## Where it is used

It is used in multi-stage artificial intelligence applications, agent systems, and automation projects.

## Commonly confused with

It differs from a standalone artificial intelligence model; the focus here is on managing multiple pieces.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why is an orchestration runtime needed?**

In large projects, it is impossible to track steps manually; this system makes everything automatic and orderly.
