# What is Relay?

*Dictionary · Dev · Last updated: July 23, 2026*

It is a system that transfers data from one point to another and carries it to places where the signal cannot reach or bridges the connection.

## Overview

Relay is a node that maintains the connection by acting as an intermediary in cases where data cannot go directly in the digital world. It is often used to extend the range of the network or to establish a secure bridge between two different networks. It is a kind of traffic police that ensures that data reaches its destination safely.

***Analogy:** It is like runners passing the baton to each other in a relay race; Each runner acts as a relay, ensuring that the flag reaches the finish line.*

## How it works

When a signal gets weak or needs to switch to another network, the relay receives that signal, refreshes it, and forwards it to the next point.

## Where it is used

It is frequently encountered in Wi-Fi signal boosters, email transmission protocols and complex network structures.

## Commonly confused with

Although similar to proxy, relay generally represents transmission at the physical or network layer.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why don't we connect directly?**

Relay prevents the connection from being lost when the distance is too far or the networks cannot reach each other directly.
