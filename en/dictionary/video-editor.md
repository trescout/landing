# What is Video Editor?

They are software that edits image and audio files and turns them into a video.

## Overview
Video editors are tools that help you combine raw video footage, audio recordings, and effects to create professional storytelling. Thanks to these software, you can cut unwanted parts of the videos, adjust color settings and add transitions between different scenes. Today, artificial intelligence-supported editors significantly reduce the workload by automating this process.

*Analogy: It's like a chef in a kitchen presenting a meal by preparing the ingredients, cooking them and placing them on a plate; You take the raw footage and prepare a meaningful video plate.*

## How it works
First you load your raw files into the program. Then you arrange, cut and combine these files in the order you want on the timeline. In the final stage, you adjust the audio levels, save your video and convert it into a shareable file format.

## Where it is used
It is used in every field, from professional film editing to social media content production.

## Commonly confused with
Video editing software is often confused with players that only watch videos.

## Frequently asked questions
**Is it necessary to be a professional to use a video editor?**
No, today there are editors with very simple interfaces that work with drag-and-drop logic.

**What does AI do in video editing?**
It can automatically cut, delete background noise, or recommend music suitable for the subject of the video.
