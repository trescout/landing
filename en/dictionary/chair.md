# What is Chair?

He is the person who heads an institution or the board of directors and is responsible for strategic control.

## Overview
The Chair is usually the person who leads the board of directors and oversees the overall direction of the organization. He/she has a say in corporate governance and long-term strategies rather than the company's day-to-day operational decisions.

*Analogy: He is like a judge in a court of law; moderates discussions, ensures compliance with rules, and regulates the process of making final decisions.*

## How it works
He organizes board meetings, sets the agenda, and oversees the performance of the company's CEO.

## Where it is used
He serves on boards of directors, associations and large organizations.

## Commonly confused with
Can be confused with CEO; The CEO manages the company, and the Chair manages the board that manages the company.

## Frequently asked questions
**Does Chair manage the daily affairs of the company?**
Generally no, the Chair is more concerned with the functioning of the board and strategic oversight.
