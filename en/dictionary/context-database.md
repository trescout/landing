# What is Context Database?

It is a private data store that stores the information needed by artificial intelligence to produce more consistent and personalized responses.

## Overview
When answering a question, AI models can use not only their own general information but also your past interactions with you or private documents. Context database is an area where this data is stored regularly and quickly recalled when needed. In this way, artificial intelligence can exhibit long-term and meaningful memory without forgetting the context.

*Analogy: It's like a librarian having a special agenda in which he notes what books you've read before and what topics you've done research on; so it can offer you the most suitable recommendation every time.*

## How it works
The system divides the data or documents coming from the user into pieces and saves them in this database. When artificial intelligence receives a question, it first looks at this database, finds the relevant information, and enriches its answer with this information and presents it to you.

## Where it is used
It is used in personal artificial intelligence assistants, in-house document management systems and all artificial intelligence applications that require long-term memory.

## Commonly confused with
It is often confused with vector databases; However, context database represents a broader management and access strategy of this data.

## Frequently asked questions
**Why is this needed?**
It is necessary for artificial intelligence to recognize you and not forget your previous conversations.

**Is my data safe?**
The context database structure is protected by local or cloud-based security protocols, depending on where and how the data is stored.
