# What is Optical Character Recognition?

> OCR

Technology that converts visual texts into digital text that can be edited by a computer.

## Overview
This technology recognizes text on scanned documents or photos and converts them into editable text files. It acts as a bridge that converts the text on a piece of paper that you see with your eyes into digital characters that the computer can understand. Nowadays, it is frequently used in invoice scanning or digitizing old documents.

*Analogy: It is like magic glasses that can take a photo of a book and copy and paste the text in that photo into a Word file.*

## How it works
The system first scans the image and analyzes the letter shapes. It then matches these shapes with characters in its database. In the final stage, it creates meaningful texts by combining these characters.

## Where it is used
It is used to make scanned PDF documents editable, automatically read ID card information, and digitize handwritten notes.

## Commonly confused with
Unlike browsers that just save the image as is, it converts the content into text.

## Frequently asked questions
**Can he read handwriting?**
Yes, advanced systems can now digitize handwriting with high accuracy.

**Does it work in all languages?**
Most systems support common languages, but special scripts may require additional training.
