# What is Video Editing Software?

*Dictionary · Dev · Last updated: September 27, 2026*

Digital editing programs used to cut and trim footage, add effects, and edit audio.

## Overview

Video editing software refers to desktop or cloud-based programs that allow you to create professional or amateur videos by combining raw camera footage, audio files, and visuals. Operating on a timeline-based logic, they offer a range of creative tools such as cutting, trimming, color grading, and adding transition effects. Today, these software solutions are also equipped with artificial intelligence features.

***Analogy:** These programs are like the digital desk of an editor sitting with film strips, scissors, and glue in their hands.*

## How it works

Raw video files are imported into the program, arranged on the timeline, and unwanted parts are removed. Voiceovers, music, and visual effects are added on top, and it is exported as a new video file.

## Where it is used

They are frequently used in filmmaking, social media content creation, and advertising agencies.

## Commonly confused with

They are different from media players that merely play videos; they are used to modify and create content.

## Frequently asked questions

**How has artificial intelligence impacted this software?**

They can perform tasks such as automatic subtitle generation, background removal, and smart audio cleanup in seconds.

**Which formats do they support?**

In addition to common video formats like MP4, MOV, and AVI, they also support raw camera formats.
