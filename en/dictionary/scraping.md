# What is Scraping?

*Development · Last updated: November 21, 2026*

It is the process of automatically copying and extracting data from websites.

## Overview

It is a method of automatically extracting and saving the information you are looking for by scanning the code behind websites. Instead of copying and pasting manually one by one, it allows you to collect data from thousands of pages in seconds through software. It is often used for tasks such as market analysis, price tracking, or content aggregation.

***Analogy:** It is like a super-fast assistant that quickly takes photos of all the sentences containing the word you are looking for among hundreds of books in a library and gathers them digitally instead of searching for them manually one by one.*

## How it works

Specially written software or scripts send requests to the target website and download the HTML code of the page. Then, the desired texts, images, or tables are extracted from this code according to rules and saved to your computer.

## Where it is used

It is frequently used on price comparison sites, news aggregation platforms, data science projects, and market research reports.

## Commonly confused with

It generally means the same thing as web scraping, which is web browsers crawling data in the background, but here the focus is directly on the action of data extraction.

## Questions and answers

**Is scraping legal?**

In general, collecting publicly available data is legal, but collecting personal data without permission or sending requests so intensely that it crashes the servers of the sites can lead to legal issues.

**Which programming languages are used for it?**

Python is the most commonly preferred language, along with libraries such as BeautifulSoup, Selenium, and Scrapy.
