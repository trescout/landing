# What is Tutoring Models?

*Dictionary · AI · Last updated: July 18, 2026*

They are artificial intelligence systems that explain subjects step by step to students, answer questions and provide personalized educational support.

## Overview

Tutoring Models are trained using pedagogical methods, unlike general artificial intelligence models. It does not just give the answer, it guides the student to understand the subject, gives hints and adjusts the difficulty level according to the student's speed. These systems simulate the one-on-one private lesson experience in a digital environment.

***Analogy:** It is like a digital teacher who works with you patiently, understands where you are struggling and shows you the right path.*

## How it works

Analyzes the student's past mistakes and speed of understanding. Then, he divides the topic into simpler parts and presents it to the student and supports it with different examples until the student fully understands it.

## Where it is used

It is used in education platforms, language learning applications and academic support systems.

## Commonly confused with

Its difference from chatbots that only answer questions and answers is that it follows a teaching-oriented strategy.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can it teach every subject?**

Yes, but he does much better, especially in structured subjects such as logic, languages ​​and basic sciences.
