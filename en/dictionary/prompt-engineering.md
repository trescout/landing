# What is Prompt Engineering?

It is the art of optimizing the commands given to get the best results from artificial intelligence.

## Overview
Prompt engineering is the ability to structure commands to get the most accurate, creative or specific response from artificial intelligence. A good command ensures that the AI ​​understands the context correctly and produces the intended output.

*Analogy: It's like giving instructions to an assistant. Instead of saying 'Write me a report', saying 'You are a marketing expert, write a 300-word, professional and persuasive summary using the following data' gives a much better quality result.*

## How it works
The user assigns a role to the AI, defines the task, explains the context, and specifies the desired format. This structured input keeps the model focused and reduces the margin of error.

## Where it is used
It is used in every field where artificial intelligence is interacted, especially in content production, code writing and data analysis processes.

## Commonly confused with
It might seem like you're just asking questions, but it's a process of strategy and trial and error.
