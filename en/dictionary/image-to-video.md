# What is Image-to-Video?

It is a technology that turns a still image into a moving video using artificial intelligence.

## Overview
Image-to-video is a process that takes a single photo frame and turns it into a video by artificial intelligence predicting how objects in that scene might move. This technology brings static images to life, producing realistic or artistic videos.

*Analogy: It's like a magic wand that makes people in an old family photo start acting as if they're living that moment.*

## How it works
The artificial intelligence model analyzes the structure and texture of the objects in the picture. It then creates a video stream by calculating how those objects will change in the next frames.

## Where it is used
It is used in creative content platforms, video editing tools, and AI-powered design applications.

## Commonly confused with
It can be confused with video editing; This technology does not edit, it produces moving images from scratch.

## Frequently asked questions
**Can any kind of picture be made into a video?**
It depends on how well the AI ​​understands the picture; Clear and distinct objects give better results.

**How long does it take?**
Depending on the complexity of the model, it can range from a few seconds to a few minutes.
