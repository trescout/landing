# What is Memory Layer?

*Dictionary · AI · Last updated: September 27, 2026*

It is a digital memory layer that enables artificial intelligence models to remember past interactions.

## Overview

The memory layer is the component that ensures AI systems do not forget past conversations, preferences, and important information shared with users. Normally, AIs reset their memory with every new conversation, but thanks to this layer, a long-term context is established. Thus, it remembers your specific preferences and generates responses accordingly.

***Analogy:** It is like a hotel receptionist noting down your name and favorite room in a ledger every time you arrive.*

## How it works

It selects important information during a conversation, stores it in an external database, and brings it back to the model when needed.

## Where it is used

It is featured in personalized AI assistants, long-term projects, and chatbots.

## Commonly confused with

It differs from the context window, which is the model's immediate short-term memory; the information here is stored permanently.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is the data in the memory layer kept private?**

Depending on the system architecture and settings, this data is stored on your local device or in a secure cloud.
