# What is Centralized Memory Layer?

It is a common memory area that allows artificial intelligence systems to remember past information and experiences from a single center.

## Overview
It ensures that different artificial intelligence tools are fed from the same information pool, rather than independently of each other. Information learned by one agent can be instantly used by another agent. This increases the overall intelligence and consistency of the system.

*Analogy: It's like all employees in a company writing their notes in one common digital notebook and everyone can access that notebook.*

## How it works
Create a centralized flow of information by connecting all your AI systems to the same database or memory API.

## Where it is used
It is used in multi-agent systems and corporate artificial intelligence infrastructures.

## Commonly confused with
It may be confused with a database, but the structure here includes not only data but also experience and context.

## Frequently asked questions
**Why should it be central?**
To prevent fragmented information and make consistent decisions.

**What data is kept?**
Agents' past decisions, user preferences and new information learned.
