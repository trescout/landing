# What is Omni-channel Desk?

It is a support system that collects all different communication channels such as e-mails, messages and calls from customers on a single screen.

## Overview
In customer service, it is difficult to track the messages coming from each channel separately. This system unifies all channels, allowing the agent to view the customer's history in one place. This way, no matter what channel the customer writes, the representative knows everything.

*Analogy: It is like managing the phone, e-mails and the customer at the door from a single computer screen at a hotel reception.*

## How it works
Different platforms (WhatsApp, email, live chat) connect to this system. Each incoming message is automatically turned into a 'ticket'.

## Where it is used
It is used in e-commerce sites, customer support units of large companies and in the service sector.

## Commonly confused with
Can be mixed with multi-channel; Omni-channel is a structure where all channels talk to each other.

## Frequently asked questions
**What does artificial intelligence do here?**
It can analyze incoming messages and suggest automatic replies or direct them to the relevant unit.

**What is the advantage for the customer?**
He does not have to explain himself from scratch every time to solve his problem.
