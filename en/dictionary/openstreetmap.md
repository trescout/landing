# What is OpenStreetMap?

It is a free and open source map project that anyone in the world can contribute to.

## Overview
Unlike commercial maps like Google Maps, OpenStreetMap (OSM) is created entirely by volunteers. Anyone can add new roads, buildings or landmarks to the map. This data is publicly available.

*Analogy: It's like the Wikipedia of maps; Anyone can add something, fix bugs, and it stays constantly updated thanks to the community.*

## How it works
You can download map data from the internet and use it in your own applications or analysis free of charge.

## Where it is used
It is used in logistics applications, navigation tools and city planning projects.

## Frequently asked questions
**Why should I use OSM?**
Ideal when you want full control over data and want to avoid the fees of commercial mapping services.
