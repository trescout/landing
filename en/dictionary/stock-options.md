# What is Stock Options?

It is a financial incentive mechanism that grants employees the right to purchase company shares in the future at a specified price.

## Overview
Stock options are a method of granting rights used in technology companies and startups to increase employee loyalty. Instead of directly giving shares to employees, they are offered the option to buy shares in the future at a predetermined fixed price. As the company gains value, employees can exercise this right to achieve significant financial gains.

*Analogy: It is similar to buying a discount coupon at a restaurant today for a future dinner at today's prices. Even if the restaurant becomes very famous and multiplies its prices, you still get to have your meal at the cheap price of the first day.*

## How it works
The company signs an agreement with the employee and defines a specific vesting period. As long as the employee remains within the company, the shares gradually become exercisable. When the period expires, the employee can purchase the shares at the exercise price and sell them at market value.

## Where it is used
It is frequently used, especially in early-stage startups, to attract talented software developers to the team when high salaries cannot be offered. It is also preferred in large technology companies to maintain employee loyalty.

## Commonly confused with
It can be confused with direct stock sharing. While direct stock sharing grants shares to the employee immediately, the stock options mechanism only grants the right to purchase them in the future.

## Frequently asked questions
**Do options always make a profit?**
No. If the company's value drops or an IPO does not take place, the options may lose their financial value.

**What is a vesting period?**
It is the minimum time an employee must spend at the company in order to earn the right to all of the promised stock options.
