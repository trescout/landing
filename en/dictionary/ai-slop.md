# What is AI-slop?

*Dictionary · AI · Last updated: July 14, 2026*

It is a pile of content that is quickly produced by artificial intelligence, is of low quality and generally does not add any value.

## Overview

The term AI-slop is used to describe unthought-out, repetitive, or inaccurate AI output that floods the internet. It is often created by sacrificing quality for the sake of clicks or content production speed. Since these contents do not involve human labor, they are generally superficial.

***Analogy:** Instead of a meal carefully prepared by a chef, it is the online equivalent of ready-made foods that are mass-produced in a factory and tasteless.*

## How it works

Texts, images or videos produced only by automatic commands, without being controlled by humans, fall into this category.

## Where it is used

It frequently appears on social media platforms, low-quality news sites, and search engine results.

## Commonly confused with

Not everything produced by AI is slop; only sloppy and worthless ones are called that way.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I know if a piece of content is AI-sloped?**

If the content is very general, contains unreasonable errors, is repetitive, and does not add any new information when you read it, it is most likely a slop.
