# What is Reverse Engineering?

*Dictionary · Dev · Last updated: October 6, 2026*

It is the process of breaking down a finished product, system, or software into its components to figure out its working principles and understand how it works.

## Overview

Reverse engineering is the art of understanding the inner structure of a construct for which you do not have design documents or source code. During this process, the product's response to inputs is observed and all its components are examined one by one. As we frequently emphasize on the TreScout platform, this method is used not to copy existing technologies, but to improve them and make them more secure.

***Analogy:** It is similar to a chef carefully chewing every bite of a mysterious dish eaten at a competing restaurant to figure out the spices and cooking technique inside it.*

## How it works

First, the target system is closely observed and tested. Then, the system is taken apart step by step, either physically or in software. In the final stage, you combine the data you have gathered to create a new blueprint showing the working logic of the system.

## Where it is used

You can frequently resort to it in cybersecurity to figure out the operating logic of malware. Additionally, you can use this method in the modernization of aging systems that lack documentation.

## Commonly confused with

It is confused with the term 'software reverse engineering', which is specific to the software world. Reverse engineering is a much broader umbrella concept that also covers physical machines and hardware.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is it legal to perform reverse engineering?**

It depends on your purpose of use. While it is generally considered legal when done for security analysis or ensuring system compatibility, doing it for copyright infringement can lead to legal issues.

**Is knowing how to code a requirement to perform this process?**

If you are examining a physical machine, mechanical knowledge is sufficient. However, if you want to decipher software in the digital world, you need advanced knowledge of coding and system architecture.
