What is SEO?

Search Engine Optimization

These are technical and content arrangements made to ensure that websites rank higher in search engines.

Overview

SEO allows search engines to better understand your site and match it with the information users are looking for. Site speed, accurate titles, quality content, and links from other sites are part of this process. The goal is to make it easier for people to find your site.

Analogy: It's like sticking labels that will attract the librarian's attention so that your book is placed on the most visible shelf in a library.

How it works

Increase the speed of your site, establish a clean structure that search engines can understand, and produce quality content that people are actually looking for. Use keywords naturally.

Where it is used

It is used in digital marketing, web development and content management.

Commonly confused with

Not to be confused with advertising (SEM); SEO aims to improve completely natural (organic) results.

Frequently asked questions

Will my ranking increase immediately?

No, SEO is a long-term process and it may take time to see results.

Is only the keyword important?

No, content quality and technical performance of the site are now much more important.
