# What is Frontier?

*Artificial Intelligence · Last updated: October 28, 2026*

It is a concept that refers to the most advanced capacity currently available in artificial intelligence technologies, the cutting edge, and the highest-level models discovered so far.

## Overview

Frontier is used to describe the latest limit reached by technical capabilities in the world of artificial intelligence and the most powerful models located at this limit. It encompasses technologies whose boundaries have not yet been fully explored, trained with high processing power, and possessing unprecedented reasoning skills. Technologies at this level form the epicenter of innovations and safety debates in the industry.

***Analogy:** It is similar to the most advanced rocket technology in space exploration where no one has set foot yet, but which allows going beyond the boundaries.*

## How it works

Highest-level models are trained in massive data centers using high-cost hardware investments and new architectures. These efforts push the known boundaries of artificial intelligence one step further.

## Where it is used

It is frequently discussed in technology companies' flagship models, the focus of academic research, and international AI safety summits.

## Commonly confused with

It is confused with standard commercial models. The concept of Frontier defines only the technologies that are at the very forefront of the industry and push the limits.

## Questions and answers

**What does it depend on for a model to be considered 'frontier'?**

It depends on having significantly higher reasoning, generalization, and processing capacity compared to previous models.

**Are these models open for everyone to use?**

They are usually offered through restricted access or API services due to high costs and security concerns.
