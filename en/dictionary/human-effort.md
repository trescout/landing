# What is Human Effort?

*Dictionary · Dev · Last updated: June 13, 2026*

It is the totality of mental and physical effort made by people to complete a project or task.

## Overview

Even in the age of artificial intelligence and automation, the human touch is essential for installing, monitoring and fine-tuning systems. This concept encompasses creative thinking, ethical evaluation, and complex problem-solving processes that machines cannot do. It plays a critical role especially in artificial intelligence training and data labeling.

***Analogy:** It's like a chef cooking; The food processor can chop the ingredients, but the cook determines the taste and presentation of the food.*

## How it works

It occurs in the processes of planning, writing code, debugging, and checking the output of the model. As automation increases, human labor shifts from physical work to strategic decision-making processes.

## Where it is used

It is used in software development processes, artificial intelligence training (such as RLHF) and general project management.

## Frequently asked questions

**Will artificial intelligence completely replace human labor?**

No, it just changes the nature of human labor and allows it to focus on more strategic areas.

**Why are people still needed?**

Machines are still not at human level when it comes to decision making, ethical judgment, and understanding complex contexts.
