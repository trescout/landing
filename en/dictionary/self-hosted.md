# What is Self-hosted?

It is hosting a software on your own computer or dedicated server rather than on a third-party cloud service.

## Overview
The self-hosted approach puts you in complete control of your data. Since you run the software on your own hardware, you are not dependent on the servers of an outside company. This provides complete control, especially regarding privacy and security issues.

*Analogy: It's like building your own house and placing all your belongings there instead of staying in a hotel; You have complete control.*

## How it works
Download the source code or installation file of the software you want. Prepare a server or local computer and install and run the software there. Do updates and maintenance yourself.

## Where it is used
Personal cloud storage is preferred in all projects that require special artificial intelligence models and privacy.

## Commonly confused with
Not to be confused with cloud services; In the cloud, you rent someone else's computer, in self-hosted you use an area under your own control.

## Frequently asked questions
**Does it require technical knowledge?**
Yes, basic system administration knowledge may be required to manage installation and maintenance processes.

**Is it safer?**
It is safer in terms of data privacy, but it is entirely your responsibility to ensure security.
