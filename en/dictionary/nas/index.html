What is NAS?

Network Attached Storage

A device that provides a central storage area that can be shared by all devices in the home or office network.

Overview

A NAS is a dedicated storage box that connects to your network. Even if your own computer's memory is full, you can transfer your files to this device and access them over the network at any time.

Analogy: It's like a library that you put on a common shelf in your home and where everyone can go and get books when they need them.

How it works

You connect the device to your modem and other devices on the network (phone, computer, TV) treat it like a hard drive.

Where it is used

It is used as a media server at home or for common file sharing in offices.

Commonly confused with

It is not just a hard disk connected to the internet, it is a smart storage unit with its own operating system.

Frequently asked questions

Are NAS and cloud storage the same?

The logic is the same, but NAS allows you to keep your data in your own home or office.

Is internet required for NAS?

If you are only working on the local network, there is no need for the internet, you can access it through your own network.
