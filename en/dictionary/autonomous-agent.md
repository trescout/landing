# What is Autonomous Agent?

It is software that sets goals on its own, without human intervention, and takes the necessary steps to achieve these goals.

## Overview
Autonomous agents are not just vehicles that wait for commands, but systems that make their own plans to complete a task. When they encounter a problem, they can try different methods and manage the process until they reach their goals. Thanks to their ability to make their own decisions, they can carry out complex tasks end-to-end.

*Analogy: Instead of a robot that you just say 'bring me coffee', it's like a personal assistant that goes into the kitchen, finds the coffee, starts the machine and cleans the cup.*

## How it works
AI models create a task list and perform each step in order, updating its strategy based on feedback.

## Where it is used
It is used in automation systems, data analysis processes and complex workflows.

## Commonly confused with
It can be confused with ordinary chatbots; bots just respond, while autonomous agents do the work.

## Frequently asked questions
**Can autonomous agents make mistakes?**
Yes, they may follow a wrong strategy as they decide on their own, so it is important to keep them under control.

**What models are used for autonomous agents?**
Generally based on LLMs with advanced reasoning ability.
