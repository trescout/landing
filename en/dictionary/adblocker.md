# What is Adblocker?

It is a software plug-in that prevents ads from appearing in your internet browser.

## Overview
Adblocker is a tool that automatically detects and hides ads that appear when you visit websites. These tools stop ads from loading, making pages open faster and preventing you from getting distracted. They are usually installed as browser add-ons.

*Analogy: Imagine reading a magazine and cutting out all the ads on the page with a pair of scissors; adblocker does exactly this for you in the digital world.*

## How it works
When you download and install an adblocker plugin from your browser's store, this tool starts working in the background. While websites are loading, the plugin blocks connections to ad servers. This way, ads never reach your screen.

## Where it is used
It is used as an add-on in internet browsers such as Chrome, Firefox or Safari.

## Commonly confused with
It can be confused with a firewall, but while it controls all internet traffic, adblocker only targets advertising content.

## Frequently asked questions
**Does using adblocker harm websites?**
Many websites generate their income through advertisements; That's why some sites may notice that you are using adblocker and ask you to turn off ads.

**Does it block every ad?**
It blocks most ads, but some sites may be more difficult to block because they combine ads with content.
