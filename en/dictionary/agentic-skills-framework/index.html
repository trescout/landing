What is Agentic Skills Framework?

It is a set of rules used to improve the ability of artificial intelligence to act independently and use vehicles.

Overview

It is a guide that determines not only how an artificial intelligence speaks, but also what steps it will take to complete a job. This framework increases the capacity of AI to make autonomous decisions. It teaches you how to recover when you make a mistake and which tools to use and when.

Analogy: It's like a handbook that tells an intern not only what to do, but what rules to follow when handling things on his own.

How it works

Define the tools that AI can use, determine its decision-making criteria, and govern what it should do in case of an error.

Where it is used

It is used in autonomous artificial intelligence systems and complex workflows.

Commonly confused with

It may be confused with the concept of AI Agent, but this concept is the management style of the abilities that the agent has.

Frequently asked questions

Why is a framework needed?

To ensure that artificial intelligence acts with a certain discipline, not randomly.

Is it different for each agent?

Yes, the skill frame is customized according to the agent's role.
