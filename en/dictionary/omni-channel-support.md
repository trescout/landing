# What is Omni-channel Support?

*Dictionary · Dev · Last updated: June 16, 2026*

It is the support structure that allows customers to reach a brand in an uninterrupted and connected manner through all channels.

## Overview

This approach aims to ensure that the customer's experience is not disrupted when switching between different channels such as social media, email or store. Since all channels are interconnected, a support request started in one channel can continue where it left off in the other channel. For the customer, the brand acts as a single whole.

***Analogy:** It's like talking to a friend on the phone and texting at the same time, and you both know where the topic is; Whichever channel you choose, the story continues.*

## How it works

Companies combine all communication channels into one central system. Thus, the customer representative can instantly see what the customer has previously written on which channel.

## Where it is used

It is used on e-commerce sites, banking applications and large customer service centers.

## Commonly confused with

Mixed with multi-channel support; In multi-channel support, channels are independent from each other, while in omni-channel they are all interconnected.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why is omni-channel important?**

It prevents the customer from explaining the same problem over and over again and increases trust.

**What does it take to set this up?**

A strong central database and integrated software infrastructure that feeds all channels is required.
