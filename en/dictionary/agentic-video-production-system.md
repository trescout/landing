# What is Agentic Video Production System?

They are intelligent systems that edit and edit videos without human intervention.

## Overview
Agentic video production system is an autonomous structure that takes a subject or scenario, selects the necessary images, applies effects and edits the video. These systems make the creative decisions that a video editor makes through artificial intelligence agents. It enables rapid content production by automating the video production process end-to-end.

*Analogy: Imagine that in a movie studio, the entire team (editor, soundman, effects man) is locked into a single software and works automatically.*

## How it works
You enter an instruction or scenario into the system. The system pulls appropriate images from the database, edits them, adds voice-over and presents you with the final video.

## Where it is used
It is used in news agencies, social media content production and advertising agencies.

## Commonly confused with
Not to be confused with simple video editing tools; These systems also make creative decisions themselves.

## Frequently asked questions
**Is human intervention completely eliminated?**
Although systems are much improved, a human's approval is often needed to determine the creative vision.

**What type of videos is it suitable for?**
It is very suitable for news summaries, educational content and social media clips.
