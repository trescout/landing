# What is Diagrams?

They are visually simplified diagrams of complex systems, processes or structures.

## Overview
Diagrams are visual maps that allow us to understand many complex structures, from software architecture to business processes. Instead of getting lost between lines of code, it helps you see at a glance how parts connect together and how data flows. A good diagram can be more descriptive than thousands of pages of documents.

*Analogy: It is like the illustrated installation guide that comes out of the box when assembling a piece of furniture; It shows you step by step which part to install where.*

## How it works
Using boxes, arrows, and lines, you draw the components of the system and the interactions between them. Nowadays, there are also tools that create this automatically by writing code.

## Where it is used
It is used during system design, team meetings and technical documentation.

## Commonly confused with
It is not just a picture, it is a model that represents the logical functioning of the system.

## Frequently asked questions
**Can anyone draw a diagram?**
Yes, the important thing is not that it is artistic, but that it accurately reflects the functioning of the system.
