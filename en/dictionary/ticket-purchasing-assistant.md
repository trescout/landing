# What is Ticket Purchasing Assistant?

A digital assistant that searches for tickets and makes purchases on behalf of the user.

## Overview
This assistant scans online ticket sites for you, compares prices and completes the purchase according to the criteria you choose. It takes care of dozens of manual clicks with a single command.

*Analogy: It is like a personal assistant that searches for concert or flight tickets for you and completes the transaction using your credit card information securely.*

## How it works
It uses custom integrations that provide web browsing capabilities and access to payment systems. It offers you the appropriate options, obtains your approval and carries out the transaction.

## Where it is used
It is available in travel apps, event platforms or personal digital assistants.

## Commonly confused with
It may be confused with a simple search engine, but this assistant has the ability to 'complete' the process.

## Frequently asked questions
**Is it safe?**
Yes, it usually works over secure payment gateways and encrypted connections.

**Which tickets can he buy?**
As long as integration is achieved, you can buy plane, train, concert or movie tickets.
