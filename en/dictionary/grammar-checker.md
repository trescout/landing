# What is Grammar Checker?

They are software that automatically detects and corrects grammar, spelling and punctuation errors in written texts.

## Overview
Grammar checker tools analyze your text and check its compliance with spelling rules. It usually flags errors in sentence structure using artificial intelligence or rule-based algorithms. Not only can he find errors, but he can also make suggestions for smoother narration.

*Analogy: It's like a teacher checking your homework with a red pen in his hand and correcting your mistakes.*

## How it works
You paste your text into the relevant tool or use it as a browser add-on. The system compares the text with the language rules in its database. It highlights the incorrect parts and offers the correct option when you click on it.

## Where it is used
It is frequently used in emails, academic articles, blog posts and daily messaging.

## Commonly confused with
Can be confused with AI-based copywriters; However, these tools focus on improving existing content rather than producing content.

## Frequently asked questions
**Does Grammar Checker always give accurate results?**
No, sometimes he may misunderstand the context and make incorrect corrections, so you should always do the final check.

**Does it only work in English?**
Nowadays, many tools support many languages, including Turkish.
