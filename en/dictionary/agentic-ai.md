# What is Agentic AI?

It means that artificial intelligence takes the initiative to achieve goals instead of just producing text.

## Overview
Artificial intelligence ceases to be a passive tool and takes on a structure that acts proactively in line with its goals. The system's ability to recognize and correct its own errors is central to this concept.

*Analogy: It's like a car being an autonomous vehicle that knows the route and chooses the best path based on traffic, instead of just waiting for you to drive.*

## How it works
The system coordinates different tools with each other to solve a complex problem. If a step fails, it tries to achieve its goal by trying an alternative path.

## Where it is used
It is used in software that automates in-company workflows and complex software development processes.

## Commonly confused with
It is confused with artificial intelligence; This is essentially an evolution in the way AI is implemented.

## Frequently asked questions
**What is the difference between Agentic AI and AI Agent?**
AI Agent refers to a single unit, and Agentic AI refers to the general name given to all of these capabilities.

**How does this technology change the business world?**
It allows us to completely transfer repetitive and planning-requiring tasks to artificial intelligence.
