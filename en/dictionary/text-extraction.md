# What is Text Extraction?

It is the process of converting written data in digital documents or images into text format that the computer can process.

## Overview
This process transfers text on a piece of paper or text in an image file to a digital text document. For computers, an image is just colored pixels; Thanks to this technology, those pixels are transformed into meaningful words.

*Analogy: It's like a digital typist that reads the handwriting in a photo and converts it into a computer note.*

## How it works
The document enters the system, AI or character recognition algorithms match pixels to letters and give you editable text.

## Where it is used
It is used in invoice processing, authentication systems and digitization of legacy archives.

## Commonly confused with
It is almost the same as OCR; While text extraction is the general name for this process, OCR is the name of the technology used.

## Frequently asked questions
**Can he read handwriting?**
With modern models, yes, but the readability of the text directly affects the quality of the result.
