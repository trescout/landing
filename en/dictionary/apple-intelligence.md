# What is Apple Intelligence?

It is a privacy-focused personal artificial intelligence system that works integrated into devices within the Apple ecosystem.

## Overview
Apple Intelligence is an AI architecture directly integrated into the operating systems of iPhone, iPad, and Mac devices. By understanding the user's personal context, it performs tasks such as text editing, notification summarization, and image generation. It aims to protect user data privacy by completing a significant portion of processing on-device.

*Analogy: It is like a personal secretary that lives inside your device, knows your daily habits and notes very well, but never leaks your secrets.*

## How it works
It runs lightweight models locally using the neural processing engine in the device's own hardware. For more complex queries, it resorts to the Private Cloud Compute infrastructure, processing data encrypted and without persistent logging. It directly communicates with applications throughout the system to instantly transform text and media content.

## Where it is used
It is used when writing emails, reviewing notification summaries, removing unwanted objects from photos, and executing complex actions with voice commands.

## Commonly confused with
It can be confused with traditional cloud-based chatbots; however, this system is deeply integrated into the device's operating system, and its primary focus is the local privacy of personal data.

## Frequently asked questions
**Does Apple Intelligence work without internet?**
Yes: Basic text and visual features running on on-device models can also be used without an internet connection.

**Are my personal data stored on servers?**
No: Requests sent to the cloud are processed instantly in a private server architecture and are not permanently saved.
