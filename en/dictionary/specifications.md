# What is Specifications?

It is a set of detailed rules that specify what a product or software should do and how it will work.

## Overview
Specifications, or 'specs' for short, are the roadmap of a project. It creates a common language between designers, developers and managers. What features a software will have, how fast it will run, and what errors it will find acceptable are written here.

*Analogy: It is like the detailed plan and materials list drawn by the architect before building a house; It determines what goes where and how it looks.*

## How it works
It is usually prepared in the form of technical documents, diagrams or user stories. It is approved before the development process begins.

## Where it is used
It is used in software development projects, product design processes and engineering studies.

## Commonly confused with
It's not just a list of ideas or wishes; Contains applicable technical details.

## Frequently asked questions
**Why should we write specifications?**
It is necessary to ensure that everyone is working on the same goal and to prevent going beyond the scope of the project.
