# What is Agentic Systems?

It is a set of systems in which more than one artificial intelligence unit collaborates to solve complex problems.

## Overview
Agentic Systems go beyond the limits of a single artificial intelligence and are formed by the combination of multiple artificial intelligence units (agents) specialized in different tasks. These systems distribute tasks among themselves to achieve a goal and proceed by checking each other's outputs. They can manage complex processes without human intervention.

*Analogy: Instead of a single employee rushing to do every job, it is like a team of experts in their own field supporting each other to complete a project.*

## How it works
The system divides the main target into small pieces and assigns each piece to the agent with the relevant ability. Agents achieve the goal by exchanging data and completing each other's shortcomings. In this process, errors are minimized with a continuous feedback loop.

## Where it is used
It is used in software development processes, data analysis projects and automation of complex workflows.

## Commonly confused with
It differs from a single artificial intelligence model by the coordinated work of multiple units and self-decision-making mechanisms.

## Frequently asked questions
**Why are Agentic Systems important?**
Because while a single artificial intelligence has the possibility of making mistakes, specialized agents working together produces more reliable and comprehensive solutions.

**Is it difficult to install these systems?**
Yes, ensuring that agents work in harmony with each other requires serious engineering and planning.
