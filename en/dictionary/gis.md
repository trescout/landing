# What is GIS?

> Geographic Information System

A digital system that collects, analyzes and visualizes geographical data on the world on a map.

## Overview
Geographic Information Systems is a technological infrastructure used to process location-based data. Not just drawing maps; It allows you to understand the patterns, relationships and trends behind this data. It plays a decision-making role in many fields, from urban planning to natural disaster management.

*Analogy: GIS allows you to think of the world as a layered transparent file; One layer has roads, another has buildings, another has trees, and you put them all on top of each other to see the big picture.*

## How it works
First, data is collected via satellite, GPS or field measurements. This data is transferred to the computer and organized in layers. Then, thanks to the software, you can query this data or predict future changes.

## Where it is used
City planning, logistics routing, environmental protection projects and disaster management.

## Commonly confused with
Not to be confused with just a navigation tool like Google Maps; it only shows the result, GIS is the kitchen of data.

## Frequently asked questions
**Is GIS just a map?**
No, the map is just the output. GIS is the entire process of collecting, storing and analyzing data.
