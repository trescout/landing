# What is Red-team Work?

*Development · Last updated: November 21, 2026*

It is the process of adversary simulation and penetration testing conducted to find vulnerabilities in systems.

## Overview

Red-team work is a secure attack simulation carried out to push the limits of an artificial intelligence model or software infrastructure and discover potential flaws. By mimicking the tactics of real-world hackers or malicious actors, it measures how resilient the system is against manipulation. As TreScout, we consider such tests a critical phase for you to detect security vulnerabilities early in your projects.

***Analogy:** It is like hiring professional thieves to test the sturdiness of a newly built bank building and asking them to break in secretly.*

## How it works

Security experts give unauthorized commands to the system, attempt prompt manipulations, and check whether the model leaks confidential information. Based on the results, weak points of the system are patched and firewalls are strengthened.

## Where it is used

It is heavily used just before artificial intelligence models are deployed to production, in critical infrastructure security, and in penetration testing.

## Commonly confused with

Unlike software that automatically scans for security vulnerabilities, it is a proactive process that entirely requires human intelligence, strategy, and creativity.

## Questions and answers

**Why is red-team work necessary?**

It is essential to preemptively detect hidden vulnerabilities and malicious use cases in systems that cannot be discovered by normal users.

**Is this process only done for artificial intelligence?**

No; it is widely applied to enhance security in both traditional software systems and modern artificial intelligence infrastructures.
