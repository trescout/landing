# What is Foundation Model?

They are extensively trained mainstream AI models that form the basis for many different applications.

## Overview
Foundation Models are comprehensive and multi-purpose trained basic artificial intelligence models on which other applications are built. These models are trained once and then used by customizing them according to different needs.

*Analogy: It's like laying the foundation of a house; You can build a villa, office or skyscraper on that foundation.*

## How it works
They undergo general training on huge datasets. In this process, they learn the basic rules of language, logic or visuals.

## Where it is used
A foundation model underlies most modern artificial intelligence applications. For example, models such as the GPT series or Llama are foundation models.

## Commonly confused with
They are mistaken for just a chatbot; Yet they are the basis for many things, from writing code to image processing.

## Frequently asked questions
**Is the Foundation model alone sufficient?**
It is sufficient for general work, but additional improvements are often made for professional needs.

**Can anyone make their own foundation model?**
It is usually made by large technology companies because it requires a huge amount of computing power and data.
