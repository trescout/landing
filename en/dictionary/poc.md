# What is PoC?

*Dictionary · Dev · Last updated: September 22, 2026*

> Proof of Concept

A PoC (Proof of Concept) is a small trial that demonstrates whether an idea works.

## Definition and Word Origin

"Concept" means concept, and "proof" means proof. Before a major investment, it is demonstrated that the core technology works. A finished design is not awaited, only the core functionality is tested.

***Analogy:** It is like making a scale model before erecting a building, or trying a dish at home before adding it to the menu.*

## How to Know and Use in Daily Life?

**Software:** Risky integration trial.
**Investment:** Pre-investment validation.
**Hardware:** Pre-prototype circuit test.

## Technical Depth and Architecture

Plan:

**Goal:** Answer to a single question.
**Scope:** The most critical feature.
**Metric:** Numerical success threshold.
**Duration:** Timebox, usually 1-2 weeks.

Example framework:

```
Hedef: Öneri motoru çalışır mı?
Veri: 1000 satır örnek
Başarı: %70 isabet
Süre: 2 hafta
```

If the result is successful, proceed with investment; if not, abandon it. Both are gains: one shows the way, the other teaches a lesson.

## Frequently Mixed Things

Often mistaken for a prototype. A prototype is a product sample, a PoC is a feasibility experiment. One shows, the other proves.

## Use in Different Disciplines

**Mockup:** Scaled model before the building.
**Home trial:** Kitchen test before entering the menu.
**Trailer:** Reaction testing before the movie.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What happens if it succeeds?**

Feasibility is proven, full development begins.

**How long does it take?**

Usually 1-2 weeks. A prolonged effort has turned from a PoC into a project.

**What happens if it fails?**

Losses remain limited, lessons are learned. Early quitting is a gain.

**What is the difference from a prototype?**

A prototype shows, a PoC proves. One is a showroom, the other is an experiment.
