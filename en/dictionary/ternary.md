# What is Ternary?

It is a system that operates not just on two, but on three different states or values.

## Overview
Meaning a triple system, ternary structures in computer science go beyond traditional binary logic. While standard logic only has values of 0 and 1, ternary systems typically include three states: -1, 0, and +1. Recently, it has been frequently preferred in artificial intelligence models to reduce parameter size and save energy.

*Analogy: You can think of it like a three-position switch having open, closed, and standby positions, instead of a standard switch that only has open and closed positions.*

## How it works
Operations are carried out through three different logical states instead of two. In artificial intelligence models, weights are rounded to the numbers -1, 0, and 1, eliminating the need for multiplication operations. As a result, high speed is achieved by performing only addition operations at the hardware level.

## Where it is used
It is used in low-power consuming AI hardware, model compression techniques, and alternative processor architectures.

## Commonly confused with
It should not be confused with the binary system: while binary uses only 0 and 1, ternary works with 3 different values.

## Frequently asked questions
**Why are ternary AI models important?**
They massively reduce memory and energy usage by converting complex multiplication operations into simple addition operations.

**Are ternary systems supported at the hardware level?**
Although traditional processors work with the binary system, ternary architectures can be used through software simulations and next-generation custom chips.
