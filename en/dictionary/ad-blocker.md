# What is Ad Blocker?

It is a software tool that stops advertising content on web pages from loading.

## Overview
An ad blocker is a utility that prevents your browser from communicating with ad servers when opening a website. These tools catch ad codes before they even reach your computer and leave their spaces on the page empty, making the site look cleaner. Additionally, because ads do not load, pages open faster and your data usage decreases.

*Analogy: It is like a very fast assistant that throws away flyers tucked between the pages of a magazine while you are reading it, leaving you with only the main content.*

## How it works
You install it as an extension to your browser, and this extension tracks a list of servers that host ads. When a website is loading and the browser tries to connect to addresses on this list, the extension cuts the connection. Thus, ads are never displayed.

## Where it is used
It is generally used as an extension in web browsers or is found as a built-in feature in some browsers.

## Commonly confused with
Sometimes blocking ads and blocking website content can be confused, but these tools only target ad servers.

## Frequently asked questions
**Does using an ad blocker harm websites?**
Since it blocks the site's advertising revenue, it can lead to a loss of income for content creators.

**Does it block every ad?**
It blocks most ads, but some sites may detect ad blockers and restrict access.
