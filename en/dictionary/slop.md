# What is Slop?

*Artificial Intelligence · Last updated: November 4, 2026*

Unnecessary content generated by artificial intelligence that is of low quality, repetitive, or carries no value.

## Overview

It is a term used to describe identical texts, images, or videos produced cheaply and automatically by artificial intelligence tools on the internet and social media that benefit no one. It refers to the mass production of information pollution by the hand of artificial intelligence.

***Analogy:** It is the digital content equivalent of factory-made, bland, inedible, cheap fast-food that merely fills the stomach.*

## How it works

It emerges when content produced in bulk via automated scripts or bots, without passing through any human filter, is uploaded to platforms. It is generally used for the purpose of generating clicks and advertising revenue.

## Where it is used

It frequently appears in social media feeds, search engine results, and blog sites that produce low-quality content.

## Commonly confused with

It is confused with quality content production supported by artificial intelligence. The main difference here is that the content is completely careless, repetitive, and worthless.

## Questions and answers

**Why is it seen as a problem?**

It lowers the quality of information on the internet, clutters search engines, and prevents people from accessing genuinely useful data.

**How can it be prevented?**

It can be reduced by platforms tightening filters against AI spam content and users focusing on original quality.
