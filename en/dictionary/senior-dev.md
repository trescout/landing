# What is Senior Dev?

*Development · Last updated: October 21, 2026*

An experienced developer who guides software projects through their experience and makes critical decisions.

## Overview

A senior developer is not just someone who writes code; they are an experienced software engineer who designs the architecture of a project, anticipates challenges, and leads the team. Thanks to years of experience, they solve complex problems with minimal cost and maintain code standards. They typically play a key role in the success of the project by bridging business processes and technical details.

***Analogy:** They are like an experienced architect or site manager in a construction project; they don't just lay bricks, they plan the durability and aesthetics of the building from start to finish.*

## How it works

Developers reach this stage over the years by making mistakes in different projects, learning new technologies, and taking on major responsibilities. Beyond writing code, they take on tasks such as code reviewing, mentoring junior developers, and preventing security vulnerabilities.

## Where it is used

They serve in leadership positions in software development teams, technology companies, and large-scale digital transformation projects.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can anyone who writes a lot of code become a senior dev?**

No, seniority is measured not just by time or lines of code, but by competencies such as problem-solving skills, risk management, and team leadership.

**What is the main difference between a junior and a senior?**

While a junior developer focuses on properly executing a given task, a senior developer determines what needs to be done and calculates potential risks in advance.
