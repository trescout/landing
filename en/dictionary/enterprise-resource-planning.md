# What is Enterprise Resource Planning?

> ERP

It is a software system that allows a company to manage all business processes such as accounting, inventory and human resources from a single center.

## Overview
ERP is like the nervous system of a large company. It combines data from different departments into a single database, ensuring everyone sees the same up-to-date information. In this way, you can simultaneously track what is left in stock, how much has been sold or the status of salaries.

*Analogy: It is like a central management panel that manages the ingredients in a restaurant's kitchen, sales at the cash register, and waiters' orders from a single screen.*

## How it works
It offers special modules to all units of the company, and these modules constantly exchange data with each other.

## Where it is used
It is used in corporate companies, production facilities and large-scale businesses.

## Commonly confused with
It can only be confused with accounting software; But ERP covers the entire company.

## Frequently asked questions
**Is it difficult to switch to an ERP system?**
Yes, it takes time and discipline to adapt all business processes to this system.

**What does artificial intelligence do in ERP?**
It can automate processes such as stock management or sales analysis by making forecasts.
