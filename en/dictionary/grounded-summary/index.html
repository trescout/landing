What is Grounded Summary?

It is a summary method that does not involve fabrication and is created by artificial intelligence by adhering only to the source documents provided.

Overview

Grounded summary is when artificial intelligence summarizes by focusing on the information on the documents provided by the user, instead of its own general knowledge. This method allows the model to produce reliable results by disabling imagination or misinformation (hallucination). It is especially used to increase accuracy in legal or technical texts.

Analogy: It is like a student answering an exam by just looking at the book in front of him; It simply conveys what is written in the source, without adding any outside information.

How it works

First, the texts to be summarized are loaded into the system and the artificial intelligence is instructed not to go beyond these texts. The system creates a coherent summary by identifying key points in the text and matching them to the source.

Where it is used

It is used in summarizing case files in law firms, in analyzing long reports in corporate companies, and in academic research.

Commonly confused with

May be confused with hallucination; Grounded summary is actually a technique used to prevent hallucinations.

Frequently asked questions

Why is Grounded summary important?

It prevents artificial intelligence from giving false information and ensures that it adheres to the given source.

Does it always give accurate results?

If the source text is inaccurate, the summary will be inaccurate as well, but it minimizes the possibility of the model fabricating information on its own.
