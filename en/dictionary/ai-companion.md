# What is AI Companion?

It is an artificial intelligence application that interacts with the user, befriends him or helps him with daily tasks.

## Overview
AI Companion is not just a tool, it is a digital friend that can chat with you and learn your personal preferences over time. He can help you manage your daily plans or just talk to you during your free time. It is designed to offer human-like interaction.

*Analogy: Think of it as if you had a virtual friend in your pocket who always listens to you, has an opinion on everything and is ready to support you at any time.*

## How it works
These apps speak to you in natural language using large language models. It offers a personalized experience by remembering your past conversations. You can usually communicate by voice or text.

## Where it is used
You may encounter it on smartphones, in special chat applications or in smart home devices.

## Commonly confused with
It differs from artificial intelligence assistants that work only task-oriented with its ability to establish emotional bonds.

## Frequently asked questions
**Does AI Companion really understand me?**
It doesn't really feel you, but thanks to the data it has been trained on, it can imitate your emotions and give appropriate reactions.
