What is OpenExecutive?

An open source artificial intelligence management system designed to automate corporate management processes.

Overview

It is an artificial intelligence structure that manages internal decision-making, reporting and operational processes. It aims to ease the routine workload on human managers and accelerate data-based decisions.

Analogy: He is like a digital assistant general manager who provides instant data to all departments of the company and supervises operations.

How it works

It connects to company data, analyzes processes and makes automatic decisions within the framework of established rules.

Where it is used

It is used in enterprise software management and operational efficiency projects.

Commonly confused with

It is not just a chatbot; It is a management layer integrated into all business processes.

Frequently asked questions

Will human managers replace them?

No, it just allows managers to focus on more strategic decisions by doing routine work.
