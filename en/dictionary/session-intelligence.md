# What is Session Intelligence?

*Dictionary · AI · Last updated: June 14, 2026*

A system that understands users' behavior by analyzing their interactions and aims to provide a better experience.

## Overview

It searches for an answer to the question 'what does this person want to do' by tracking each user's click, waiting time or path within the application. In this way, the system can anticipate the user's needs.

***Analogy:** It is like an expert sales representative who greets you when you enter a store and recommends special products for you because he knows your tastes.*

## How it works

The data generated throughout the user session is processed by artificial intelligence and the user's current intention is predicted.

## Where it is used

It is used in e-commerce sites or personalized artificial intelligence assistants.

## Commonly confused with

It is not just a simple user tracking, it is a process of making sense of the data.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does this violate privacy?**

It often works with anonymous data, focusing on behavioral patterns rather than personal identity.

**What is its purpose?**

It is to enable the user to achieve what he wants with less effort in the application.
