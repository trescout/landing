# What is Streaming Applications?

*Dictionary · Dev · Last updated: June 17, 2026*

These are applications that offer instant viewing or listening by pulling large files piece by piece over the internet, without having to download them completely.

## Overview

These applications continuously send data in small packets like a river flow. As you watch, the next tracks continue to load in the background.

***Analogy:** Instead of waiting for all the pages to be printed to read a book, it is like having the pages of the book mailed to you one by one every minute and new ones arriving as you read.*

## How it works

The application establishes a permanent connection with the server. The data stream reaches your device without interruption, and the player instantly processes this data and displays it on the screen.

## Where it is used

It is used on Netflix, Spotify or live broadcast platforms.

## Commonly confused with

It is confused with download; In downloading, you cannot watch without buying the entire file, while in streaming, you start immediately.

## Frequently asked questions

**What happens if my internet speed is slow?**

The application reduces the image quality or makes short pauses to prevent the flow from stopping.
