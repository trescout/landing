# What is Financial Services?

*Dictionary · Dev · Last updated: September 27, 2026*

All professional services related to money management, such as banking, investment, and insurance.

## Overview

Financial services is a broad industry that enables individuals and companies to save money, borrow, invest, and secure their risks. Banks, insurance companies, and investment funds are the foundational pillars of this structure. Today, a large portion of these services has been digitalized and is operated through software systems.

***Analogy:** This term is like a massive banking and trade hub that manages the entire money flow and economic heart of a city.*

## How it works

Users and businesses connect to these systems via mobile applications or websites. Software running in the background processes payments, performs risk analysis, and updates account balances.

## Where it is used

It appears in mobile banking applications, stock market platforms, and insurance portals.

## Commonly confused with

It is not directly a software tool or program; it refers to the general sectoral area served by financial technologies.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do financial services relate to software?**

Today, almost all of these services are operated by software running on secure databases and cloud systems.

**Which institutions does it cover?**

It covers banks, brokerage firms, insurance companies, and payment institutions.
