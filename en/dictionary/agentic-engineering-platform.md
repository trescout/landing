# What is Agentic Engineering Platform?

*Dictionary · AI · Last updated: June 19, 2026*

It is a comprehensive development environment used to create, manage and test autonomous artificial intelligence agents.

## Overview

It is a toolkit offered for those who want to produce self-deciding software. These platforms allow you to manage the agent's memory, what tools it can use, and how it makes decisions through a visual or code-based interface. It allows you to control an agent's lifecycle from start to finish.

***Analogy:** It's like a movie studio; you select the actors (agents), write their scripts (tasks), and monitor their performance (results).*

## How it works

You log into the platform, determine the agent's target, assign abilities to it, and monitor whether the agent successfully completes its mission. You interact with and direct the agent if necessary.

## Where it is used

It is used in large-scale artificial intelligence projects and autonomous system development processes.

## Commonly confused with

It is not just a code editor, it covers the entire agent management process.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need to be an expert to use this platform?**

It depends on the platform, but some AI knowledge is required to grasp the basic logic.

**How free are the agents here?**

They are completely free within the rules and limits you set.
