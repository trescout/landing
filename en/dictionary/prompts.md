# What is Prompts?

These are written commands or questions you give to artificial intelligence to get a result from it.

## Overview
They are the instructions you use when interacting with AI models. These commands can be a simple question or detailed instructions indicating how the model should behave. A good command will allow the AI ​​to respond to you more accurately and in the format you want.

*Analogy: If we compare artificial intelligence to a very knowledgeable librarian, the question you ask or the task you give to it is your command; The more clearly you ask, the better answer you will get.*

## How it works
You start by typing a task into the AI ​​chat box. Depending on your needs, you can strengthen your command by adding clear expressions such as 'summarize this', 'translate in this language' or 'act in this role'.

## Where it is used
ChatGPT is used in all AI chat interfaces such as Claude or Gemini.

## Commonly confused with
It can be confused with Prompt Engineering; While prompt is the command itself, engineering is the process of making this command most efficient.

## Frequently asked questions
**How to write a good command?**
You should write clearly stating what you want, in what format, and any restrictions you have.

**Will my commands train artificial intelligence?**
Usually no, but you can control whether this data will be used for training in the settings of the platform you use.
