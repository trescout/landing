# What is Agentic System?

Autonomous structures that make decisions and take action on their own to achieve goals without human intervention.

## Overview
The agentic system is a system that not only carries out the command given to it, but also plans what steps it needs to take to carry out this command. These structures can change their strategies to overcome the obstacles they encounter and manage the process until the goal is achieved. He uses his own initiative, sort of like a digital employee.

*Analogy: Instead of a robot just waiting for the command 'bring me a coffee', it is like an assistant who goes to the kitchen and realizes there are no coffee beans left, then goes to the market and shops.*

## How it works
You simply define the end goal to the system. The system identifies the subtasks needed to achieve this goal, selects the tools, and executes each step sequentially. It corrects errors by checking its own performance throughout the process.

## Where it is used
It is used in the automation of complex workflows, software development processes and autonomous research projects.

## Commonly confused with
Can be confused with ordinary automation tools; While automation only follows the predefined path, the agentic system draws the path itself.

## Frequently asked questions
**Do agentic systems always make the right decision?**
No, sometimes they may follow wrong strategies, which is why human supervision is still important in critical processes.

**Do these systems learn on their own?**
Yes, most agentic systems are designed to learn from experience and work more efficiently on the next task.
