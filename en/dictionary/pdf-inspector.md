# What is PDF Inspector?

It is a tool that allows you to examine the text, visual and structural data in PDF documents in detail and detect errors.

## Overview
PDF files sometimes contain complex layers and hidden structures. This tool checks how the data inside the file is arranged, what fonts are used, and whether the texts are read correctly.

*Analogy: It is like a magnifying glass that examines how the pages are bound, the quality of the paper, and whether the ink is smeared, rather than just looking at the cover of a book.*

## How it works
You upload the document to the tool, and the tool displays the internal structure of the file like a map. You can easily extract missing or incorrect data from this map.

## Where it is used
It is used in document automation systems, data entry processes and digital archiving projects.

## Commonly confused with
Can be mixed with PDF parser; The parser pulls the data, and the inspector checks the quality and structure of the data.

## Frequently asked questions
**Does it only examine texts?**
No, it can also examine the resolution of images, form fields, and metadata of the file.
