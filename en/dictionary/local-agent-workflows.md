# What is Local Agent Workflows?

They are autonomous task processes that run directly on your own computer, without the need for the Internet.

## Overview
This method ensures that AI-powered tasks are processed on your local hardware rather than being sent to cloud servers. Privacy is at the highest level since your data does not leave your computer. In addition, your work continues uninterrupted even if the internet connection is lost.

*Analogy: It's like having your work done by a private secretary sitting at your own desk, rather than having it done over the phone by an outsourced assistant.*

## How it works
You create job definitions through an infrastructure you install on your computer. Your local processing power performs these tasks step by step and delivers the results to you.

## Where it is used
It is preferred when working with confidential data, in personal artificial intelligence infrastructures and in situations requiring offline work.

## Commonly confused with
It is mixed with cloud-based AI services; This method uses entirely local resources.

## Frequently asked questions
**Is my computer's power sufficient?**
It depends on the complexity of the job, but modern local models now work quite successfully even on mid-level hardware.
