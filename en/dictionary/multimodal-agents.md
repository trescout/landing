# What is Multimodal Agents?

*Artificial Intelligence · Last updated: October 26, 2026*

They are artificial intelligence agents that make decisions and take action by simultaneously processing different data types such as text, image, audio, and video.

## Overview

Multimodal agents are advanced AI systems that understand the world not only through written inputs, but also with data coming from different sensory channels. They can examine a screenshot, listen to voice commands, and blend this data to perform actions on a computer. They offer an interaction close to how humans perceive their environment.

***Analogy:** Instead of an assistant that only reads letters: It is similar to a versatile colleague who listens to you, sees your screen, examines the document, and responds to you.*

## How it works

Vision-language models (VLMs) and audio processing modules are combined and integrated into the agent. The agent merges the different input types it receives into a single semantic space, uses tools, and consequently produces text, audio, or visual outputs.

## Where it is used

They are frequently preferred in desktop automations, screen-reading-supported software, smart robotic systems, and customer service bots.

## Commonly confused with

Their difference from models that only produce multimodal outputs is their ability to analyze input data and execute independent actions with the help of tools.

## Questions and answers

**Can multimodal agents control desktop applications?**

Yes, they can analyze screenshots to locate buttons and operate applications via mouse clicks or keyboard input.

**What is the difference between multimodal agents and multimodal models?**

While models only process data and generate responses, agents make decisions based on these responses and take action in the outside world.
