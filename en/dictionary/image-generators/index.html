What is Image Generators?

These are systems that take written prompts and create images or photographs from scratch.

Overview

Image generators are artificial intelligence tools that analyze text written by users and transfer imagined visuals onto a digital canvas. They capture details within the text and generate a new visual based on rules of color, light, and composition.

Analogy: It is like telling a painter, 'Draw me a cat drinking coffee in space,' and the painter instantly picks up the brush and creates that picture.

How it works

The user types what they want to see into the text box. The model builds a new image pixel by pixel, combining knowledge learned from millions of images and their associated tags.

Where it is used

They are widely used in art works, the advertising industry, game design, and social media content creation.

Commonly confused with

They differ from photo editing programs because they do not improve an existing photo, they create one entirely out of nothing.

Frequently asked questions

Do these tools draw every text we want flawlessly?

They are usually amazing, but they can sometimes make strange mistakes when drawing hands, texts, and certain complex details.
