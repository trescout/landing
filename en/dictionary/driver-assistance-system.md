# What is Driver Assistance System?

*Dictionary · AI · Last updated: June 27, 2026*

> Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS)

They are smart assistive technologies that warn the driver or automatically control the vehicle to increase safety while driving.

## Overview

These systems monitor the vehicle's surroundings with cameras and sensors and detect dangers in advance. It supports the driver with features such as lane tracking, automatic braking or distance protection. Unlike fully autonomous driving, it does not take full control, but tries to prevent the driver from making mistakes.

***Analogy:** It is like an experienced co-driver who sits next to you while driving and constantly watches the road, warning you in case of danger.*

## How it works

Sensors on the vehicle constantly collect data, artificial intelligence processes this data and, if necessary, warns you with a sound or slows down the vehicle.

## Where it is used

It is found in modern automobiles and vehicles developing autonomous driving technology.

## Commonly confused with

It may be confused with autonomous vehicles, but these systems focus on supporting driver control.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can these systems take the vehicle without me driving it entirely?**

No, these are for support purposes only and the driver must be careful at all times.
