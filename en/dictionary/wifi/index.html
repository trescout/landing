What is WiFi?

Wireless Fidelity

It is the technology that allows devices to connect to the internet via radio waves without the need for cables.

Overview

You send and receive information over the air via radio signals. A router takes the internet and turns it into an invisible network. Your devices connect to this network and communicate with the world.

Analogy: It's like a radio station broadcasting music and you listening to this sound wirelessly with your radio.

How it works

Plug in your modem, activate the signal from your internet provider, select the network name from your device's settings and enter your password.

Where it is used

It is used for internet access in homes, workplaces and public areas.

Commonly confused with

It may be confused with the internet itself, but WiFi is just a transport method that delivers the internet to you.

Frequently asked questions

Why does WiFi slow down?

Walls, distance, or connecting too many devices at the same time weaken the signal.

Is WiFi safe?

It's pretty secure if you use strong encryption (like WPA3).
