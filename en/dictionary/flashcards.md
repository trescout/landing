# What is Flashcards?

They are digital cards with questions on the front and answers on the back, which are used to quickly memorize or learn information.

## Overview
In artificial intelligence-supported learning processes, it ensures that the information is permanent by dividing complex information into small pieces and turning them into 'cards'. When combined with the spaced repetition method, it increases the learning speed.

*Analogy: It is the digital and smart version of the small papers you hold in your hands while studying for the exam.*

## How it works
Artificial intelligence automatically generates question and answer cards from your notes and makes you repeat them according to your tendency to forget.

## Where it is used
It is used in educational applications and personal development tools.

## Commonly confused with
It can be confused with a quiz; Quiz is an exam, flashcard is a learning tool.

## Frequently asked questions
**How does artificial intelligence do these?**
It analyzes your texts, identifies the most important points and turns them into question patterns.
