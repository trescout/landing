What is Marketing Skills?

Marketing skills are the skills to deliver the product to the right audience.

Definition and Word Origin

It is more than advertising: Research, content, brand and analysis come together. It is the art of explaining the value of the product correctly and delivering it to the right person. Personalization has deepened with artificial intelligence.

Analogy: It's like arranging a store window and attracting passers-by.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is AI changing marketing?

It analyzes the data quickly and adapts the message to the person. The segment is being automated.

Where to start?

One audience, one message and one channel. No expenditure is made before measurement is established.

How is it measured?

With conversion and acquisition cost. Channel code (UTM) tracking is a must.

How is it different from sales?

Marketing prepares, sales closes. They are both ends of the same funnel.
