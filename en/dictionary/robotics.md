# What is Robotics?

*Artificial Intelligence · Last updated: October 14, 2026*

The field of technology concerned with the design, manufacturing, and performance of tasks in the physical world by robots.

## Overview

It is an engineering branch that enables the software world to emerge from digital screens and meet the physical world. Systems that process data from sensors allow mechanical arms or mobile vehicles to perceive the world.

***Analogy:** It is like the nervous system that enables the human body to move by sending signals from the brain to its muscles.*

## How it works

Electronic circuits, motors, and artificial intelligence algorithms work together to perform physical actions.

## Where it is used

It is used in manufacturing factories, autonomous vehicles, and warehouse automation.

## Commonly confused with

Unlike pure software projects, it involves physical and mechanical hardware.

## Questions and answers

**What is its relationship with artificial intelligence?**

Artificial intelligence is used for robots to understand their environment and make decisions.
