# What is AI Team Operations?

It is the discipline of efficiently managing the workflows of teams developing artificial intelligence projects.

## Overview
AI projects differ from traditional software because they require model training, data cleaning, and continuous testing processes. This discipline determines how the team plans these complex processes, updates models, and collaborates.

*Analogy: It's like running a racing team; It is not enough to just build a fast car (model), you also need to manage the pit stop time and tire change strategy (operation) perfectly.*

## How it works
Teams adapt agile methods to artificial intelligence. The process is managed with version control systems, automatic tests and regular model evaluation meetings.

## Where it is used
It is implemented in artificial intelligence startups and AI departments of large technology companies.

## Commonly confused with
It can be confused with pure software operations (DevOps); AI operations present additional data and model-driven challenges.

## Frequently asked questions
**Why is a special operation needed?**
Because artificial intelligence models may lose performance over time and need constant monitoring.

**What determines success?**
The accuracy of the model is determined by how quickly the team can update it.
