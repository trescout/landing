What is Research Framework?

It is a set of standard methods and tools used to carry out a research process step by step.

Overview

A research framework is the road map you should follow to solve a complex problem or generate new knowledge. It determines what data will be collected, how that data will be analyzed, and what tools will be used. Thanks to this structure, your research will be consistent and repeatable.

Analogy: It's like a recipe book; It tells you step by step which ingredients to use and in what order to cook them.

How it works

First, determine your goal and list what steps are needed to achieve that goal. Then choose software or methods that will support these steps. Make this entire process a template and use the same layout for each new research.

Where it is used

It is used in academic studies, data analysis projects and new technology development processes.

Commonly confused with

It is not just a project management tool; rather, it is the methodology of a scientific or technical discovery process.

Frequently asked questions

Why do I need a frame?

By standardizing the process, it reduces your margin of error and makes your results more reliable.

Does the same framework fit every project?

No, the nature of each project is different; so you need to customize your framework according to the goals of the project.
