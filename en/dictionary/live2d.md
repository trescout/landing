# What is Live2D?

It is a technology that brings life to characters by moving two-dimensional drawings as if they were three-dimensional.

## Overview
Unlike traditional 2D animation, Live2D separates drawings into layers and stretches and rotates them. This way, characters can turn their heads, blink or speak as if they were a three-dimensional model. It makes characters look very natural, especially in virtual broadcasting and games.

*Analogy: It's like pulling the strings of a paper puppet and giving it realistic movements.*

## How it works
The drawings are divided into parts, a motion point is assigned to each part, and animation is created by combining these points.

## Where it is used
It is used in virtual YouTuber (VTuber) broadcasts, mobile games and interactive stories.

## Commonly confused with
Can be mixed with full 3D modeling; Live2D is essentially clever manipulation of 2D drawings.

## Frequently asked questions
**Do I need 3D knowledge to make Live2D?**
No, it requires more 2D drawing skills and layering logic.

**Can it be used with artificial intelligence?**
Yes, artificial intelligence, which follows the user's facial movements, can move the Live2D character instantly.
