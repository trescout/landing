What is AGI?

Artificial General Intelligence

AGI (Artificial General Intelligence) is the targeted intelligence that does every intellectual work at the human level.

Definition and Word Origin

Today's models are narrowly specialized, AGI is versatile: It learns in the field it does not see, changes strategies. The horizon of research is beyond products.

Analogy: Instead of a chess master, he is like the intelligence that both cooks and writes poetry.

How to Know and Use in Daily Life?

Research: Roadmap target.
Policy: Editing discussion.
Education: Curriculum philosophy.

Technical Depth and Architecture

Distinctions:

Narrow: Master of one task.
General: Cross-task transfer.
Criteria: The threshold of expertise in the new field.

Path: Big model, agent loop, and feedback learning merge. The calendar forecast is speculation, the benchmark debate is real.
