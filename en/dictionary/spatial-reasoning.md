# What is Spatial Reasoning?

The ability to mentally grasp the position, size, and movement of objects in space.

## Overview
It is the ability of an artificial intelligence or human to understand where objects stand relative to each other in the three-dimensional world. For example, calculating what can and cannot fit inside a box falls into this skill.

*Analogy: It is like imagining the location of objects in the room with your eyes closed and walking without bumping into them.*

## How it works
The system creates a 'mental map' by analyzing objects' coordinates, volumes and distances from each other.

## Where it is used
It is used in autonomous robots, artificial intelligence models that understand the physical world, and architectural design tools.

## Commonly confused with
It is not just visual recognition, but an understanding that includes depth and logic.

## Frequently asked questions
**How does artificial intelligence learn this?**
It is trained through 3D simulations and depth-sensing cameras.

**Why is it important?**
It is essential for robots to move without making mistakes in the physical world.
