# What is Guidance Computer?

It is the guidance brain that calculates the route of a vehicle or system and enables it to reach the target autonomously.

## Overview
The guidance computer is a system that corrects the vehicle's route by continuously collecting environmental data and comparing it with the target. It doesn't just follow a map; It finds the most efficient route by instantly calculating variables such as wind, obstacle or deviation. It is the decision-making mechanism at the center of autonomous vehicles and robots.

*Analogy: It's like the navigation in your car not only showing the way, but also having a driver who turns the steering wheel for you and adjusts your speed according to traffic.*

## How it works
It receives data from sensors, compares the current situation with the targeted situation and sends commands to engines or guidance systems.

## Where it is used
It is used in unmanned aerial vehicles (drones), autonomous cars and spacecraft.

## Commonly confused with
It is not just a GPS device; actively decides and manages the vehicle.

## Frequently asked questions
**What happens if he makes a mistake?**
Modern systems have security protocols that constantly monitor themselves to minimize the margin of error.
