# What is Pipelines?

A series of interconnected automatic stages in which data is received from a point, processed and transformed into a final product.

## Overview
Pipeline is like a production line in a factory. Raw data enters, goes through various filters, transformation processes and analysis, and finally comes out as processed information or result.

*Analogy: Consider a water pipeline; Water is taken from a source, purified, minerals added, and eventually flows from your tap in a drinkable form. Each stage takes the quality of water one step further.*

## How it works
You identify the data source, then sequentially connect each processing step (cleanse, transform, analyze) that this data will go through. The system automatically transfers the output of each step to the next.

## Where it is used
It is widely used in data science, software deployment processes, and training artificial intelligence models.

## Commonly confused with
Not to be confused with just a simple list; In the pipeline, each step is dependent on the previous one and the process is continuous.

## Frequently asked questions
**What happens if the pipeline breaks?**
Often, because one link in the chain breaks, the entire process stops or produces incorrect results.

**Do every job require a pipeline?**
It is unnecessary for one-off jobs, but it saves time in repetitive jobs.
