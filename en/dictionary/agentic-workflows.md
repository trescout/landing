# What is Agentic Workflows?

*Dictionary · AI · Last updated: June 27, 2026*

Artificial intelligence tools make their own decisions and complete a job end-to-end without human intervention.

## Overview

Ordinary AI models answer only one question. Agentic workflow plans to achieve a goal, uses tools, fixes errors, and works until the job is finished. It's as if you told an assistant 'prepare me this report' and it goes and collects the data, analyzes it and presents it to you.

***Analogy:** Instead of a robot that just brings an ingredient, it's like a chef robot that walks into the kitchen, reads the recipe, prepares the ingredients, and cooks the meal.*

## How it works

Artificial intelligence is given a goal, and it manages the process by dividing it into subtasks.

## Where it is used

It is used in autonomous software development, data analysis and complex business processes.

## Commonly confused with

It can be confused with standard AI usage; Here, artificial intelligence does not just respond, it acts.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is there no need for human intervention?**

Approval is usually expected for critical decisions, but most processes proceed on their own.
