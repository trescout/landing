# What is Live Streams?

It is the simultaneous transmission of an event or image to the audience over the internet.

## Overview
Live broadcasts refer to the processing of data as soon as it is produced and delivering it to users over the internet. This process aims to provide a real-time experience by minimizing latency. It is used in a wide range of areas, from social media platforms to professional publishing systems.

*Analogy: It is like a digital version of television broadcast over the internet and watched by everyone at the same time.*

## How it works
Image and audio data are taken from your device, quickly divided into digital packages and instantly sent to viewers via servers.

## Where it is used
It is used in social media live broadcasts, online education platforms and game broadcasting.

## Commonly confused with
The difference from video recording is that the content is not a pre-prepared file, but a stream that takes place at that moment.

## Frequently asked questions
**Why is live broadcast delayed?**
Millisecond delays may occur due to the time it takes to encode the data and transmit it over the internet.
