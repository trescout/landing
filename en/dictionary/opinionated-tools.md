# What is Opinionated Tools?

They are software tools that predetermine and impose the best working method rather than offering the user too many options.

## Overview
These tools work with the philosophy of 'our way is best'. It doesn't give you the chance to customize everything, instead it offers a standard structure. This allows you to focus directly on work instead of dealing with complex settings.

*Analogy: It is like a restaurant that brings you the chef's best dish, instead of offering you thousands of ingredients for your meal.*

## How it works
The software has its own rules and folder structure. As long as you follow these rules, everything works very quickly.

## Where it is used
It is used in modern software development frameworks and rapid production tools.

## Commonly confused with
It may be confused with flexible tools, but while flexible tools can change everything, these tools require a certain discipline.

## Frequently asked questions
**Why are they so restrictive?**
To ensure speed and standardization. They eliminate decision fatigue.
