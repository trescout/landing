# What is SaaS?

> Software as a Service

It is a model of using the software by renting it over the internet instead of installing it on your computer.

## Overview
In the past, in order to use a program, you had to buy a CD and install it. In the SaaS model, you just open your browser and connect to the software over the internet. All maintenance and updates are done by the company that offers the software.

*Analogy: It is like using municipal water provided by the municipality for a monthly fee instead of drilling a water well in your own home; You don't have to deal with infrastructure.*

## How it works
Membership is created and the system is logged in via the internet browser.

## Where it is used
Popular platforms such as Google Workspace, Netflix or Salesforce all follow this model.

## Commonly confused with
Can be confused with PaaS; SaaS is for the end user, PaaS is for developers.

## Frequently asked questions
**Where is my data stored when using SaaS?**
Your data is stored on the secure servers of the company that provides the software.
