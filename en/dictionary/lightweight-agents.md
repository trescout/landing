# What is Lightweight Agents?

*Artificial Intelligence · Last updated: October 26, 2026*

They are lightweight artificial intelligence software designed to operate quickly and efficiently with low system resources.

## Overview

Lightweight agents are AI systems that perform specific tasks without requiring high processing power or large memory space. Compared to large and complex agent architectures, they use smaller language models (SLMs) or optimized code structures. This allows them to operate with low latency on edge devices and serverless architectures.

***Analogy:** It is similar to a small and portable smart screwdriver designed just to tighten a single screw, rather than a giant factory robot.*

## How it works

When developing lightweight agents, developers prefer small-sized models or rule-based microservices. These agents are packaged with minimal dependencies and run in resource-constrained environments.

## Where it is used

They are used in mobile devices, IoT sensors, browser extensions, and automation processes that require instant responses.

## Commonly confused with

Their difference from massive autonomous giant agents with broad reasoning capacity is that they focus quickly on a single task with limited resources.

## Questions and answers

**Can lightweight agents use large language models?**

They generally keep memory and CPU usage to a minimum by using local small language models or lightweight API calls.

**What is the advantage of lightweight agents?**

They offer low latency, are more cost-effective, and can run on any device without requiring high hardware specifications.
