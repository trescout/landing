# What is Hermes Agent?

*Dictionary · AI · Last updated: June 3, 2026*

It is a special artificial intelligence unit that can make autonomous decisions on behalf of the user and complete certain tasks end-to-end.

## Overview

Hermes Agent is a software that can create its own plan in line with the target given to it. It determines the steps, corrects the errors and continues working until the result is achieved, without the need for human intervention. These structures are designed to manage a workflow rather than just answering questions.

***Analogy:** Instead of telling an assistant 'find me a flight ticket', it's like entrusting the entire process to him by saying 'plan my trip and buy the tickets'.*

## How it works

You define a target, Hermes Agent divides this target into small pieces. It checks the situation at each stage and reaches the goal by changing its method if necessary.

## Where it is used

It is used in the automation of business processes, complex data analysis projects and autonomous assistant systems.

## Commonly confused with

Its difference from the general AI Agent concept is that it is usually specialized around a specific architecture or intended use.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can Hermes Agent do anything?**

He can only act within the powers and tools defined for him.

**Is it safe?**

Since it is autonomous, it is important that its powers are limited and constantly monitored.
