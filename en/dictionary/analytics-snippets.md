# What is Analytics Snippets?

They are small pieces of code that websites add to their pages to track visitor behavior.

## Overview
An analytics snippet is a piece of code placed on a website that records where visitors came to the site and how much time they spent on which pages. This data helps site owners improve their sites. It usually runs silently in the background of the page.

*Analogy: It is like an invisible counter placed at the door of a store that notes how many people enter and in which aisles they stop.*

## How it works
The site owner adds this shortcode received from the analysis service to the source files of the website. When the user opens the page, the code runs and sends the visit information to the analytics server.

## Where it is used
It is produced on platforms such as Google Analytics and placed in the HTML codes of websites.

## Commonly confused with
It is often confused with cookies; While cookies keep data in your browser, snippet is the tool that collects data and sends it to the server.

## Frequently asked questions
**Will these codes steal my personal information?**
They often collect anonymous data; however, sites must be transparent about how they use this data.

**Why is it on every site?**
Site owners want to provide a better experience by measuring what users like and dislike.
