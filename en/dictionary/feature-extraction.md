# What is Feature Extraction?

*Dictionary · AI · Last updated: October 13, 2026*

A process that extracts important information from texts and converts it into numerical data that a computer can understand.

## Overview

Feature extraction is the process of extracting the most important features, patterns, and meanings from raw data. Artificial intelligence models cannot process words or images directly in their raw form; therefore, they capture the essence, key concepts, and relationships of the text and convert them into numerical vectors. This process ensures that the model is freed from unnecessary details and finds the main point it needs to focus on.

***Analogy:** It is similar to reading a thick novel, extracting the most crucial events, character traits, and the main idea to prepare a single-page summary card.*

## How it works

The text or visual given as input passes through the layers of the model. The model filters out prominent patterns inside and produces number sequences representing the essence of the data. These obtained outputs are then used in other tasks such as classification or search.

## Where it is used

It is frequently used in natural language processing, computer vision, search engines, and data analytics pipelines.

## Commonly confused with

It is different from directly training a model; it is the step of summarizing the most valuable information from existing data and reducing it into a numerical format.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the difference from embedding?**

Embedding is a general representation format, whereas feature extraction is focused on pulling and extracting specific attributes from the data for that representation.

**Which data types can it be applied to?**

It can be used in all of text, audio, visual, and numerical time-series data.
