# What is Productivity?

It is the capacity to produce higher quality and more work within a certain period of time.

## Overview
Productivity in technology aims to reduce human labor and increase the quality of output by using artificial intelligence or automation tools. It doesn't just mean working faster, it also means working smarter and reducing the margin of error. In the world of TreScout, this is directly related to reducing the workload of AI agents.

*Analogy: It's like using a digger instead of digging by hand; You can dig a much larger area in the same time.*

## How it works
It is increased by automating repetitive tasks, creating quick drafts with the help of AI, and using the right tools.

## Where it is used
It is the main goal in software development, content production, data analysis and general business management processes.

## Commonly confused with
It can be confused with just 'working harder'; whereas productivity means 'working more efficiently'.

## Frequently asked questions
**How does artificial intelligence increase productivity?**
It does boring and repetitive tasks in seconds, allowing people to focus on more creative and strategic tasks.
