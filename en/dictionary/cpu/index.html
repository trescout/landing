What is CPU?

Central Processing Unit

It is the main brain of the computer that performs and manages all operations.

Overview

The processor is the part that processes every command you give to your computer. When you press a key or open an application, the CPU receives that request, calculates it and sends the result to your screen. It is the mathematical intelligence of the computer.

Analogy: He is like the head chef in a kitchen; takes all orders, decides what to do and when, and handles all operations required to cook the meals.

How it works

It works by performing billions of small operations per second. The speed of your software depends largely on how fast the CPU can perform these operations.

Where it is used

It is at the heart of all kinds of digital devices, from smartphones to supercomputers.

Commonly confused with

Mixed with GPU; While the GPU deals with visual and parallel tasks, the CPU deals with general logical tasks.

Frequently asked questions

What does CPU core mean?

They are small independent processing units within a processor that can perform multiple tasks at the same time.
