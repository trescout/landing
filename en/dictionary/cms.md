# What is CMS?

> Content Management System

It is a user-friendly software that allows you to create, edit and publish content on websites without knowing how to code.

## Overview
CMS manages the technical infrastructure of the website in the background. You simply add your texts, images and videos to the site as if you were using a word processor. It allows you to manage a professional site without dealing with design and technical details.

*Analogy: Instead of dealing with the architecture of a building, it is like a ready-made house where you just arrange the items inside and paint the walls.*

## How it works
You log in to an administration panel. There are simple buttons here such as 'add new post' or 'edit page'. Any changes you make are instantly updated on your website.

## Where it is used
It is common on blog sites, e-commerce platforms and corporate websites.

## Commonly confused with
It is similar to website builders; but CMS usually offer more customization and plugin support.

## Frequently asked questions
**Do I need to be a software developer to use CMS?**
No, anyone who can use a computer at a basic level can manage a site with CMS.

**Which is the most popular CMS?**
WordPress is the most widely used CMS example worldwide.
