# What is Self-orchestration?

Artificial intelligence plans complex tasks on its own and manages the necessary steps in order.

## Overview
Artificial intelligence plans complex tasks on its own and manages the necessary steps sequentially. The model breaks a large goal into smaller parts and performs each step sequentially.

*Analogy: Just as a chef manages all the cooks in the kitchen and ensures that the food comes out on time, artificial intelligence manages the flow of tasks.*

## How it works
The model focuses on the goal, identifies missing steps, selects the necessary tools, and plans the next step based on the results of each step.

## Where it is used
It is used in autonomous agents, complex software development processes, and data processing pipelines.

## Commonly confused with
It is confused with simple automation, whereas this method involves dynamics and decision-making ability.

## Frequently asked questions
**What happens if he makes a mistake?**
Well-designed systems recognize the error and change their strategy and try again.

**Is human intervention required?**
Usually you only need to set the target initially, the system handles the rest.
