# What is Communications Operating System?

It is a software platform that allows managing different communication tools and channels from a single center.

## Overview
These systems combine dispersed communication channels such as email, messaging apps, phone and social media into a single interface. It allows the user to follow and respond to all messages from one place, regardless of the channel they come from. It is designed to speed up workflow and reduce complexity.

*Analogy: It is like a central receptionist who receives packages from all the different courier companies in your home from a single door.*

## How it works
It connects to the APIs of different services, converts the incoming data into a standard format and presents it to the user through a single panel.

## Where it is used
It is used in customer service centers, large corporate operations, and personal productivity tools.

## Commonly confused with
Not to be confused with just a messaging app; This is a management platform.

## Frequently asked questions
**Why is it needed?**
Since keeping track of too many applications at the same time reduces efficiency, collecting them all in one center provides great convenience.
