How does TreScout work?

Every day TreScout scans four source feeds, summarizes the highlights with AI and gathers them into a single report. Reports are open to everyone.

How It Works

Your daily tech report, step by step.

The shared daily source selection comes first. When email delivery opens with early access, you will choose when the report arrives; the day’s scan is shared.

01

Scan

Collects daily highlights from GitHub Trending, Hacker News, and Hugging Face models and Daily Papers.

GitHubHacker NewsHuggingFace
02

Summarize

Produces a short, readable report with AI. Technical terms stay in their original form in parentheses and are explained in your selected report language in the glossary.

“…command line interface (CLI) driven interaction…”

03

Deliver

When email delivery is available, the report arrives at the hour you choose. The day’s scan is shared; you decide when to read it.

PDF Report

Full report, effortless reading.

The email provides a quick digest; detailed trends, source links, and the Tech Terms Dictionary appear in a PDF report in your selected language.

  • 📄Daily Highlights grouped by source (GitHub, Hacker News, HuggingFace…)
  • 📚Tech Dictionary · Plain-language definitions for modern AI & software terms
  • 🗂️Archivable · Clean downloadable PDF reports for your permanent archive
  • 🔗Clickable Source Links · Direct links to repos, papers, and model cards
