# Free computer science education path

Offering a free and self-learning oriented curriculum in the field of computer science, this resource creates an educational path that complies with academic standards. It provides a structured learning process covering software development and basic informatics disciplines.

**TreScout note:** A roadmap to rebuild the university curriculum with free resources. It requires discipline: There is no one lecturing you, you measure progress yourself. It is not a substitute for a diploma, but it is a good map to fill your gaps.

## What you get

- Curriculum structured in academic standards
- Course contents from universities around the world
- Self-paced learning and community support

## Getting started

To get started with this curriculum, simply visit the official OSSU CS website or GitHub repository. You can review courses on the website, create your study plan, and interact with other students by joining the Discord community.
