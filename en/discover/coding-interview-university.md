# Software Interview Preparation Guide

Coding-interview-university offers a comprehensive study plan covering the basics of computer science for those who want to become software engineers. This resource combines the theoretical and practical knowledge needed in preparation for technical interviews with a structured curriculum.

- ★ 349,290

**TreScout note:** An interview preparation plan that spans months. Trying to finish the list as it is leads to burnout for most people; It is more realistic to choose the subjects that the target company asks about and focus on those branches.

## What you get

- Structured computer science curriculum for technical interviews
- In-depth theoretical knowledge of data structures and algorithms
- Comprehensive preparation for software engineering interview processes
