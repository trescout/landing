Guide to open source intelligence tools

Legendary_OSINT is a comprehensive list of open source intelligence (OSINT) tools and resources for fraud investigators and cyber threat intelligence (CTI) analysts. It gathers the digital review tools needed in know your customer (KYC) and anti-money laundering (AML) processes in a single center.

What you get

  • Provides methods for person and social media research
  • Allows tracking of aviation, maritime and rail movements
  • Includes resources for cyber threat intelligence and fraud analysis

Getting started

This is not a software tool, but a directory that categorizes open source intelligence tools and resources. By visiting its page on GitHub, you can click on the title of the field you are interested in and start directly examining the listed tools and websites.
