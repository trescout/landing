# Watch world television in one center

The iptv-org project, which gathers public television channels around the world in a single center, presents television broadcast over internet protocol (IPTV) lists as an organized dataset. Developed with TypeScript, this open source collection standardizes users' access to publications in different countries.

- ★ 134,991

**TreScout note:** A list collecting links to open television broadcasts around the world. It is not a program to be installed, it is the addresses and connections you give to your player. Connections die frequently and you are responsible for which broadcast can be watched in which country.

## What you get

- It brings together public television channels from around the world in a single center.
- It standardizes television broadcast listings over internet protocol.
- Provides access via video players that support live broadcasts.
