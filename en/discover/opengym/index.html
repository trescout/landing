Workout tracking software that runs on your own server

OpenGym is an open-source gym management tool used to plan workout routines and track bodyweight exercises. Allowing users to host their data on their own servers, this software supports data imports from popular fitness applications (Strong, Hevy).

What you get

  • You can store your workout data in a secure area you control yourself, instead of third-party company servers.
  • You can easily import your past workout data from popular fitness applications like Strong and Hevy.
  • You can plan and track your workouts from your phone or computer even without an internet connection.
