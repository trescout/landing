# Claude Obsidian

Claude-obsidian is a tool that runs in the Obsidian notes app and provides personal knowledge management (personal knowledge management) with artificial intelligence support. Integrated with Claude Code, it analyzes data, establishes connections, and transforms them into an organized knowledge graph, offering an open-source alternative to platforms like Notion.
