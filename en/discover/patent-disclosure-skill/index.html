Patent Disclosure Skill

Patent Disclosure Skill is a Python tool that automates the processes of identifying invention points in patent applications and preparing technical disclosure texts (patent disclosure). It provides support for interpreting patents clearly, tracking policy changes, and generating responses for examination processes.
