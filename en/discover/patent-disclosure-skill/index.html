Automate patent disclosure and claims drafting with AI

Python-based Patent Disclosure Skill parses technical invention disclosures to draft compliant patent specifications, claims trees, and prior art novelty analyses.

What you get

  • Structured patent specification drafting: Drafts field of invention, technical background, summary, and detailed description sections aligned with patent standards.
  • Independent & dependent claims architecture: Constructs hierarchical claim trees designed to maximize legal protection breadth.
  • Prior art novelty differentiation: Sharply articulates technical improvements and non-obvious inventive steps over baseline technologies.
  • Accelerated attorney collaboration: Translates raw engineering memos into structured disclosure packages ready for patent attorney review.
  • International jurisdiction terminology: Harmonizes technical terminology with WIPO, EPO, and USPTO filing guidelines.

Technical architecture and working principle

Patent Disclosure Skill executes a phased agent workflow mapping technical memos into patent requirements:

  • Invention feature extraction engine: Extracts core inputs, transformations, mechanisms, and novel methodology from technical draft memos.
  • Claim syntax and vagueness validator: Audits claims for clarity, antecedent basis compliance, and formal terminology standards.
  • Modular markdown specification generator: Exports structured patent disclosure documents formatted ready for word processing conversions.

Patent analysis workflows and claims drafting

R&D organizations and patent engineers utilize this workflow to expedite intellectual property capture:

  • Transforming software into patentable disclosures: Derives patent-eligible system and method specifications directly from software architectures.
  • Drafting responses to patent office actions: Assists in drafting technical counterarguments defending novelty against examiner rejections.
  • R&D portfolio patentability audits: Systematically scans technical project deliverables to identify and capture protectable inventive concepts.

Frequently asked questions

  • Does this tool replace a certified patent attorney? No. It is designed as a productivity accelerator that prepares well-structured invention disclosures for patent attorney review and final legal filing.
  • Which large language models are supported? Configurable to use leading frontier models such as Claude 3.5 Sonnet and GPT-4o, or private local models via Ollama and vLLM.
  • Does it protect proprietary trade secrets? When executed alongside a local LLM runner (e.g. Ollama), all processing occurs on your physical machine with zero internet transmission.
  • Can it parse architectural diagrams and flowcharts? When configured with multimodal vision models, it analyzes technical block diagrams and incorporates them into descriptions.
