# Smart home management with artificial intelligence assistants

Developed for Home Assistant, ha-mcp allows you to configure and automate your smart home platform with AI assistants like Claude and ChatGPT. Integrated via HACS, this tool works with full feature support on all Home Assistant installation types.

The tool is installed using HACS or Marketplace on Home Assistant. After installation, Home Assistant is restarted and the necessary service settings are completed from the configuration screen.
