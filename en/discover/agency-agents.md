# Build a digital agency with AI agents

The Agency-agents project creates a digital agency structure by bringing together artificial intelligence agents with different areas of expertise. It allows managing processes such as software development, content production and data verification through specialized characters and workflows.

## What you get
- It optimizes workflows with artificial intelligence agents specialized according to their areas of expertise.
- Provides professional support in processes such as software development, data validation and content production.
- It integrates with popular development tools such as Claude Code, Cursor and GitHub Copilot.
