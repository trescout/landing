# Key-value store for distributed systems

etcd is a reliable key-value store designed to store critical data in distributed systems. Developed in Go, this system focuses on ensuring data consistency within distributed architectures.

## What you get

- securely stores critical data in distributed systems
- achieves tens of thousands of writes per second
- offers automatic TLS encryption and client certificate authentication
