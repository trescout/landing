# Build your personal AI infrastructure

Personal AI Infrastructure offers a framework for creating agent-based artificial intelligence (agent AI) systems designed to augment human capabilities. Developed with TypeScript, this structure supports a modular architecture that allows users to manage their own autonomous workflows.

## What you get
- It allows you to install an operating system that manages your personal goals and life flow.
- Creates autonomous workflows and task management through your digital assistant.
- It divides complex tasks into small and verifiable criteria with the ideal state principle.

```

```
