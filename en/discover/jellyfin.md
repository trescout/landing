# Build Your Own Media Server

Jellyfin is a free media server that allows you to manage and stream your own movie, TV series and music collection to all your devices. An alternative to Plex and Emby, this platform has no premium locks, ads or hidden fees.

- ★ 55,268
- C#
- GPL-2.0
- GitHub Trending · 26 May 2026

## What you get
- All your media in one place and accessible from any device.
- You won't have to deal with subscription fees or ads.
- Your data stays with you and is not shared with third parties.
- An alternative to Plex and Emby that offers complete freedom.

## How to install?
Docker is installed on a server or NAS. Installation guide is at jellyfin.org; After installing it, you introduce your media folders.

## How to install, how to use?
**Run with Docker**

```
docker run -d --name jellyfin -p 8096:8096 jellyfin/jellyfin
```
