# Swarm intelligence engine for data prediction

MiroFish offers a simple and universal swarm intelligence engine developed to predict various types of data. This Python-based tool uses collective computing methods to identify patterns in complex systems.

## What you get
- Creates digital simulation worlds from real world data.
- It enables interaction with artificial intelligence agents with independent personalities.
- Rehearses complex scenarios and possible future outcomes.


**Starting Services**
