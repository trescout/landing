# A collection of prompts for AI models

togg53192-cmd/jailbreaks offers a collection of system prompts, persona locks, and workspace policies designed to bypass the security restrictions of AI models. This work serves as a technical compilation aimed at modifying the operating principles of various open-weight and closed models.

Click on the file for the model you are interested in from the list on GitHub to view its content. Then, copy the entire text and paste it into the system prompts or custom instructions field of the AI tool you are using.
