# Tool for installing applications on iOS devices

Developed with TypeScript, iloader is a user-friendly sideloader that facilitates the process of installing applications on iOS devices. It simplifies the installation of apps outside of Apple's official store, improving the developer and user experience.

## What you get

- Easily installs applications outside the Apple store
- Provides automatic certificate and provisioning profile management
- Offers smart suggestions to assist with troubleshooting

## Getting started

Go to the official website at iloader.app and download the version suitable for your operating system. After installing the necessary drivers such as iTunes (for Windows) on your computer, you can connect your device, run the application, and log in with your Apple ID credentials.
