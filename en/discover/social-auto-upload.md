# Automatically Upload Your Videos to All Platforms

social-auto-upload is a Python tool that automatically uploads your videos to social media platforms such as TikTok, YouTube, Bilibili, Douyin and Xiaohongshu. It simplifies your multi-platform content distribution processes.

## How to install, how to use?
Install the social-auto-upload repository (github.com/dreammis/social-auto-upload) and use its 'sau' command line tool to first log into my account with 'sau douyin login', then auto-upload a video with title and description with the 'sau douyin upload-video' command.
