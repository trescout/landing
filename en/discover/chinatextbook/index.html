Chinese education curriculum and textbooks

The ChinaTextbook repository offers a digital archive of textbooks used in the Chinese education curriculum from primary school to university level in portable document format (PDF). This resource provides a central access point to a wide range of academic materials.

What you get

  • Provides access to a large archive of Chinese textbooks from primary school to university.
  • It facilitates access to free resources by collecting educational materials at a central point.
  • It offers special tool support to combine large files.

Getting started

You can directly view or download textbooks at primary, secondary, high school and university levels in PDF format by visiting the GitHub page of the ChinaTextbook repository. If the file you downloaded is divided into pieces, you can download the merge tool to your computer by going to the '下载文件合并程序' link on the project page and merge it by running it in the same folder with the relevant files.
