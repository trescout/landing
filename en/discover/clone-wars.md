# Open Source Copies of Popular Platforms

Clone-Wars brings together more than 100 open source clones of popular platforms such as Airbnb, Instagram and Netflix under one roof. Developers can examine application architectures by accessing project source codes, technology stacks and live demo links.

- ★ 35,393
- GitHub Trending · 2026-06-15

## What you get

- Opportunity to examine the architecture of popular platforms
- Opportunity to learn different technology stacks
- Practical development experience through open source projects

## Getting started

Clone-Wars is not a software tool, but a guide listing open source clones of popular platforms. To access the details of the project you want to examine, you can visit the official website gourav.io/clone-wars and directly access the source codes and training content of the relevant projects through the links in the table.

- [Official source →](https://gourav.io/clone-wars)
