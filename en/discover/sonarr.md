# Automatically manage your TV series archive

Sonarr is a smart personal video recorder (PVR) developed for newsgroup and bittorrent users. It is an open-source media management tool that allows users to automatically track, download, and organize their TV series content.

## What you get
- Automatically detects and downloads new TV series episodes.
- Automatically upgrades your existing files with higher quality versions.
- Organizes your media files and renames them according to your preferences.
