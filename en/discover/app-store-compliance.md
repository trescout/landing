# Guide to preventing app store rejections

mjmirza/app-store-compliance offers an open guide containing 117 different rejection patterns and their solutions encountered during app store review processes. It aims to simplify approval processes for developers on the App Store and Google Play through tools like regulatory compliance tracking and privacy notices.

## What you get

- You can scan your application before submitting it to Apple and Google for approval to detect potential rejection risks.
- You can directly match encountered rejection clauses with official review guidelines and resolution methods.
- You can verify privacy notices and test account requirements prior to store upload.
