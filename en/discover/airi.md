# Create Your Own Voice AI Character

AIRI is an AI-based virtual character platform that you can host on your own server, capable of real-time voice chat and play games like Minecraft or Factorio. It brings Neuro-sama-style interactive digital assets to desktop and web.

## What you get
- Your own AI character, entirely on your server.
- Real-time voice chat opportunity.
- Ability to play games like Minecraft and Factorio.
- Your data remains entirely with you (self-host).

## Getting started
You can easily install it on your own system by following the documentation (airi.moeru.ai/docs). Web, macOS and Windows platforms are supported.
