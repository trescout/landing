Train a 64M-parameter LLM from scratch in two hours

MiniMind teaches LLM architecture fundamentals from first principles, covering tokenization, pretraining, supervised fine-tuning (SFT), LoRA, and DPO in clean PyTorch.

What you get

  • Train in two hours on consumer GPUs: Train a functional transformer LLM completely from scratch in ~2 hours on a single RTX 3090/4090 GPU.
  • End-to-end LLM lifecycle pipeline: Covers Byte-Pair Encoding tokenization, pretraining, SFT, LoRA adapters, and DPO alignment.
  • Pure PyTorch clarity: Readable, transparent transformer layers without obfuscating third-party framework wrappers.
  • Mixture of Experts (MoE) support: Experiment with dense transformers as well as 8x sparse Mixture of Experts architectures.
  • Exceptional pedagogical resource: The ideal practical sandbox for students and AI engineers looking to demystify generative model training.

Technical architecture and working principle

MiniMind adopts a modern auto-regressive Decoder-Only Transformer architecture modeled after LLaMA and Mistral:

  • RoPE and SwiGLU activations: Incorporates Rotary Position Embeddings (RoPE) and SwiGLU activation functions following frontier standards.
  • Stable RMSNorm normalisation: Uses Root Mean Square Normalization (RMSNorm) for accelerated, stable gradient propagation over LayerNorm.
  • Flash Attention v2 integration: Optimizes memory bandwidth and compute speed via fused Flash Attention GPU kernels.

Training stages: Pretraining, SFT, and DPO

MiniMind's modular scripts walk through the step-by-step evolution of an LLM from raw text to dialogue:

  • Stage 1 - Pretraining (1-pretrain.py): Acquires syntax and general knowledge by predicting the next token over raw web and wiki corpora.
  • Stage 2 - Supervised Fine-Tuning (2-sft.py): Aligns the model to follow user instructions using conversational question-answer datasets.
  • Stage 3 - Direct Preference Optimization (4-dpo.py): Steers model behavior toward preferred conversational styles using paired positive/negative responses.

Frequently asked questions

  • How much VRAM is required to train MiniMind? The 64M model trains comfortably in 6GB to 12GB of VRAM, running smoothly even on budget GPUs like the RTX 3060 or 4060.
  • Does it run on Apple Silicon Macs? Yes. PyTorch MPS (Metal Performance Shaders) support enables training and inference directly on Apple M-series chips.
  • How capable is a 64M-parameter model? At 64M parameters, it excels at demonstrating language fluency, dialogue formatting, and structured generation rather than complex multi-step reasoning.
  • What datasets are bundled or supported? The repo provides automated scripts to download curated open-source pretraining and SFT conversational datasets.
