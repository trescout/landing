Privacy Notice

Last updated: 2026-10-10

This English text is provided for convenience. TreScout operates under Turkish law and the binding document is the Turkish original, prepared under Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK): Aydınlatma Metni. If the two texts differ, the Turkish text applies.

This notice explains how personal data is processed on trescout.com, under the KVKK and the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles for Fulfilling the Obligation to Inform. It is for information only and does not ask for your approval.

1. Data controller

TreScout is operated by Burhan Arıkan, who is the data controller for your personal data. Contact: hello@trescout.com

2. What we process, why, and on what legal basis

Early access sign-up. When you submit the form we process your email address, the time of sign-up, the language of the form and the page you signed up from. The purpose is to send you your early access invitation and the TreScout launch announcement, and to manage the list. The legal basis is the explicit consent you give with the checkbox on the form (KVKK Art. 5(1)). For announcements that qualify as commercial electronic messages, we rely on your consent under Law No. 6563.

Abuse prevention. When you submit the sign-up form, your IP address is used to limit repeated attempts from the same address within a short time. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in keeping the site secure (KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)). This record is deleted automatically after 10 minutes.

Hosting and security logs. When you visit the site, our hosting provider processes your IP address, browser details, the requested page and the time in its server logs, to serve the site and keep it secure. The legal basis is our legitimate interest (KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)). These logs are kept for the provider's retention period.

Optional product measurement. This runs only if you explicitly turn on the "Anonymous product measurement" preference; details are in section 7. The legal basis is your explicit consent (KVKK Art. 5(1)), and you can withdraw it at any time.

Product after launch. If you join the TreScout product after launch, your report preferences and delivery history are processed to provide the service (KVKK Art. 5(2)(c)). You will receive separate information about this when you join.

The sign-up notifications our team receives do not contain your email address. We do not sell your personal data or share it with advertising networks or with third parties for marketing.

3. How we collect your data

Your data is collected electronically, partly by automated means, through the sign-up form on the site and your browser.

4. Who we share your data with

Your data is shared only with the following service providers, for the stated purpose:

  • Vercel Inc. (USA): hosting the site, running the sign-up form and optional product measurement.
  • Resend (USA): keeping the early access list and sending emails.
  • Upstash (USA-based): keeping your IP address for 10 minutes to prevent abuse.
  • Cloudflare Inc. (USA): domain name service and forwarding emails sent to hello@trescout.com.
  • Supabase (data stored in Germany): product data after launch.

Because some of these providers are located abroad, your personal data is transferred outside Türkiye. Each provider's security and data processing practices are governed by its own documentation.

5. How long we keep your data

  • Email address: until launch. If you do not join the product after launch, it is deleted within 90 days at the latest.
  • If you withdraw your consent: you are removed from the list and we send you no further messages.
  • IP address (abuse prevention): 10 minutes.
  • Hosting logs and product measurement: for the relevant provider's retention period.

6. Your rights

Under KVKK Art. 11 you have the right to:

  • learn whether your personal data is processed
  • request information if it has been processed
  • learn the purpose of processing and whether the data is used for that purpose
  • know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom it has been transferred
  • request correction if it is incomplete or inaccurate
  • request deletion or destruction under KVKK Art. 7
  • request that correction, deletion or destruction be notified to the third parties it was transferred to
  • object to a result against you arising solely from automated analysis
  • claim compensation if you suffer damage from unlawful processing

7. Cookies, local storage and product measurement

Vercel Web Analytics and Vercel Speed Insights load only if you explicitly turn on the "Anonymous product measurement" preference. Before that, the provider scripts do not run; if you turn the preference off, they are not loaded on later page views. When enabled, these services may process a timestamp, the page address, the referring page, filtered query parameters, approximate location, browser, operating system and device details, and performance measurements.

When the same preference is on, anonymous product measurement (telemetry) sends contextual event data such as page type, content link, placement and limited campaign parameters. Your email address and name are never included in these events. No event is sent without your preference, and your browser's localStorage stores only your preference and return-visit state, and only after you give it. You can change your preference at any time in the Anonymous product measurement panel.

The Nunito and Nunito Sans typefaces used on the site are served directly from trescout.com, without connecting to a third-party service such as Google Fonts. Your IP address is therefore not sent to an outside party when the fonts load, and no cookie is used.

8. How to make a request

To exercise your rights, send your request to hello@trescout.com from the email address you signed up with. Please include your full name, the email address concerned and your request. We will respond free of charge within 30 days at the latest; if handling the request involves an additional cost, the fee schedule set by the Personal Data Protection Board may apply.

If your request is rejected, you find the response insufficient, or we do not respond in time, you can lodge a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Board (KVKK Board). If you are in the European Union, you can also contact the data protection authority of your country.

9. Security measures

  • HTTPS only: encrypted transmission over TLS 1.3 (registered for HSTS preload).
  • Key isolation: the email service key is kept only on the server and is never sent to the browser.
  • Request validation: the sign-up form accepts requests only from our own site, and no sign-up is accepted unless the consent box is ticked.
  • Bot protection: the form has a mechanism that filters out automated submissions.
  • Content Security Policy: loading of foreign scripts on the page is blocked at browser level.
  • Clickjacking protection: the site cannot be framed by other sites.

If you find a security vulnerability, please report it to hello@trescout.com.
